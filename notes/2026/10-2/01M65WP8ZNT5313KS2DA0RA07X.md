Sprint 98, Contract Review (ADR 0134). Gap found while building COM-1443.

## What people see

- When confirming or editing a commitment, **How it's met** offers controls, policies, incident rules, recovery targets and certificates, or Tracked here.
- A commitment met by a **data asset** ("client data stays in the UK" met by the UK customer database), a **subprocessor** (from the Trust Centre list), or a **plan activity** (a SAP entry) can only be linked through Polaris or the API today.
- After this, the picker offers those three as well, searchable by name, and the commitment's "Met by" links straight to the record.

## Who can do what

Unchanged: *Review contracts* or the commitment's owner. Only records the person can see are offered.
