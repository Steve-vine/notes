Sprint 100, Actions. Raised by Steve on 2026-10-30. His list had 36 "Answer a question for DDQ-6/DDQ-8" rows, one per question.

## What people see

**For someone answering questions:**
- One row per DDQ: **DDQ · Answer questions**, "DDQ-6 (Company Two) · 12 questions".
- It's due on the earliest date any of their questions was asked by.
- The link opens that DDQ **filtered to their questions**. Someone without any DDQ permission can still open it and answer, as today's single-question link allows.
- It goes when they've submitted or handed back the last one.

**For the DDQ's owner:** one row per DDQ, following its step:

| Step | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Received through the Trust Centre, not set up | **Set up questions** | N days after arrival |
| Being answered | **Get it answered** ("9 unanswered, 3 overdue with colleagues") | the DDQ's due date |
| Answers waiting for approval | **Approve answers** (for the approvers, "4 to approve") | the DDQ's due date |
| Ready | **Send** | the DDQ's due date |

- Today's separate "questions overdue", "due in 5 days" and "waiting for approval" rows fold into this.
- Files a visitor added still make one row (**DDQ · Note new files**) until marked noted. That's a different thing, so it doesn't count as a second action for the same step.

## Notes (technical)

- `ddq_assignments.py`: group by (DDQ, assignee).
- The frontend's assigned-questions page needs a per-DDQ view, e.g. `/ddq/assigned?ddq=<id>`, that lists only the reader's questions and is reachable without DDQ permissions. Mind the page tests' prefix stubs, which answer a new sub-route with the parent object.
- `ddq_tracking.py` and `ddq_received.py` fold into the owner's stepped row.
