Sprint 80, Update user portal. Steve chose this on 2026-10-31 in answer to "which other modules should the portal have?".

## Why

A risk assessment, DPIA, BIA or AI impact assessment often needs someone outside the governance team. A project lead fills in a DPIA, a department head describes their business functions. A portal-only assessor, approver or follow-up owner gets the action with nothing to open.

## What people see

- A **Risk assessments** menu item, shown to anyone who is an assessor or approver on an assessment, or owns a follow-up from one. Nobody else sees it.
- The page is **My risk assessments**. It lists those assessments with their reference, kind (risk assessment, DPIA, BIA, AI impact, PCI), subject, company, their part (Assessor / Approver / Follow-up), due date and status.
- Opening an assessment:
  - an **assessor** gets the same sections they'd fill in internally: threats, DPIA screening and risks, BIA functions, AI impacts. They can save and **submit** it;
  - an **approver** reads it all and **approves** it or **sends it back** with a reason. Nobody approves their own;
  - a **follow-up owner** sees the follow-up and **marks it done**, with a note.
- Starting a new assessment, withdrawing one, or importing threats stays internal.
- Their My actions rows for these open this page.
