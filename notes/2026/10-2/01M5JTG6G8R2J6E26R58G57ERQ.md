## What people see

**Admin ▸ Rubrics ▸ Data sensitivity:** each level gains **Can be given in a DDQ**: *Always* · *Only with an NDA in place* · *Never*. It's edited alongside the wording, and each change is kept in the level's revision history (who, when, from → to).

- Rubrics brief updated in the ADR task. Assistant tool that reads the rubric returns the rule.
