Sprint 97, Audit Management (ADR 0133, COM-1425).

Reading only. Comments and verdicts in the portal come in COM-1432.

## What people see

**Inside Compass: Audit ▸ <audit> ▸ Auditors** (external audits):
- each auditor's name, email, firm, status (Invited / Active / Expired / Revoked), access until, last visit;
- **Invite** (from the audit's auditors, or a new one). Compass emails a link from the company's sender. Access runs until 30 days after the audit's planned end by default, and can be changed;
- **Resend**, **Extend**, **Revoke**;
- **Activity**: who viewed or downloaded what, and when.

The portal can be switched off for the whole organisation in Admin ▸ Settings ▸ Auditor portal. While it's off, every link is refused.

**The portal**, on the same external address as the Trust Centre, under the company's branding. It says "<Company> · <Audit name>" and shows nothing else of the company. It has:
- **Overview**: scope, frameworks, dates, audit lead (name and email), and request and finding counts by status;
- **Requests**: the list with ref, request, requirement, status and due date, filterable. A request shows the **evidence provided** (name, type, period covered, provided on) and its **shared** comments;
- **Downloads** are watermarked "Provided to <auditor>, <firm>, for <audit>, <date>". **Download all** builds a zip of everything provided, indexed by request;
- **Findings**: each with grade, wording, the corrective action plan once submitted, and the closing evidence;
- what's **withdrawn** is shown as withdrawn, never hidden.

An auditor never sees:
- another audit;
- internal comments or notes;
- owners' names (only "the company");
- evidence not provided to this audit;
- anything else in Compass.

## Who can do what

- Inviting, extending and revoking needs *Manage audits*.
- Switching the portal off needs an admin setting permission. That switch is absolute.

## Notes (technical)

- Reuse the Trust Centre's sign-in and link machinery (ADR 0120 §5–§7, ADR 0051): token in the body, SHA-256 only, one live link per auditor, a code by email, the identical `rejection()` for every failure, the visit cookie. Extract the shared parts into `core/external_visit/` rather than copying them, keeping the Trust Centre's behaviour the same (its tests must pass unchanged).
- Routes go under `/api/auditor/…` (a separate router, like `api/trust_centre`). The frontend is a separate portal app shell like the Trust Centre's.
- Watermark with `core/trust_centre_watermark.py`. Mark `seen_by_auditor_at` on first view of evidence and comments, which locks them per COM-1428 / COM-1430.
- Rate-limit downloads and zips. Build the zip in the worker.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration + API).
