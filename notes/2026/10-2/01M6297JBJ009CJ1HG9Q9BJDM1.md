Sprint 96, Continuous Control Checks (ADR 0132, COM-1415).

## What people see

**Compliance page** (Posture ▸ Compliance): a **What changed** section below the trend.
- **Period picker:** This month, Last month, This quarter, Last quarter, or custom dates. It follows the page's Measure filter: Core controls or a framework, and a tier or level.
- **Headline:** "Compliance 61% → 68% (+7) · Average maturity 2.4 → 2.7 · Open gaps 31 → 22". The figures come from the nightly snapshots at each end of the period. A reconstructed day is labelled as in the chart.
- **Then sections**, each with counts and an expandable list linked to the record:
  - **Controls improved**, with status and/or maturity from → to, who changed it and when;
  - **Controls slipped**;
  - **Gaps:** opened, closed, now overdue;
  - **Risks:** new, closed, moved over or under appetite;
  - **Acceptances:** given, expired, renewed (the exceptions too, once sprint 94 is in);
  - **Checks:** started failing, recovered;
  - **Frameworks:** requirements newly met or no longer met, when a framework is chosen.
- A control that changed back and forth in the period shows once, start → end, with "changed 3 times".
- **Download PDF:** "What changed, Q3 2026, <company>", in the house style (ADR 0110), with the headline and every section.

## Who can do what

Anyone who can see the Compliance page sees it. Risks, gaps and acceptances follow their own read permissions, so a section someone can't read is left out.

## Notes (technical)

- All read-only. The sources are:
  - `assessment_revisions` (start and end state per control in the period);
  - `risk_revisions`;
  - gaps (`created_at`, `closed_at`, `target_date`);
  - risk acceptances (ADR 0123 §9);
  - `check_results`;
  - `posture_snapshots` for the headline and the per-framework breakdown.
- Compare each control's state at the period's start and end, never the sum of the events in between.
- One function, `core/what_changed.py` `summarise(db, company, start, end, measure)`, serves both the page and the email (COM-1423).
- Revisions record the library as it was. If a control was added or retired in the period, say "new to the library" or "retired" instead of counting it as improved or slipped (ADR 0070's warning about replays).

**Chain:** on the chain (API).

**Done when:** on staging, Steve picks Last quarter, sees the headline match the chart's two ends, opens Controls improved, and downloads the PDF.
