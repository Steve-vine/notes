Sprint 94, Control Exceptions (ADR 0130, COM-1398). Builds on COM-1399, 1402, 1403, 1404, 1405. Polaris works as the person, with exactly their role (ADR 0127).

## What people see

Asking Polaris:
- "What exceptions do we have / what runs out this quarter?" — the Exceptions list, with states and dates.
- "Request an exception to ACC.8 because …" — it fills the request form's fields, asking for anything missing (approver, residual scores), and submits it. Raising is not a sign-off, so it doesn't ask first; it says who will approve.
- "Make R-14 an exception to ACC.8" / "remove …" — the link.
- "How compliant are we with ISO 27001?" — met % and excepted % both, and the shortfalls list marks excepted ones.
- The SoA and Coverage report answers carry the Exception column / Excepted state.
- **Polaris never approves an exception** on anyone's behalf without asking — approving is a sign-off (ADR 0127 amendment, COM-1340) — and nobody approves their own, so it can't approve one the person requested.
