## What Steve said

"Can you move the cover fields into the main questionnaire so they work like all the other questions. Add a new questionnaire section at the top called 'Cover fields'. Then they just get answered and approved like every other question."

## Behaviour

- **The questionnaire's cover fields are questions** ("Company Name", "Completed by", "Date"…). They sit in a section called **Cover fields**, first in the questions list, in the order they appear in the client's file.
- Each one:
  - is worded as the client's label ("Company Name");
  - has one answer field placed in the cell next to the label (C1, E1 on DDQ-4);
  - has no evidence list.
- **They work like every other question:**
  - they start with Polaris (COM-1291), and can be assigned to anyone;
  - they're drafted, go In review, are approved or sent back, and appear in Review answers (COM-1300), the progress counts and the completed file's read-back;
  - the questionnaire is ready to send when every question is approved, cover fields included, with no separate rule.
- **Polaris drafts them** from what Compass knows: the company's name, and the person completing it (whoever the questionnaire is with, or its owner). The date is the day it's sent. Polaris says where each value came from in its note to the reviewer.
- **The Cover fields card goes**, and so does the separate cover-field approval.
- **Set-up:** when Polaris reads the layout, it sets up cover fields as questions in the Cover fields section. Manual question setup offers the same: a cover field is a label cell and an answer cell.

## Existing questionnaires

- Each existing cover field becomes a question in a Cover fields section at the top:
  - filled in and approved → **Approved**, with the same value;
  - filled in but not approved → **Drafted** (In review on a questionnaire that's In review, once COM-1299 is in);
  - empty → **Unanswered**, with Polaris.
- On staging, DDQ-4's "Company Name" (Moneypenny) and "Completed by" (Steve Vine) become two approved questions, so DDQ-4 stays ready to send with 31 questions.
- Sent questionnaires are frozen and keep their record as sent.

## My calls (awaiting Steve)

- **A cover-field answer is Public by default.** It's who is answering, not information to protect, so it needs no Disclosure override (COM-1289 / COM-1297). Someone can still set it lower.
- **"Add to library" is never offered** for a cover field, because its answer belongs to one questionnaire.
- **Progress counts include them**: DDQ-4 reads "31 questions".
- **Questions with no client reference show their label as their heading** ("Company Name"), not "Question 1". The client's own numbering of the real questions stays as it is.

## Implementation notes

- **Model:** add `ddq_questions.kind` (`question` | `cover`, default `question`), or derive it from `section = 'Cover fields'`. A kind is safer, because the section name could be renamed.
- **Migration:**

  - add a detail field at its place, and an answer version holding the value (classification Public, approved stamp copied);
  - add a current Polaris assignment, or keep whoever filled it as the drafter;
  - then drop `ddq_cover_fields`, or keep it read-only for sent snapshots if they reference it.
  - Test it on a populated DB: filled and approved, filled only, empty, and a sent DDQ.
- **Core:** remove the cover-field paths: `core/ddq_layout` `set_cover_fields` / `fill_cover_field` / `_replace_covers`, the cover counts in `ddq_review` readiness, and `approve_cover_fields`. The writers (`ddq_output`) write cover questions' answers like any other.
