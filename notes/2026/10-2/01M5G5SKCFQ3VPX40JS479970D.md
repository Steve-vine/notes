Sprint 83, Risk Assessments (ADR in COM-1219). GDPR Art. 35(2), 35(7), 35(9); the ICO's DPIA template structure.

**Chain:** on the chain — migration + API

## What people see

A DPIA is an assessment of kind **DPIA** with the same start, assessors, approver, submit/approve/send back and freeze as a risk assessment, and these cards in place of Threats:

1. **The processing** — nature, scope, context, purposes; the data assets it covers, with their purpose, lawful basis, data types, volume, recipients, transfers and retention shown from Inventory (edit them there); the technology assets and vendors involved.
2. **Necessity and proportionality** — lawful basis and why; does it achieve the purpose, is there a less intrusive way; data minimisation; data quality; how people are told; how their rights are supported; processors' compliance; international transfer safeguards.
3. **Consultation** — the **DPO** (a person in Compass, or an external name) and their **advice**, and whether it was **followed** — with reasons if not (Art. 35(2)); **people's views** sought and what they said, or why they weren't sought (Art. 35(9)); others consulted (security, processors).
4. **Risks to people** — each: the source of harm, the kinds of harm, likelihood × severity on the **Risk to people** scale → Low/Medium/High.
5. **Measures** — for each risk: what reduces it, the effect (eliminated / reduced / accepted), **residual** risk on the same scale, whether the measure is approved, and its owner and due date (becomes a treatment if it needs doing).
6. **Sign-off** — measures approved by, residual risks approved by (the approver), DPO advice summary, who keeps it under review.

**Rules:** a High residual can't be approved as accepted — it requires **prior consultation** (next task). A DPIA may also raise an ordinary register risk (e.g. a regulatory risk to the company) — "Raise a company risk".

- The PDF follows the ICO template's section order.
- Evidence of operation on **PDH.12**; it appears in Audit ▸ Evidence.
