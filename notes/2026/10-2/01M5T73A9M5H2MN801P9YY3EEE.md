## What Steve asked (2026-10-25)

"Create an 'integration' between DDQ and the Trust Portal, which would allow a client to submit their questionnaire and any other evidence via the Trust Portal, which would then automatically get added to the DDQ module. Also once a DDQ has been completed a new option to upload a DDQ + supporting evidence to the Trust Portal automatically creating a new visitor link for them and emailing to them. The documents are uploaded into an area specifically ringfenced to their visitor link, so that they can download it."

## Steve decided (2026-10-25)

1. **Only signed-in visitors can send a questionnaire** (link + code). The public "ask for access" form gets an "I have a questionnaire to send" tick. Anonymous people still can't upload anything.
2. **A client we deliver a DDQ to becomes a normal visitor.** They see everything shared with all visitors, plus their own Questionnaires area. Restricted documents still need granting as today.
3. **A questionnaire that arrives waits as Received** until someone asks Polaris to set it up. Compass still calls no AI by itself (ADR 0124 §1).
4. **No virus scanning this sprint.** Instead there are strict checks on what a file actually is, macro-enabled files are refused, and there's a size limit.

## The shape

Each visitor gets a private **Questionnaires** area in the portal that only they can see. Questionnaires go into it in one direction and come back out of it in the other.

- **In:** a visitor sends a questionnaire in one of three ways: a file, the questions pasted as text, or the address of their own system. They can add supporting files, a due date, what it's for and their reference. A DDQ appears straight away as Received, filled in from the visitor, and its owner gets a to-do.
- **Out:** on a DDQ that's ready to send, or already sent, **Send via Trust Centre** shares it with the contact, and anyone else added from the same organisation. Each becomes or stays a visitor, gets a fresh link by email, and finds the completed file and the evidence in their area. The DDQ is marked sent "via the Trust Centre".
- The DDQ shows who it was shared with and when each person opened or downloaded it. Sharing can be stopped.

## The ADR records

- Amends ADR 0120:
  - §4/§9: the completed questionnaire is served as the client's own file, unstamped. Evidence PDFs keep the DDQ pack's stamp.
  - §10: the portal gains a per-visitor Questionnaires area.
  - §11: the request form gets the questionnaire tick.
  - §14: the portal gains its first visitor-authenticated file upload.
- Amends ADR 0124:
  - §6: a DDQ can be received through the Trust Centre.
  - §14: sending via the Trust Centre serves the stored sent copies.
- All the calls below, listed as awaiting Steve.

## My calls (awaiting Steve)

- **One switch per company**, "Questionnaires through the Trust Centre", in Trust Centre Settings, off by default. It covers both directions. Switching it on needs a person named to receive incoming questionnaires (someone who can manage DDQs).
- **Sending via the Trust Centre** needs both Manage DDQs and Manage visitors, because it invites people.
- **The delivery area isn't behind the NDA click-through.** The DDQ's own sharing rules already applied the NDA position when the answers were approved.
- **What the visitor gets is exactly what went out:** the completed file and the evidence pack's files, as stored when the DDQ was sent (ADR 0124 §14). There's no second, per-visitor stamp. Each download is still logged against the visitor.
- **Access lasts the company's usual link length** (30 days by default), extended or resent as for any visitor.
- **Accepted types:**
  - the questionnaire: .xlsx, .csv or .docx;
  - supporting files: also PDF;
  - .xls/.doc/.xlsm/.docm are refused with "save it as …".
  - A PDF questionnaire is accepted only as a supporting file. The portal suggests pasting the questions instead.
- **The client sees three plain states:** Received, Being worked on and Completed. Withdrawn or declined shows "Closed — contact <contact email>", with no email and no reason. They never see internal statuses, answers in progress or who is working on it.
- **The client can add files until the DDQ is sent.** They can't withdraw it themselves this sprint.
- **The DDQ register shows where a DDQ came from:** "Trust Centre" beside the requester, and a filter.

## Out of scope

Virus scanning; Polaris starting by itself; anonymous uploads on the public page; the client withdrawing or editing a questionnaire; answers shown in the portal (only the files go back).
