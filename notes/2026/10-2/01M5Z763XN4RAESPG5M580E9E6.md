Sprint 91, Vulnerabilities - Register (ADR 0129). Split out of COM-1364 while building it (2026-10-27).

## What people see

- **Attachments** on a vulnerability's page: screenshots, a report extract or other files, added by anyone who can work vulnerabilities.
- They can also be added in the Record window.
- Each file is kept as evidence. It shows in Audit ▸ Evidence as coming from that vulnerability, and is replaced or archived there, never deleted.

## Notes (technical)

- A new `AttachmentOwnerType.vulnerability` and an `OWNER_TABLES` entry, plus the enum value migration.
- The Evidence register's "where it came from" classification gains Vulnerabilities.
- Follow `risk_attachments.py` for upload, list, download, replace and archive.

**Why it was split out:** attachments go through the Evidence register (ADR 0122). That is a change to evidence classification, not just the register, and COM-1364 shipped without it.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration + API).

**Done when:** a file can be added when recording and from the page, appears in Audit ▸ Evidence, and is archived rather than removed.
