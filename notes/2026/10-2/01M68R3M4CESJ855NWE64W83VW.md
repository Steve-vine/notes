Sprint 80, Update user portal. Requested by Steve on 2026-10-31.

## What people see

- The menu has one item, **Security incidents**, where it had "Report an incident" and "My reports". Every signed-in employee sees it, as both do today.
- It opens what was My reports: the incidents you have reported, in plain words. The page title is **My security incidents**.
- A **Report an incident** button sits in the page header. It opens the same form, with the same fields and wording, **in a modal** rather than on its own page.
- When you send it, the modal shows today's "Thank you — your report is in" message with your reference. Closing it leaves you on the list with the new report at the top.
- If you have reported nothing yet, the empty state also offers **Report an incident**. The person who most needs the button has never reported anything, which is why it had its own menu item until now.
- The old Report an incident address still works. It opens My security incidents with the form already open, so existing links and emails keep working.
- Closing the modal before sending asks before throwing away anything typed.

## Notes (technical)

- Turn the form in `PortalReportIncidentPage.tsx` into a component used by a modal on `PortalIncidentReportsPage`. Point `/portal/report-incident` at `/portal/incidents?report=1`, which opens the modal.
- In `portalNav.ts`, two entries become one. Its `match` covers both paths.
- On sending, refetch the reports list.
