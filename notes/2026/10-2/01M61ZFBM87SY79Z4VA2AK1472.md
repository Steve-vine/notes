Sprint 95, Business Continuity (ADR 0131, COM-1407). Builds on COM-1410 (runbooks recover systems) and COM-1409 (needed-back-within targets). Amends ADR 0115 §2/§5.

## What people see

- **A Testing entry** (one-off or series) gains, optional:
  - **Runbooks tested** — picked from the Playbook's runbooks; the systems they recover for this company are listed under them;
  - **Kind of exercise** — Tabletop, Walkthrough, Simulation, Full failover.
  - Both belong to the series, like linked controls (ADR 0115 §7).
- **Marking it done** asks, when runbooks are named, for:
  - a **result**: **Passed**, **Partly**, **Failed**;
  - per system: **recovered in** (hours/minutes) and **data lost** (time), each shown against the system's target (needed back within / data loss tolerated, or its own RTO/RPO when there's no BIA), green or red. "Not attempted" is allowed for a system, with a reason;
  - a tabletop or walkthrough can skip times (nothing was actually recovered) — the form says so.
- The done entry shows all this frozen, as the rest of a done entry is; **Correct** (ADR 0115 §5) can fix times and result with a reason.
- **The standard plan's** DR / continuity exercise gets Kind of exercise = Simulation by default; its tabletop sits beside incident response's.
- **The auditor export PDF** prints runbooks, kind, result and the times table.
- **Plan summary** counts exercises by result.

## Who can do what

Unchanged: plan managers set runbooks and kind; the owner (or a plan manager) completes it.

## Notes (technical)

- SAP series/entry gain `exercise_kind`, a runbook link (series-level), and on completion `result` + per-system rows (system, recovered_minutes, data_lost_minutes, not_attempted_reason) with the target copied at completion so a later BIA doesn't rewrite history.
- Freeze rules enforced server-side as for the other done fields.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration + API).

**Done when:** on staging, a DR exercise naming a runbook is completed with times, one inside and one outside target, and the entry, PDF and summary show it.
