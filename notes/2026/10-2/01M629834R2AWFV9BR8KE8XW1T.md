Sprint 96, Continuous Control Checks (ADR 0132, COM-1415).

## What people see

In Polaris (and over MCP), someone can ask:
- "Which checks are failing for Acme?"
- "Why is the MFA check failing?" Polaris names the accounts if the person may see them, and says what to do, such as registering MFA or adding them to the Conditional Access policy.
- "Run the dormant accounts check again."
- "What changed last quarter?" Polaris gives the summary and offers the PDF link.

Polaris doesn't switch checks off or change thresholds. Those stay in Settings, because they change what Compass judges.

## Who can do what

Polaris acts as the person, with their permissions (ADR 0109). Run now needs the same permission as on screen.
