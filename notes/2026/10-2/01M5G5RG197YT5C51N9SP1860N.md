Sprint 83, Risk Assessments (ADR in COM-1219). ISO 27001 6.1.3 c–d: the SoA is produced by comparing the risk treatment with Annex A.

**Chain:** on the chain — API

## What people see

**Statement of Applicability** (Frameworks ▸ ISO 27001 ▸ SoA, and its export):
- A **Risks treated** column: for each Annex A control, the register risks whose treatment relies on a core control mapped to it (R-14, R-22…), and "from RA-7" when they came from the current approved assessment.
- A control included with no risk behind it says "Included without a linked risk", so the justification can say why (legal, contractual or good practice).

**Reports ▸ Risk register** (CSV and PDF) gains: band (inherent and residual), over appetite, appetite category, status, treatments (type, owner, due, status), acceptance (approver, expiry), last reviewed, next review, last assessed in (RA-7). Closed risks only when asked for.

## Notes (technical)

- `api/v1/soa.py` and `/reports/risks`; the link is risk ↔ core control (risk_control_link and assessment items) ↔ mapping.

**Done when:** the SoA export lists R-14 against A.5.7 when R-14 relies on a control mapped to it; the register CSV has the new columns.
