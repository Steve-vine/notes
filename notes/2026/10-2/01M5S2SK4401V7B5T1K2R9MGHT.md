## What I saw

While smoke-testing sprint 86 on DDQ-4, after Polaris drafted 9 answers and 20 questions went to Steve, the DDQ's progress read **"29 questions: 9 answered, 20 unanswered; 20 assigned"**. The same DDQ's statuses were Drafted 9, Unanswered 20.

Since COM-1286, **Answered** means a person wrote or confirmed the answer, and **Drafted** means nobody has yet. "Assigned" is no longer a word we use (COM-1286, COM-1287). The progress line contradicts both.

## Behaviour

- Progress shows each status as its own part, in the same words as the questions list: **"29 questions: 9 drafted, 20 unanswered · 20 with people"**. A fuller example: "4 approved, 3 answered, 2 drafted, 3 unanswered".
  - The bar in the Requests register gets a separate band for Drafted, in a lighter shade than Answered.
  - Its label, the DDQ page and Polaris's reads all use the same wording.
- Who has questions is said apart: "· n with people", counting questions a person has. Questions with Polaris aren't counted, because that's the default.
- "3 assigned questions overdue" becomes **"3 questions overdue"**: on the register, in the Actions line, and in Polaris's reads.

## Implementation notes

- Frontend `src/ddq/requests/tracking.ts`:
  - `progressBands`: separate `answered` and `drafted` bands;
  - `progressLabel`: the "with people" tail;
  - `assignmentsOverdueText`;
  - their tests and `TrackingMarks`.
- Backend `assistant/tools/ddq_common.py`: `progress_text` and `assignments_overdue_text`, the same wording. Also the examples in the `ddq_list_ddqs` description ('12 questions: 4 approved, 3 answered, 2 assigned, 3 unanswered').
- `core/actions/ddq_tracking.py`: the "n assigned questions overdue on DDQ-n" Action title.
- The `progress.assigned` count in the API can keep its name, or be renamed `with_people` if that's cheap. Renaming it changes OpenAPI, which puts this task on the chain; otherwise it's independent.
