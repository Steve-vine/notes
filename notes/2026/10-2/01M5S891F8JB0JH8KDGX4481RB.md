## What Steve said

"There doesn't appear to be a 'Review' status, so when a question is sent for review it stays in drafted."

Then: "what's the difference between Drafted and Answered, it seems to go Unanswered -> Drafted -> In review -> Approved." He agreed to both changes as one task.

Seen on DDQ-4. The questionnaire was In review, but its questions still read Drafted (28) and Approved (1). The 20 questions with Steve were still open on his Actions as though he were working on them.

## Behaviour

**Statuses:** a question's status says where its answer has got to:

- **Unanswered:** nothing written yet.
- **Drafted:** an answer is written, by Polaris, from the library or by a person. Who drafted it is shown beside the status ("Drafted · by Polaris", "Drafted · by Steve Vine"), as the answer line already says. A person's change by hand is still Drafted, by them.
- **In review:** the answer has been submitted and waits for an approver. It's **locked**: nobody changes it while it's in review.
- **Approved.**

"Answered" goes. Who the question is *with* stays separate from its status (COM-1286, COM-1287).

**How a question reaches In review:**

- **The person who has it submits it**, from their Actions or the question window ("Submit for review"). Their Action closes. They keep the question, so a send-back comes back to them.
- **The questionnaire is submitted for review.** Every Drafted question moves to In review at once, including Polaris's. Each person holding one of them has that Action closed ("submitted with the questionnaire"). A question still Unanswered stays where it is, with whoever has it, on their Actions.
- **An answer changed while its questionnaire is already In review**, for example after a send-back, goes back to In review when its holder submits it again.

**In review, the approver:**

- **Approves:** the question becomes Approved.
- **Sends back:** the question returns to Drafted with the reason, to whoever has it. A person gets it back on their Actions (as COM-1287); Polaris's sits with Polaris to redraft.
- **Approve all** approves every In review answer that can be given. It still leaves held-back ones, and approves the cover fields.

**An approved answer that's changed** goes back to Drafted, and must be submitted and approved again.

**Everywhere statuses show, the words match:**

- the questions list and its status filter;
- the question window;
- Actions;
- the progress bar and its line (COM-1295: approved, in review, drafted, unanswered, "· n with people");
- the completed-file read-back's "not yet approved" list;
- Polaris's reads and tool descriptions.

## Existing questions

- Every **Answered** question becomes **Drafted**, by whoever wrote it.
- On a questionnaire that's **In review**, every Drafted question becomes **In review**, and the open Actions of people holding those questions are closed. On staging that's DDQ-4: its 28 Drafted questions become In review, and Steve's 20 Actions close.
- Sent and withdrawn questionnaires are frozen and unchanged.

## My calls (awaiting Steve)

- Submitting the questionnaire moves Polaris's drafts to In review along with everyone else's. The approver is the person who confirms them.
- A person keeps a question after submitting it, so a send-back returns to them. It doesn't go to whoever assigned it.
- In review means locked, even for whoever has the question. To change it, an approver sends it back.
