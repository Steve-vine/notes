Sprint 92, Vulnerabilities - Scanner sources (ADR amendment in the sprint's first task). Builds on sprint 91.

## What people see

- **Vulnerabilities ▸ Unmatched**: one row per unplaced target, not per finding. A Nessus host with 40 findings is one row reading "40 findings, 3 critical". Columns:
  - the target (hostname, IP, repository, image or URL);
  - the source;
  - counts by severity;
  - first seen.
- **For a row:**
  - **Place on a system**: choose a technology asset. Its findings become vulnerabilities there, with fix-by dates from the day each was first seen, not today.
  - **Save as a rule**: the same, and remember it, e.g. "hostname matches `web-*.prod.example.com`", "IP in 10.20.0.0/16", "repository `acme/billing`", "image `ghcr.io/acme/api`". It applies to future findings.
  - **Ignore**: with a reason, e.g. "lab host, not ours". Its findings stay out of the register. Ignored targets are listed under a filter and can be un-ignored.
  - **Make a technology asset**: opens the new-asset window pre-filled with the target's name. On save, the target is placed on the new asset.
- **Settings ▸ Matching rules:** the company's rules in order, each with how many targets it currently matches. They can be edited, reordered and removed. A **preview** shows what a rule would match before saving.
- **The Unmatched tab's count** shows in the tab title. The Overview gets a line: "N findings aren't on a system yet".

## Who can do what

- Viewing Unmatched needs view vulnerabilities.
- Placing, ignoring and rules need manage vulnerabilities.

## Notes (technical)

- Rules in the shape of `discovery_rules` (ordered, previewable).
- Match first through discovery's attached resources and directory servers, then by rule.
- Rematching runs when a rule is saved and after every import.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration + API).

**Done when:**
- findings with unknown targets land in Unmatched;
- placing and rules turn them into vulnerabilities with the right first-seen dates;
- ignore keeps them out;
- the preview matches what saving does.
