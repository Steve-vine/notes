Sprint 83, Risk Assessments (ADR in COM-1219).

**Chain:** on the chain — migration + API

## What people see

**Risk Assessments ▸ Assessments:** the company's assessments, newest first — `RA-7`, title, kind, status, approver, approved on, next due. Filters: kind, status. **Start an assessment** (Manage).

**Starting one** (kind: *Risk assessment* this task; the other kinds come in their own tasks):
- **Title** and **Scope**: a statement ("All Moneypenny services and the systems that run them"), plus optional technology assets, data assets, vendors and frameworks it covers (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, ISO 42001 for an AI risk assessment…).
- **Assessors** (people in Compass) and **Other participants** (free text, e.g. an external consultant).
- **Approver**: a person with *Approve risk assessments and accept risks* — never one of the assessors.
- **Why now**: Scheduled · Significant change · Incident · New system or processing · Other — with a line saying what (SOC 2 CC3.4, ISO 8.2 "on change"). Started from an incident, it links to it.
- **Start from:**
  - **The threat catalogue** — pick the threats that apply, grouped by category (all ticked by default);
  - **The last approved assessment** of this kind — carries its threats and scores forward as the starting point;
  - **Nothing** — add threats one by one.
- Created as **Draft**; the page shows the details card, editable until submitted, and History.

## Notes (technical)

- Tables: risk_assessment (company, number, kind, status, scope fields, trigger, dates), assessors join, scope links (containers, data assets, vendors, frameworks). Revisions append-only.
- Kind enum ships with every kind now — risk assessment, DPIA screening, DPIA, PCI targeted risk analysis (incl. customised approach), AI impact assessment — so later tasks don't alter it (create the type explicitly — see the Alembic enum notes).

**Done when:** a manager starts RA-1 from the catalogue with a scope, two assessors and an approver; an assessor can't be chosen as approver; starting RA-2 "from the last approved" copies its threats.
