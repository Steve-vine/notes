Sprint 98, Contract Review (ADR 0134, COM-1435).

## What people see

- **Download for the client** (approved reviews only):
  - **the client's own Word file**, unchanged except for **comments in the margin** on each clause we responded to. Each comment holds the client-facing response and any suggested wording, authored as the company (e.g. "Moneypenny Legal"), not a person;
  - a PDF contract gets a **Word response schedule** instead: a table of clause number, clause extract, our response and suggested wording.
  - **Internal comments, flags, reasons and citations of internal records never appear.** The download says "Generated by Compass" in the document properties only.
- **Summary PDF** (internal, house style): the client, contract and version, standard contract version, counts by flag, every escalation with who signed it off, and every commitment we'd be taking on. It's for the file or a director.
- **Mark as sent** records when and to whom, and the status becomes Sent.
- **The client's next draft:** **Upload revised version** on a Sent review creates version 2. Compass compares it with version 1 clause by clause:
  - **unchanged** clauses keep their decisions and show "Carried from v1";
  - **changed** clauses show a word-level difference and go back to In review, to their previous assignee, with Polaris re-flagging them;
  - **new** clauses start with Polaris;
  - **removed** clauses are listed.

  Then the round repeats: decide, approve, send. Every version and its decisions stay on the review's **Versions** tab.

## Who can do what

- Downloading for the client and marking as sent need *Review contracts* or being the owner.
- Uploading a revised version needs the same.

## Notes (technical)

- Word comments: python-docx ≥ 1.2 `Document.add_comment` anchored to the clause's runs, which needs a mapping from clause to paragraph ranges kept at split time (COM-1438). Bump the dependency floor, with care: CVE floors and `[tool.uv]` cooldown (see memory "uv tool table needs cooldown").
- Compare versions by clause: match on number, then by text similarity for renumbered clauses. Use the `difflib` word diff for display.
- The PDF uses the house style (ADR 0110) and is generated in the worker.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration + API).
