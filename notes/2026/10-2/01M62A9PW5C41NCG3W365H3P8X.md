Sprint 97, Audit Management (ADR 0133, COM-1425).

## What people see

- **Audit ▸ <audit> ▸ Findings:** a table showing ref (AUD-12-F3), grade, requirement, summary, gap, owner, response due, status. Counts by grade across the top: "1 major · 3 minor · 4 observations".
- **Add a finding** (or **import** findings from a spreadsheet, as with requests):
  - **grade**: Major nonconformity, Minor nonconformity, Observation, Opportunity for improvement, SOC 2 exception;
  - the auditor's wording;
  - requirement(s) or control(s);
  - the request it arose from, if any;
  - **response due** (the auditor's deadline).
- **Gap:**
  - a major or minor nonconformity or a SOC 2 exception **raises a gap** on each control concerned, or **links an existing open gap** if the person picks one;
  - for an observation or improvement opportunity, raising a gap is offered but optional;
  - the gap shows "From audit AUD-12, finding F3 (minor nonconformity)" and links back. The SAP activity linked to the audit lists it among its findings too.
- **Corrective action plan** on the finding: **correction** (immediate fix), **root cause**, **corrective action**, owner, target date, then **Submit to auditor**.
- **Closing evidence:** chosen or uploaded as with requests (COM-1428), then **Submit for closure**.
- **Status:** Open → Plan submitted → Plan accepted → Closure submitted → **Closed**, with "Plan needs work" and "Closure not accepted" returning it a step. Plans and closures are accepted by the auditor (COM-1430 / COM-1432).
- **Closing the gap doesn't close the finding**, and closing the finding doesn't close the gap. Each says if the other is still open.
- **Actions:**
  - the finding's owner: "Write the corrective action plan for AUD-12-F3, due 20 Nov";
  - the audit lead: findings past their response date.

## Who can do what

- *Manage audits* adds, imports and edits findings.
- The finding's owner writes the plan and submits closure evidence.
- A finding can't be deleted once submitted to the auditor, only **withdrawn by the auditor's agreement**: the lead records it with a reason.

## Notes (technical)

- New tables `audit_findings` (numbered per audit) and `audit_finding_targets` (requirement or control), plus `audit_finding_gaps`. Gaps link by id, both ways. Reuse `SapEntryFinding` semantics for the SAP side. Corrective action fields live on the finding, revisioned.
- Raise gaps through the gap service, with `raised_via` = "audit" and a link row. The owner and target date come from the plan, once there is one.
- Add an Actions source for finding owners and the audit lead.
