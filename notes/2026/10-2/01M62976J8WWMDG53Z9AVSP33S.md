Sprint 96, Continuous Control Checks (ADR 0132, COM-1415).

## What people see

- **Audit ▸ Evidence** (the Evidence register, sprint 82): each control with a check shows a line **"Compass check: <name>"** with the kind **System check**. It can't be edited, archived or replaced, because Compass made it.
- **Audit pack** for a period: for each control in scope, a **Checks** page in the PDF that shows:
  - each check;
  - the share of the period it was passing ("passing 94% of 1 Jul–30 Sep");
  - every change of state with its date;
  - any gap it raised and how that gap closed.
- Failing items are **counts only** in the pack ("4 people"), never names. A pack goes to auditors.
- **Statement of Applicability / Coverage report:** controls with checks carry "Checked automatically: passing / failing" beside their status.

## Who can do what

The same as the audit pack and Evidence register today.

## Notes (technical)

- `core/audit_pack.py`: add a section per control from `check_results` over the period. The pass share is the time-weighted share of the kept results, with Can't tell shown as its own share.
- Evidence register: add a read-only, derived row type, not stored in the evidence table, so the register can't drift from the results.
- `api/v1/soa.py`, `api/v1/reports.py`: one extra field per control.

**Chain:** on the chain (API).

**Done when:** on staging, an audit pack for a quarter shows IDM.13's check history, and the Evidence register lists it as a System check.
