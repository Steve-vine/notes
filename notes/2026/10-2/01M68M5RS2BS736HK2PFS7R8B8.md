Sprint 100, Actions (ADR 0137 rules 4 and 5).

## What people see

- **Every row has a Due date.**
  - Things with a date keep it (a gap's target date, a vulnerability's fix-by date, a DSR's deadline).
  - Things without one are due **N days after they arrived**, with N set per type on Admin ▸ Actions (COM-1469). Today these show a blank: a report to triage, an unrequested change, a gap with no target date, a trust centre request.
- **Email follows the window.** An action is mailed when it enters its window ("from N days before it's due"), at the reminders inside it, and while it's overdue.
  - Outside the window it's on the list but nobody is emailed.
  - Example: your eight fixes due in Jan 2027 stop being mailed now and start 7 days before 25 Jan.
  - Work where someone is waiting (approvals, triage) has a window that covers the day it arrives, so it is still emailed straight away.

## Notes (technical)

- One effective due-date and window function, used by the digest and by Status (COM-1474).
- Sources declare either a natural due date or `due_after_days` (a type setting).
- The digest (ADR 0055) sends only rows inside their window or overdue. The ledger is unchanged, so nothing is re-sent.
- Each module's existing lead windows become the type settings' starting values. That includes the DDQ reminder lead time in DDQ Settings: decide in COM-1468 whether the company setting or Admin ▸ Actions wins, then keep only one.
