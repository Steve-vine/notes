Sprint 83, Risk Assessments (ADR in COM-1219). ISO 27001 6.1.2 / 7.5 documented information; HIPAA 164.316.

**Chain:** on the chain — API

## What people see

**Download PDF** on any assessment (View). A draft is watermarked **Draft**; an approved one isn't. Contents, in the company's house style:

1. Title page: company, RA-7, kind, status, approved on and by, assessors, other participants.
2. **Scope** and **Why now**.
3. **Method**: the likelihood and impact scales, score bands and appetite *as frozen on approval*, with their descriptors.
4. **Summary** written at submission; the fraud statement if one was given.
5. **Results**: a residual heat-map, then a table of every threat — inherent, residual, band, over appetite, decision, owner.
6. One section per threat: what makes it possible, consequence, assets, the controls relied on **with their status on the day it was approved**, the rationale, treatments.
7. **Approval**: approver, date, comment; History of submissions and send-backs.

Each kind adds its own sections in its own task (DPIA, PCI, AI).

## Notes (technical)

- Uses the document machinery from ADR 0110 (house style, WeasyPrint). Freeze control status at approval (snapshot) so the PDF is the same tomorrow.
- WeasyPrint XML-namespace note applies to any SVG heat-map.

**Done when:** an approved RA-1's PDF shows the scales as they stood at approval, every threat and the approver; a draft's PDF says Draft.
