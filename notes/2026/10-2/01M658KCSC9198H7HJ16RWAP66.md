Defect in COM-1438, found by Claude's pre-UAT pass (COM-1452) on staging, 2026-10-30.

## What happens

On CR-2 "E2E 20261030 ACME DPA 2027" in E2E Test Company:
- 3.1 ("notify the Controller of a personal data breach within 24 hours", under "Breach notification");
- 4.1 (subprocessor consent, under "Subprocessors");
- 5.1 (stored only in the UK, under "Data location").

All three are tagged **Data protection and processing**. In the Word MSA, 12.2 was tagged Breach notification correctly.

Likely cause: in a DPA, words like "personal data / processor / controller" appear in nearly every clause and outscore the more specific topic. The section heading only breaks ties, so it doesn't help.

## Why it matters

The topic decides which position Polaris judges a clause against. A 24-hour breach notice judged against the data protection position could miss the conflict with our incident rule.

## Expected

A specific topic named in the clause's own section heading wins over a general one. For example, give a section heading's topic a strong weight, or treat Data protection as a fallback when a more specific topic also matches.
