Sprint 83, Risk Assessments (ADR in COM-1219). PCI DSS 4.0.1 12.3.2 and Appendix D/E (customised approach).

**Chain:** on the chain — migration + API

## What people see

- On a PCI requirement in scope, the company can mark it **met with the customised approach** (Frameworks ▸ PCI ▸ requirement), which then needs a **customised-approach analysis**.
- The analysis (Appendix E2 shape):
  - the requirement and its **customised approach objective**;
  - the **customised control** — what the company does instead;
  - the **assets** in scope;
  - **who could attack it and how** (the malicious actor and their motivation), and the ways the control could fail;
  - likelihood of the objective not being met; the effect;
  - how the control will be tested and how often;
  - the conclusion — the control meets the objective.
- **Approval by senior management**: the approver must have *Approve risk assessments and accept risks*, and the page labels the role in the PDF ("approved by senior management: …"). Same freeze, due every 12 months.
- The requirement page shows "Customised approach — RA-18" and Missing when there's none.
