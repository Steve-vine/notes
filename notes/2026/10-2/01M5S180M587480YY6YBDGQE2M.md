## What Steve said

"I think this feature should probably be removed as it could lead to incorrect information being used or at best is redundant. If a question was generated from existing controls or evidence last time it should again, if none of the information existed and someone had to write the answer from scratch it would be added to the Q&A section, where it gets reviewed periodically."

Agreed with one gap: **client-specific answers**. Examples are DDQ-4's "Please list the services that you provide to MS Amlin" (questions 1, 2, 3, 13 and 16). They're facts about one customer relationship. No control or evidence holds them, and they don't belong in the Q&A library. Steve chose option 1: remove carry-forward, and keep last time as reference for Polaris.

## Behaviour

- **"Carry forward from a previous DDQ" is removed.** That covers the button, the preview, the "Same / Reworded / New this time" marks on the questions list and its filter, and Polaris's carry-forward tools. Answers are always drafted afresh from controls, evidence, Security Assurance Plan entries and the Q&A library.
- **"Start the next one from this" stays.** It still copies the requester, contact and details into a new DDQ.
- **Polaris can read what this requester was told last time.** It reads the frozen record of their most recent sent DDQ(s), using the same "same requester" rule as today (organisation, or contact email domain; the exact address for personal mail). It's matched question by question with this DDQ's questions (same / reworded / no match). It's **reference, never a source**:
  - **Consistency:** when Polaris's new draft says something materially different from last time, it says so in its note to the reviewer, e.g. "Last year we said RTO 4 hours; the BC plan now says 8".
  - **Client-specific questions:** when nothing in Compass answers a question but last time's approved answer does, Polaris may draft it from that. The answer is flagged **"Last time we said this (DDQ-n) — confirm it's still true"** wherever it's shown: the answer window, the review list and the approver's view. It needs "Who can this go to?" like any answer without a source in Compass (COM-1289). The approver's normal approval is the confirmation.
  - Last time's DDQ is **never listed as a source** and never makes an answer citable or shareable.
- Polaris is told in its instructions: prefer a source in Compass; use last time only for what nothing in Compass holds; always flag it.

## Existing questions

- Answers already carried forward stay as **Drafted** answers and are reviewed like any other.
- Each "from DDQ-n — check it's still true" mark becomes the same "Last time we said this (DDQ-n)" flag, so the reviewer still sees where it came from.
- Sent DDQs are frozen and unchanged.

## Implementation notes

- **ADR:** amend ADR 0124 §15 (recertification carry-forward, COM-1264) and record the reference-not-source rule (an ADR 0125 amendment, beside COM-1282/1283).
- **Remove:**
  - `core/ddq_carry.py` apply/plan, the carry-forward routes in `api/v1`, and the assistant tools `ddq_carry_forward`, `ddq_carry_forward_options` and `ddq_preview_carry_forward`;
  - the frontend carry-forward modal, the marks in `QuestionsCard` (`carriedLines`, the "last time" filter), and their tests.
- **Keep and move:** `earlier_ddqs` and the wording matcher (trigram plus the same-reference bonus) into a read module, e.g. `core/ddq_last_time.py`.
- **New read-only Polaris tool**, e.g. `ddq_read_last_time`: for each question, last time's matched question and approved answer as sent, plus its match (same / reworded).
- `ddq_save_answer` gains `from_last_time: "DDQ-n"`. It's stored on the answer version as a flag, not a source link, and shown as above. A sourceless answer still requires the classification.
- **Migration:**
  - convert `ddq_questions.carried_forward` marks that drafted an answer into the new flag on that answer version;
  - drop the column, or keep it read-only if simpler;
  - `write_carried` / "drafted carried forward" (COM-1286's Drafted origin) folds into the Polaris origin.
- The sent-record snapshot (`ddqs.sent_record`) is unchanged; it's the source of the reference.
- Tests: no carry-forward routes or tools remain; the reference tool's matching (moved tests); the flag shown to the approver; the migration on a populated DB with a carried-forward question.
