Sprint 98, Contract Review (ADR 0134, COM-1435).

## What people see

- **Modules ▸ Contracts** (follows the company picker), with tabs **Reviews** (COM-1438), **Positions** (COM-1437), **Commitments** (COM-1443), **Clients** and **Standard contracts**. It shows only to people who can see something in it.
- **Clients:**
  - a table with name, other names, reviews, signed contracts, open commitments;
  - **Add client**: name and other names (e.g. "ACME Ltd", "Acme Group");
  - a client's page lists its reviews, signed contracts and commitments;
  - **Merge** two clients that turn out to be the same, which keeps everything;
  - a client with history can be **archived**, not deleted.
- **Standard contracts:**
  - each one has a name (e.g. "Master Services Agreement", "Data Processing Agreement", "Service Level Schedule") and its current version;
  - **Upload a new version** (Word): it shows version 3 of 3 with who uploaded it, when, and what changed (a summary of changed sections);
  - older versions stay readable;
  - Compass reads each version into its sections and clauses so later reviews can point at "MSA v3, clause 12.2".

## Who can do what

- *View contracts* sees them.
- *Manage positions* adds clients and uploads standard contracts.
- Everything is in the activity log.

## Notes (technical)

- New tables:
  - `clients(company_id, name, aliases[], archived_at)`;
  - `standard_contracts(company_id, name)`;
  - `standard_contract_versions(…, attachment_id, sections JSONB, uploaded_by)`.
- Read sections from Word headings and numbering with python-docx. Fall back to Polaris's layout reading (`core/ddq_layout.py`) only when the structure can't be read.
- The new permissions (four, ADR 0134) go to the built-in Admin. The Modules menu entry follows the screen conventions.
