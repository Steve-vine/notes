Sprint 99, Browser Testing (ADR 0135, COM-1446). Needs the browser (COM-1448).

## What Steve sees

When a sprint reaches staging, each of its tasks gets a comment from Claude:
- **Passed**, **Failed** or **Couldn't check**, with the reason;
- the steps taken and what was seen.

Defects become **new tasks in the same sprint**, fixed forward before Steve is told the sprint is ready.

Steve's UAT can then focus on whether it fits how the team works, not on finding broken buttons. Anything Claude finds that could break again becomes a scripted journey (as in COM-1451).

## What Claude doesn't do

- Sign off a task or move it to Done. UAT sign-off is Steve's.
- Test in any company other than E2E Test Company, or anywhere but staging.
- Claim a pass it couldn't check. If a task needs a real connection (Entra, AD, a cloud, Polaris's AI provider), it's marked **Couldn't check**, with what Steve needs to look at.
