Sprint 100, Actions (ADR 0137 rule 2). Raised by Steve on 2026-10-30. Once INC-9's notification decisions were done, the incident, which was still open, had no action at all. And a report to triage and notification decisions can show at the same time.

## What people see

One row per open incident, always called **Incident**, whose Action follows its stage:

| Stage | Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reported, not triaged | **Triage** | default owner (e.g. Incident Managers) | N days (hours?) after reporting |
| Confirmed, no owner | **Assign an owner** | default owner | N days after confirming |
| Notifications undecided | **Decide notifications** ("3 to decide": Law enforcement, Insurer, Board) | incident owner | earliest notification deadline |
| Being worked (contain, eradicate, recover) | **Respond**, named for the stage ("Contain") | incident owner | 7 days after the last update, as today's "no update" prompt |
| Review required | **Complete the review** | incident owner | review due date |
| Ready to close | **Close** | incident owner | N days after the last stage |

- Only the earliest unfinished step shows. A notification decided while still triaging isn't a second row.
- The company-wide "overdue incidents" roll-up goes (ADR 0137 call).
- Restricted incidents still only reach the people who may read them.

## Notes (technical)

- `core/actions/incidents.py` becomes one source that works out the stage. `_triage`, `_unowned`, `_notifications`, `_reviews`, `_stale` and `_overdue_rollup` fold into it.
- Keep the digest ledger entry the portal report's immediate email writes (`INCIDENT_TRIAGE_ACTION`), so triage isn't mailed twice.
- Hours: notification deadlines are exact moments. Keep "due soon inside 24 hours" and overdue to the hour.
