Sprint 96, Continuous Control Checks (ADR 0132, COM-1415).

## What people see

Six checks. Each one says in a sentence what it found, and lists the people or accounts it means.

| Check | Fails when | Controls (screen ref) |
|---|---|---|
| **Everyone has MFA registered** | any enabled member account in the company's scope has no second factor registered. A method registered that policy won't accept counts as failing too, and is listed separately | IDM.13 |
| **Conditional Access requires MFA** | no enabled policy (not report-only) requires MFA for all users on all cloud apps. Exclusions are listed, with their recorded reason where Access Control has one | IDM.13 |
| **Admins have MFA** | anyone holding a directory admin role has no MFA registered | IDM.14 |
| **No dormant accounts** | an enabled account has not signed in for 90 days (threshold). Accounts never signed in and created more than 90 days ago count as dormant | IDM.6, IDM.5 |
| **Leavers are disabled** | someone whose leaver request finished still has an enabled account | IDM.2 |
| **Top admin roles are few** | more than 4 people (threshold) hold Global Administrator or Privileged Role Administrator permanently. Eligible-only holders don't count | ACC.3 |

- **Can't tell**, with the reason in words:
  - "Compass can't see sign-ins: this needs Entra ID P1";
  - "Compass isn't allowed to read MFA registrations";
  - "The directory hasn't been read since …".
- **A person is named** as Access Control names them, and links to their person page. Someone who can't see Access Control sees counts only.
- **Active Directory only:** dormant and leaver checks use AD's last logon and enabled flag. The MFA checks say Can't tell ("MFA is an Entra ID question").

## Who can do what

The engine runs them (COM-1416). Thresholds and switches are in Settings ▸ Checks (COM-1420).

## Notes (technical)

- Data already in the mirror:
  - `DirectoryUserAuthMethods.is_mfa_registered` / `is_mfa_capable`, where no row means unknown;
  - `DirectorySyncStatus.auth_methods_available`, `sign_in_activity_available` and their reasons, which give Can't tell;
  - sign-in activity from the hourly sweep (COM-492);
  - CA policies with `requires_mfa` (directory.py `_ca_grant_control`) and the CA exclusions with reasons (COM-501 and follow-ups);
  - `DirectoryRoleAssignment` with its assignment type;
  - finished leaver requests.
- Company scope: the same set of people Access Control counts for the company (ADR 0132). Write a helper if one doesn't exist, and test it.
- Guests and service accounts: members only for MFA and dormant checks. Exclude break-glass accounts that are listed with a reason as CA exclusions from "Conditional Access requires MFA", but still list them.
- Out of scope: logs of any sort, and on-prem MFA.
