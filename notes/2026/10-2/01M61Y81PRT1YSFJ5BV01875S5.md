Sprint 94, Control Exceptions (ADR 0130, COM-1398). Builds on COM-1400 (the one "excepted on day D" rule).

Steve, 2026-10-28: excepted controls are **shown separately, not counted as met**.

## What people see

- **Compliance page:**
  - the core gauge gains an **Excepted** segment between Implemented/Partial and Not implemented, in its own colour, with its count;
  - the headline reads e.g. **72% met · 4% excepted** — the 72% is the same number as before;
  - per domain and per tier the same two figures.
- **Frameworks:**
  - a requirement whose only shortfalls are excepted controls reads **Excepted** (its own pill), not Met and not Unmet;
  - framework coverage shows met % and excepted % the same way;
  - a requirement with an excepted control *and* an unmet one stays Unmet / Partial.
- **Dashboard:** the Compliance card gains "4% excepted" under the figure; domain, tier and framework panels show an excepted sliver.
- **Over time:** the nightly snapshot records excepted too; the Compliance chart gains an **Excepted** line from the day this ships. Days before have none — the chart doesn't invent them.
- **Shortfalls** (Compliance ▸ shortfalls and the framework gap lists) keep excepted controls in, marked **Excepted until 3 Mar 2027**, sorted after the unexcepted ones, with a filter to hide them.
