## What Steve saw

He double-clicked the Polaris window's drag bar to minimise or restore it. After that, the window could no longer be resized by dragging.

## Likely cause (to confirm in a real browser)

The drag handling itself survives a double-click. The state code is clean, and `toggleCollapsed` and `setHeight` both work afterwards. The browser is the likely culprit:
- **A double-click selects text.** It picks up the word under the pointer or near the bar, such as the status bar's place name.
- **The next press on the bar starts the browser's own drag of that selection.** That fires `pointercancel`, so the grip's move handler never runs, and the window looks stuck.
- **Clicking elsewhere to clear the selection** would bring the drag back, which is a quick way to confirm this.

A second possibility: the grip keeps pointer capture through the collapse animation, and loses `pointerup` when the window moves under the pointer. `dragging` would then stay true or false wrongly.

## Behaviour

- **Double-clicking the bar** toggles between minimised and the last height, as now.
- **Dragging works straight afterwards, every time**, however many times it has been double-clicked.
- **Double-clicking the bar never selects any text** on the page or in the window.

## Notes for the build

- **Reproduce first** in the headless Chromium harness or a real browser: dblclick the grip, then try to drag. jsdom can't show this, because it has no selection or native drag-and-drop.
- **On the grip (`PolarisWindow.tsx`):**
  - add `userSelect: 'none'`;
  - call `e.preventDefault()` in `onGripDown`, which stops the selection and native drag;
  - add `onDragStart={e => e.preventDefault()}`;
  - on `onLostPointerCapture`, set `dragging.current = false`, so a lost capture never leaves the grip half-dragging;
  - make sure `onDoubleClick` doesn't fight a drag that has just finished.
- **Tests:**
  - vitest that pointerdown on the grip is default-prevented and the grip is `user-select: none`;
  - a lost capture resets dragging;
  - the real-browser check of dblclick then drag, noted in the Done comment.
- Independent: no API change and no migration.
