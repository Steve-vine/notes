Sprint 84, DDQ-1 (ADR in COM-1245).

**Chain:** on the chain — migration + API

## What people see

- On a question, or several selected: **Assign…** a person in Compass, with a due date (defaults to a few days before the DDQ's due date) and a note ("Pen test scope for the Leeds office?").
- The person gets an **Actions** item and the usual email: "Answer a question for DDQ-7 (Acme Ltd), due 30 Oct".
- Opening it shows **only that question**: its section, the client's wording, the note, and any draft. They answer it in the same form (sources, evidence, classification) and **Submit**. They don't need View DDQs and can't see the rest of the DDQ (COM-1245 call 9).
- The question becomes **Answered** and is back with the DDQ's owner. The owner can reassign or take it back at any time; the assignee can **hand it back** ("I don't know either") with a note.
- The question page shows who it was assigned to, when, and the trail.

## Notes (technical)

- Action source per ADR 0055; the mail comes from it.
- Permission: the assignee may read and write only their assigned question (and its sources' pickers, filtered to what they can see).

**Done when:** a person with no DDQ permission answers their assigned question from Actions and can't open the DDQ itself.
