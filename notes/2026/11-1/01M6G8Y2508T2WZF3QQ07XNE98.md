Found in the sprint 102 pre-UAT (2026-11-03). Needs Steve's call before it's built.

## What people see

Each source names products its own way. On staging, Cisco NX-OS appears as `nx-os` (NVD), `cisco_nx-os_software` and `cisco_nx-os_system_software_in_aci_mode` (Cisco's advisories), and `nx-os_software` (Polaris's reading of a Cisco notice). Snort 3 has four names the same way. A product rule, or a confirmed link on the Products tab, picks one of these names, so it misses items the other sources filed under another. Microsoft, Fortinet and PHP agree across sources, so it mainly affects Cisco and Polaris readings so far.

## Proposal

- Each source's names are mapped onto one product, so "Cisco · NX-OS" covers them all. The map starts from the CPE names NVD uses, plus a list for the vendors whose own names differ (Cisco first).
- Polaris is given the known product names to choose from, rather than making up its own.
- The product picker shows one entry per product.

**Chain:** on the chain (likely a migration for the name map).

**Done when:** a watch list on Cisco NX-OS catches NVD, Cisco and Polaris items alike.
