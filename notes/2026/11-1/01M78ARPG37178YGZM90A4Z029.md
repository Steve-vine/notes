Found in the sprint 116 round-3 pre-UAT pass on staging (3175d2ad, 2026-11-12). Not caused by round 3.

**What happens:** with Polaris open (the bottom panel), the panel sits above dialogs, so a dialog's footer can be hidden underneath it and can't be clicked:
- at 390 px, SAP ▸ Add entry: Cancel and Add entry are behind the panel;
- at 1440 px, New technology asset scrolled to the bottom: Cancel and Create sit at y 805–841 while the panel starts at 690, and a click lands on Polaris's resize grip.
Escape still closes the dialog.

**What it should do:** a dialog is always fully usable while Polaris is open: the dialog sits above the panel, or the panel steps aside while a dialog is open.

**Done when:** with Polaris open, Create/Cancel on the asset form and Add entry/Cancel on SAP can be clicked, on desktop and at 390 px; Polaris is usable again once the dialog closes; light and dark; console clean.

Screenshots: ~/compass-uat/spsp226/COM-1759/09-polaris-panel-over-modal.png, 11-desktop-polaris-over-tall-modal.png. Frontend only, independent.
