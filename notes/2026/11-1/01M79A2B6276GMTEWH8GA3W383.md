Found in the sprint 116 round-4 pre-UAT pass on staging (6e988b9d, 2026-11-13), from COM-1771.

**What happens:** when a technology asset's dependency is *changed* rather than added or removed — as happens when a duplicate asset is deleted and its links move to the one it duplicates — Admin ▸ Activity and the asset's history still read "updated container dependency a6804d43-… Depends on: 3dc4e349-…" or "… Container: bad2ab53-… →". COM-1771 covered dependencies being added and removed, and its rewrite of old rows touched only those.

**What it should do:** read in screen words with references, e.g. "AST-49 now depends on AST-46 instead of AST-48" (or "AST-46 now depends on AST-42 instead of …" for the other end), with no identifiers. Old rows of this kind read the same way where both assets still exist, and otherwise say what happened without identifiers.

**Done when:** a fresh duplicate-delete that moves a dependency reads in screen words; the two existing rows in E2E Test Company (12 Nov 16:34 and 17:55, Entity "Technology asset dependency") read in screen words; tests cover new and old rows. On the chain if old rows are rewritten (migration).
