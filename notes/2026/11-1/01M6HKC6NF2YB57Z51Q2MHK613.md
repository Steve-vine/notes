Sprint "Initiatives - New Feature" (ADR 0140, COM-1552). Builds on COM-1555 and COM-1556. Steve's idea (2026-11-03): controls and content may have no action of their own, so you can mark them as needing review when you bring them in.

## What people see

- **When adding.** Adding controls (COM-1555), or adding content directly (COM-1556), offers **Requires review**, ticked off by default, and a **due date**. The date defaults to the initiative's target date, or 30 days from today if there is none.
- **Later.** Any control or content row can be marked, or unmarked, from its row menu. That includes content a control brought in.
- **Who gets it:**
  - for a control, the assessment owner in this company (unowned goes to the type's default owner, ADR 0137);
  - for content, its reviewers.
- **How it shows.** It appears as "Control · Review" or "Content · Review", due on the chosen date, with the reason underneath: **"Part of initiative INI-3 · Ransomware readiness (Test Company)"**. The reason also shows on the control's and the content's own pages while it's open.
- **One row, never two.**
  - If the item already has a review scheduled, the existing row is used. It's due on the earlier of the two dates, and the reason is added.
  - Two initiatives marking the same thing share the one action and show both reasons.
- **Cleared by reviewing.** Recording the next review clears it:
  - for a control, saving its assessment with a review date on or after the day it was marked;
  - for content, recording a review.

  It then shows as **Reviewed** with the date, on the initiative's row and on the action. Scheduled review dates roll on as normal; marking never changes them.
- **Unmarking.** Unmarking before the review drops the action, unless another initiative still wants it.
- **Content is shared.** Content belongs to every company. A review asked for by one company's initiative is still one review of the library item. The reason names the company.

## Notes (technical)

- Follow the runbook sent-back-for-review pattern (`content_review_request.py`, COM-1412).
- Add `initiative_review_requests` (initiative_id, item_type control | content_item, item_id, due_on, requested_at, requested_by, resolved_at, resolved_by_review_id).
- Resolve it in the same transaction as the assessment save or content review.
- In `core/actions/company.py` `_assessment_reviews` and `library.py` `_content_reviews`:
  - due = min(next_review_at, earliest open request);
  - emit a row even when next_review_at is null and a request is open;
  - carry `reasons`.
- Keep the ADR 0137 one-action-per-thing tests green.
- Content review actions are company-less today. The reason carries the company name.
- The Actions page and the portal render the reason line. Check `links.py` for the portal destination.
