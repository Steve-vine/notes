## Why

Steve, testing on 2026-11-12: some pen-test findings can't be put on an existing asset, so he ends up "having to create a new asset as a placeholder or picking a related one".

This task covers findings that **aren't about any one asset**: a weak password policy, missing email protection on the domain, physical security, a successful phishing test, shared credentials, "no MFA on the VPN" where the tester means the whole estate. Today every vulnerability must sit on exactly one technology asset, so these get forced onto a made-up asset or onto the wrong one. Either way, that asset's figures and support picture are wrong.

The companion task COM-1775 covers the other case (the thing exists but isn't in Inventory: "New asset…"). Steve chose both and skipped "raise as a gap or risk instead" (2026-11-12).

## What the person sees

**Recording**
- On a pen-test import, each finding's system picker has **Company-wide (no single system)** as a choice of its own. It can't be combined with systems: a finding is either on systems or company-wide.
- **Record a vulnerability** offers the same choice.
- Confirming makes **one** company-wide vulnerability for that finding, with:
  - the report as its source;
  - first seen on the report's date;
  - the company's **default vulnerability owner** (there's no system owner to inherit), changeable as usual;
  - a fix-by date from its severity's target, as any other.
- A retest treats it like any other finding: Resolved, Still open or Not retested.

**In the register**
- It shows **Company-wide** where a system name would be, as a pill.
- The System filter has a **Company-wide** choice. Grouping by system puts them in their own group.
- It counts in every figure, the Overview and the Register's counts. Per-system figures leave it out, and the Overview says "N company-wide" beside the per-system breakdown so totals still add up.
- It appears on no asset's page, and in the user portal only for its owner (as "Company-wide").

**Working it**
- Everything works as on any vulnerability: assign, comment, change severity, mark fixed (a person marks it, as pen-test items are), accept, false positive, logged in error, raise or link a risk. A risk raised from it is linked to no system.
- **Move to a system…**: when it turns out to belong to one asset, the person picks that asset. The vulnerability keeps its reference, history, comments, owner and fix-by date. The move is recorded in the activity log with who did it and why (a reason is asked for). If that asset already has the same issue recorded, the move is refused with a link to it, as editing a CVE is today.
- There's no move the other way. A vulnerability on the wrong system is still logged in error and recorded again.

**Scanners never make one.** A scanner sighting Compass can't place still waits on Unmatched, as now. Company-wide is a person's choice only.

## Notes for the build

- **On the chain, and a decision**: a vulnerability's system becomes optional (migration).
  - The per-system uniqueness of an issue must still hold for company-wide ones: one company-wide record per issue per company. That needs a partial unique index, or NULLS NOT DISTINCT.
  - Check every place that assumes a system: the register, figures and snapshots, asset pages, the user portal, raising a risk "linked to its system", owner defaulting, the CSV/exports, Security Feed matches by asset, and the support (ADR 0150) and Continuity views.
  - API schemas and `schema.d.ts` change.
- Amend **ADR 0129** (dated amendment; append-only), in §1 and §7a: a vulnerability is on one system or company-wide; who may choose it; move to a system; scanners never produce one.
- **Polaris** parity:
  - `vulnerabilities_record` takes `company_wide=true` in place of systems;
  - `vulnerabilities_list` filters on it;
  - a `vulnerabilities_move_to_system` tool, with a reason;
  - COM-1772's pen-test finding update can mark a finding company-wide.

- A retest marks a company-wide finding Resolved, and it becomes Fixed, confirmed by retest.
