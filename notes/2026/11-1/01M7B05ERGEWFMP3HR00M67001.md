Steve's design, 2026-11-13. Builds on COM-1799 (periods) and the re-run / custom-range task.

## The collected check's card on a control's Evidence tab

**Main section**
- Left side: the title, description and collection dates stay as they are.
- Right side: the **Page** and **Raw data** links go. They move to each entry in the history.
- ⋯ menu: **Collect now** goes. It's now a button in the details.

**Details (expanded)**
- The "Still needs a person: …" text in the details goes. It repeats what the card already says above.
- The line per account ("FreshService · FreshService: 231 items, taken …, still true on … · Page (PDF) · Raw data") is replaced by the **history list**:
  - A scrollable box, newest first, one row per capture, showing:
    - the date it ran
    - the period it covers ("6–12 Nov 2026"), "as at 13 Nov 2026" for a snapshot, or "One-off · 1 Jan–31 Mar 2027" for a custom range
    - the item count
    - the account, when the check reads more than one
    - its state (filed, held, collecting, failed with why)
  - A tick box on each row, and an **All / None** tick box at the top.
- Buttons under the list, acting on the ticked rows:
  - **Evidence** downloads the readable pages (one PDF, or a zip when several are ticked).
  - **Raw data** downloads the JSON (one file, or a zip).
  - **Re-run** collects the ticked periods again. It asks to confirm, saying how many periods.
  - **Custom range** opens a modal with a date-range picker. On Run it starts a one-off and adds it to the list as "Collecting…".
  - **Collect now** collects now. For a snapshot check this is its only way to run outside the schedule.
- Buttons that don't apply are disabled with the reason: nothing ticked; a snapshot can't be re-run or given a range; no permission.

## Done when

- The card and details look as above for a records check, a snapshot check, and a check with two accounts.
- Tick boxes, All / None, both downloads, Re-run, Custom range and Collect now work against the API.
- The list updates when a run finishes.
- Collect now is gone from the ⋯ menu. The duplicated "Still needs a person" text and the per-account Page / Raw data line are gone.
- There are tests for selection, the disabled states, the modal, and downloads of one and several.
- The screen-conventions and Browser smoke tests pass.
