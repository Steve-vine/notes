Sprint "Checks - Coverage and batch work" (COM-1605). Builds on COM-1608 (problems grouped by cause). Held captures already reach Actions (COM-1603); this task covers the rest. All follow ADR 0137: one action per thing.

## What people see

- **A Collection integration has an Owner:** its creator by default, changeable on the tile's Overview (Manage integrations). If the owner leaves, it falls back to the people with Manage integrations, with "Owner left" on the tile.
- **"Integration · Fix sign-in"**: "MongoDB – Production can't sign in: 31 checks waiting".
  - It goes to the Collection integration's owner, or to the people with Manage integrations for a Core account (Azure, AWS, Microsoft, AD).
  - It is due at once, links to the account, and clears itself when the next read works.
- **"Integration · Grant permission"**: "Compass's AWS role needs `config:DescribeConfigRules` for 3 checks". It goes to the same people, with the exact permission, and clears when a read works.
- **"Check · Repair"** for a check Broken for any other reason (API changed, too big). It goes to whoever approved the check, or to the assessment's owner if that person has left, and offers "Ask Polaris to repair".
- **The "what changed" email** mentions broken checks in one line ("4 checks broken, 1 integration can't sign in"), not a list.
- **Nothing is raised for Can't tell** on a built-in check. That's unchanged.
