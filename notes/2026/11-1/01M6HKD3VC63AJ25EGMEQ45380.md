Sprint "Initiatives - New Feature" (ADR 0140, COM-1552). Builds on COM-1555, COM-1556 and COM-1557.

## What people see

- **Actions tab.** Every open action on the initiative's items, for **everyone**, not just you:
  - gap Remediate;
  - risk Create a treatment plan;
  - treatment Complete;
  - routine risk, control and content reviews;
  - Requires review rows (COM-1557);
  - anything else Actions already raises on those items, such as an exception running out or evidence out of date.

  Left-out items' actions are not shown.
- **Layout.** The same columns as the Actions page: Type, Action, item, owner, due, status (Overdue, Due soon, Upcoming, Later). The same sort is used: due date. The filters are owner (anyone / me) and status.
  - Rows that count towards the percentage carry a small "counts" marker. A routine review shows without it, with a tooltip explaining why repeating reviews don't count.
- **Counts.** The tab title carries the open count, and the Initiatives list's "open actions" column uses the same number.
- **The initiative's own action.** While the initiative is Active, its owner has **"Initiative · Deliver"** on their Actions list:
  - due on the target date;
  - with no target date, it's undated and handled as any undated type under ADR 0137 (due N days after it started);
  - it links to the initiative and goes when the initiative is Complete or Cancelled;
  - it's a new action kind with its own notification window, configurable on Admin ▸ Actions like the others.
- **Reading only.** Actions on items the reader can't open show as text, as the Actions page already does.

## Notes (technical)

- Simplest: run the company's action sources unscoped, then keep rows whose (type, id) is in the initiative's set. Check the cost on staging with a 40-control initiative. If it's slow, add an item-id filter to `ActionScope`.
- New `ActionKind("initiative", "Initiative", "Deliver", ...)` in a new `core/actions/initiatives.py` source. Default owner = the initiative owner (always set).
