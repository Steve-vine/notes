Sprint "Secrets - New Module" (ADR 0141, COM-1563). Builds on COM-1572. It's the first source Compass reads, and costs nothing new: Access Control already reads every app registration's and service principal's secrets and certificates, with start and end dates (COM-498).

## What people see

- **Generated by ▸ Entra app.** When adding or editing a secret, pick an app, then one of its secrets or certificates ("Billing Sync — secret 'prod', ends 12 Mar 2027"). The secret takes:
  - its **kind** (client secret or certificate);
  - its **created** date;
  - its **expiry**, which drives Next due. Entra secrets always expire, so they're never Long-lived.
- **Not yet tracked.** Secrets ▸ Discovered gains a section **"Keys at a source, not yet tracked"**: Entra secrets and certificates that no registered secret uses. It's folded by default, with a count. Make a secret from one, or discard it. It raises nothing.
- **Pairing a stored copy with its Entra key.** The Kept in card's "Link automatically" also offers `compass-source-key` = the Entra key ID. A vault entry with that tag is attached, and the pairing is recorded ("Paired by its source key tag").
- **Rotation proven without asking.** When Compass sees, in order:
  1. a new secret on the same app;
  2. every stored copy updated after it;
  3. the old secret removed from the app,

  it records the rotation itself, proven **Seen at source and store**. No confirmation is needed. The Needs attention sentences:
  - **"There's a new key at Entra, but the stored copy hasn't changed. Systems using it will break when the old key is removed."**
  - **"The stored copy changed, but the old key is still on the app and still works."**
- **The app's page** in Access Control shows which of its keys are tracked as secrets (SEC-N), linking to them.

## Notes (technical)

- The directory mirror is **global**: the tenant is shared, while secrets are per company. A secret references `(owner_kind, owner_id, key_id, credential_type)`. Any company's secret may pick any app's credential.
- `directory_app_credentials` rows are **deleted** when the credential goes (COM-498, by design). So the secret keeps its own copy of the key id and the dates it last saw. A row vanishing is how "removed at source" is detected, and it's recorded on the secret at the reconcile pass.
- The Expired credentials report in Access Control stays as it is.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration and API).

**Done when:** on staging, in E2E Test Company:
- a secret made from a seeded Entra credential shows its expiry as Next due;
- a seeded store entry tagged with that credential's key ID is paired;
- simulating a new credential, a store bump and the old credential's removal records *Seen at source and store* with no action raised;
- simulating only the new credential shows the "hasn't changed" sentence.
