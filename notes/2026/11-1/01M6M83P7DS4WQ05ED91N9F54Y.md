On an initiative's content list, the row menu's **Record review** records the review at once. It's dated today with no comment, and the person gets no chance to say anything. Recording the review from the content's own page opens the **Review content** window first, with a review date (defaulting to today) and a comment.

**Wanted behaviour:** Record review on an initiative opens the same Review content window as the content page. Saving records the review with the chosen date and comment, and resolves the item's Requires review mark as it does today. Cancelling records nothing.

**Done when:** in E2E Test Company, on an initiative with content marked Requires review, Record review opens a window with Review date and Comment. Saving with a back-dated date and a comment updates the item's Last reviewed and clears its Requires review mark. The review, comment included, shows in that content's review history. Cancelling leaves everything unchanged.

**Implementation:** on staging, `initiatives/ItemsTab.tsx:236–241` calls `useRecordContentReview` (`initiatives/work.ts:39`), which POSTs `/api/v1/content/{slug}/reviews` with `body: {}`. The window is the private `ReviewModal` in `pages/ContentDetailPage.tsx:1261` (Review date plus Comment, body `{reviewed_on, comment}`). Move it to a shared component, e.g. `content/ReviewContentModal.tsx`, and use it from both places. Pass the initiative's refresh as `onSuccess`, and keep the content page's own cache invalidation. Add a Vitest case for open → save → posted body, and one for cancel.

**Independent:** frontend only, no API change.

Found by Steve while testing Initiatives (2026-11-04).
