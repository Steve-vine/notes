## What people see

- A column of long ids may grow a little wider (e.g. a maximum around 56 characters for a column whose values are long unbroken ids), so an ~85-character id reads in two lines.
- The header row is no taller than it needs to be (two lines at most for typical names), so ten rows fit at 1440 × 900 once the page has scrolled past its heading.
- A header also breaks at camelCase humps and at @ / - / _ , so no piece runs past its column or under its menu or tick.
- A short header (≤16 characters) never wraps, on any view (including Change the fields it keeps).
