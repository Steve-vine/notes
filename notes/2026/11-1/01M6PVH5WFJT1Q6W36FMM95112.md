Sprint "Evidence Collection - Integrations" (ADR 0142, COM-1595). Builds on COM-1598 (Core reads), COM-1596 (Collection accounts) and COM-1599 (Polaris jobs). Scheduled runs come next (COM-1602). This task is designing one, and approving it.

## What people see

- **On a control's page, for a company:** a **How Compass collects this** card, beside the existing Checks card. For a control with nothing yet, it offers **Ask Polaris to collect this** (needs Record assessments).
- **Polaris's job** reads the control (its wording, guidance and framework requirements), looks at which Core and Collection accounts the company has, tries reads, and proposes one or more **collected checks**. Each has:
  - **a name in words:** "Conditional Access policies that require MFA";
  - **what it reads, and from where:** "Microsoft 365: Conditional Access policies, from the Contoso tenant". For a Collection service, every account the company has, listed;
  - **what the evidence should show:** "every policy, its state, who it covers and the controls it grants";
  - **how much of the control it covers:** *Fully*, or *Partly*, with what still needs a person ("the quarterly review of exclusions is a meeting, not a setting");
  - **a sample capture** from each account, shown as a readable table with the raw data a click away.
- Polaris can instead answer **"This can't be collected automatically"**, with the reason ("evidence is the training records held by HR"). Accepting that records the control as manual. Coverage figures use it in the next sprint.
- **Approving: the first-capture check.** The reviewer sees each sample capture with any field Polaris thinks might be sensitive **highlighted** (names, email addresses, phone numbers, anything that looks like a key or token), and can:
  - **leave a field out**: it's never kept from then on;
  - **rename** the check, or change its schedule (daily / weekly / monthly, weekly by default);
  - **Approve**, which makes it live. The sample becomes the first capture in the Evidence register, and the shape the next ones are compared with;
  - **Discard**.
- **A person can edit an approved check's read by hand** (an "Advanced" section showing the request), so a small fix doesn't need Polaris. Editing sends it back for a fresh first-capture check.
- **The card afterwards** lists each collected check: its accounts, schedule, last capture, and who approved it and when.

## Notes (technical)

- Job kind `design_collection`: tools `try_read` (COM-1598 / COM-1596, accounts limited to the company's), `read_page` (optional: vendor docs for a Collection service), and form tool `propose_checks`.
- A collected check is data:
  - `collected_checks`: company, control key (ADR 0059: by key, never ref), name, `reads` (one per platform or account, as JSON in COM-1598's / COM-1596's terms), field list with left-out fields, coverage (full / partial + words), schedule, status (proposed / live / off), approved_by / at, the approved shape;
  - `collection_captures`: check, account, taken_at, item count, sha256, storage key, status.
- Sensitive-field highlighting: Polaris's flag plus a deterministic pass (email-like, phone-like, `looks_like_key` from the Secrets module).
- Captures are files in storage (`core/storage.py`, key `evidence/{company}/collected/...`): JSON for the raw data, a rendered HTML or PDF page for reading.

**Chain:** on the chain (migration and API).

**Done when:** on staging, in E2E Test Company:
- Polaris is asked to collect evidence for an MFA control (e.g. CRD.3) and proposes a check reading Conditional Access policies with a sample;
- an email-address field is highlighted and left out;
- Approve puts the first capture in the Evidence register on that control.
