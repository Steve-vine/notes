Sprint 74, MCP - Inventory (ADR in COM-1023). Built on COM-1024 and COM-1025, and on sprint 72's **COM-1019**. Asset recertification shares its schedules with Access Control, so this task reuses those tools rather than building a second set.

## What people can ask of it

- **Read:**
  - a technology or data asset's **recertification schedule**: how often it's reviewed, by which owners, and how many sign-offs it needs;
  - its **past reviews**: who signed off, what they kept and what they asked to remove;
  - a **download link** to a review's evidence file.
- **Write (whatever the person's role allows on screen; for assets, that is Inventory's *Run recertification*):**
  - add, edit or delete an asset's schedule;
  - **start a review now**, which emails each owner their assignment.
- **Not here:** owners sign off in the Compass Portal, which is out of scope. Approving the removals they ask for is on Access ▸ Recertification, so it is sprint 72's COM-1019, and it needs Access permissions there too, as it does on screen.
- **Example:** "Which technology assets have no recertification schedule? Set AST-7 to quarterly, reviewed by its owners, and start the first one now."

## Notes (technical)

- Wraps the per-asset reads in `containers.py` and `data_assets.py`: `recert-schedules`, `recert-reviews`, `recert-reviews/{i}`, and the `evidence` link (a `link()` to the CSV, not its contents).
- **Writes go through COM-1019's schedule tools.** Make sure those:
  - accept an asset named by its ref through `find_asset`;
  - are offered to a person with `inventory.run_recertification` alone. `_require_kind` in `access_recert.py` already insists on that grant for asset schedules.

  `GET /recert-schedules` needs `access.view`. An Inventory-only person therefore reads schedules through the per-asset route, so the tools must not depend on the list.
- Trigger's description says it sends mail.
- Either a new module `tools/inventory_recert.py`, or additions to COM-1019's module. Pick the one that keeps a single schedule tool set.
- Tests: an Inventory-only role can schedule and trigger an asset review but gets no Access-only tools; the evidence link resolves; a schedule change is recorded via assistant.

**Done when:** on staging, a role with Inventory permissions only has the assistant schedule an asset review and start it, and the owner gets the review in the Portal.
