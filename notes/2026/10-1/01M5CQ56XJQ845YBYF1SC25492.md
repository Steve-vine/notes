Sprint 81, Trust Centre Portal (ADR in COM-1157). The first build task. Every later task builds on it.

## What people see

- **Modules ▸ Trust Centre**, after Data Subject Requests, shown to anyone who can view it. Company-scoped like Incidents: the company picker chooses whose trust centre you see.
- Tabs: **Documents**, **Visitors**, **Requests**, **Content**, **Activity** (each added by a later task; hidden until then) and **Settings**.
- **Settings**, per company:
  - **Portal**: on or off. While off, every link and the public page show the generic "isn't valid" page, and nothing is emailed;
  - **Branding**: logo, title, intro text (the paragraph at the top of the overview);
  - **Contact email**: shown to visitors ("questions? contact …");
  - **Emails come from**: display name and from-address, as in the vendor portal's branding;
  - **NDA wording**: optional text a visitor accepts before seeing restricted documents (used by the NDA task). Each saved change becomes a new numbered wording;
  - **Default link length**: 30 days, changeable.

## Who can do what

| Permission | Allows |
|---|---|
| View the trust centre | See the module, visitors, requests and activity |
| Manage trust centre visitors | Invite, resend, extend and revoke; open up restricted documents; approve and decline requests |
| Manage the trust centre | Change Settings, documents and content |

Built-in Admin only. The starter roles stay frozen.

## Notes (technical)

- Permissions `trust_centre.view`, `trust_centre.manage_visitors`, `trust_centre.manage` in `core/permissions.py`; a new table in `brief/permission-catalogue.md`.
- `nav.ts`: a new `NavGate`, section Modules, path `/trust-centre`; record it in the IA brief's Modules table.
- A company-scoped settings table modelled on `vendor_portal_branding` (logo stored the same way); NDA wordings as their own versioned rows so acceptances can point at one. Audited.

**Done when:** an Admin sees the module and can save the company's settings; someone without the permission doesn't see it in the menu or reach it by URL.
