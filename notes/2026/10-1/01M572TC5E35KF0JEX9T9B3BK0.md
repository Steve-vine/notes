Sprint 76, Incidents - Register (ADR in COM-1063). Builds on notifications (COM-1071) and the review (COM-1074). It answers IMA.18, HIPAA 164.414(b) and UK GDPR Art 33(5).

## What people see

**Close** (for handlers) shows a **closure checklist**. Each item is ticked or explains what's missing, and the incident closes only when all are ticked:
- triaged, with severity and categories set;
- recovered (or recovery marked not applicable);
- **every triggered notification decided**: sent, sent late or not required;
- for Privacy, **a breach conclusion recorded** (COM-1070);
- for AI, the AI section completed (COM-1072);
- **the post-incident review done**, or marked not needed where optional (COM-1074);
- a **closing summary**: one paragraph that says what happened and how it ended, for the register and exports.

**Once closed the incident is frozen.**
- Details, sections, links, notifications and the review can't change.
- Two things are still allowed: timeline entries marked "after closure", and adding evidence with a reason.

**Correct** (for handlers) changes one fact on a closed incident (a time, a count, a typo in the summary) and needs a reason. The incident's history shows the before and after and who made it.

**Reopen** (for handlers) needs a reason ("new evidence: more records affected") and returns the incident to Investigating. The first close and the reopen both stay in its history. Closing again runs the checklist again.

## Rules

- Not an incident and Duplicate outcomes from triage are closed without the checklist, and can be reopened the same way.
- **Nothing in the module has a delete**, closed or open (ADR).
- The register shows "Closed on … by …" and, if it was reopened, "reopened once".

## Notes (technical)

- Enforce the freeze on every write route (SAP's `require_open`). Corrections in their own table, as with SAP.

**Done when:** closing runs the checklist; closed incidents refuse changes outside the rules; correct and reopen work with reasons and history.
