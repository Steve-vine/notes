Sprint 76, Incidents - Register (ADR in COM-1063). The first build task; the register depends on it.

## What people see

Admin ▸ Rubrics gains an **Incidents** card, for people who can manage rubrics.

**Categories**: a list someone can add to, reword, reorder and retire.
- Each has a **name**, a **definition of what counts** (shown to whoever triages), and **which sections it adds to an incident**: Service impact, Security impact, Personal data breach, AI. It can add several sections, or none.
- It also has **"Post-incident review always required"**.
- It ships with:
  - **Availability**: a major outage (P1/P2) affecting delivery of services. Adds Service impact.
  - **Security**: a confirmed or reasonably suspected compromise of confidentiality, integrity or availability. Adds Security impact.
  - **Privacy**: any personal data breach, at any severity. Adds Personal data breach. Review always required.
  - **AI**: an AI incident as defined in the company's ISO 42001 scope (harmful output, material error, bias, drift, misuse). Adds AI. Review always required.
- A category **in use can't be deleted, only retired**. A retired one stays on the incidents that have it but can't be picked for new ones.

**Severity**: four fixed levels, **P1–P4**. The name and definition of each are editable, and each has **"Post-incident review required"** (on for P1 and P2).
- P1 Critical: a service unavailable to many customers, or a confirmed breach with serious harm.
- P2 High: a major degradation, or a confirmed compromise that is contained.
- P3 Medium: limited impact, with a workaround.
- P4 Low: minimal impact.

The wording is a starting point for Steve to edit.

## Rules

- Every edit is kept in the rubric's history, like the other rubrics.
- Organisation-wide: one set for every company.

## Notes (technical)

- Follow `data_type` (status active/retired, position, `PUT /order`, 409 on delete when in use) and the fixed-scale pattern of `maturity_level` for severity. Include revision tables.
- Store category sections as a small fixed set (`service`, `security`, `privacy`, `ai`) that the incident pages switch on.
- Seed data goes in the migration, using raw-SQL-safe inserts (memory: seeding rows in a migration).

**Done when:** the card edits categories and severity; categories in use refuse deletion and can be retired; the seeds match the above.
