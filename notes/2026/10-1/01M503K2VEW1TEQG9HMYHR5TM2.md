Configure data loss prevention enforcement across email, cloud storage, endpoints, and network egress points used to transmit or move customer PII, CRM data, call recordings, and customer content, including transfers between the UK and US offices, to EU/US cloud systems, and to third-party telecom, workflow, and AI-answering platforms. Require encrypted channels (TLS or equivalent) for all such transmission, and restrict export, extraction, or removable-media transfer of sensitive data to authorized roles with documented business need.

Given the very-high risk ratings assigned to cross-border personal data transfer and to interception or mishandling of recorded calls, configure DLP and transfer-monitoring alerts to be reviewed at least weekly rather than on an ad hoc basis, with findings fed directly into incident response. Test DLP rule effectiveness at least quarterly and retrain staff handling PII, call recordings, or CRM data on secure handling and transmission practices at least annually, or sooner after a material change to systems or vendors.

Retain DLP configuration records, weekly alert review logs, quarterly test results, and training completion records as evidence.
