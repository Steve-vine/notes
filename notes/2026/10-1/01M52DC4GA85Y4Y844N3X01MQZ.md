Sprint 72, MCP - Access Control (ADR in COM-1012). Built on COM-1014 and COM-1015 (it names what is recertified through their lookups).

## What people can ask of it

- **Read (for a named company):**
  - **Schedules:** what is recertified (a business role, group, technology asset, data asset, directory role or shared mailbox), how often, by which owners, how many sign-offs it needs, and the owners' instructions.
  - **Instances:** each run's progress, meaning which owners have signed off and how many rows are decided, and the evidence it produced.
- **Write (whatever the person's role allows on screen):**
  - add, edit or delete a schedule;
  - **start one now**, which emails each owner their assignment;
  - **approve the removals** owners asked for, which takes the access away in the directory;
  - **confirm a removal** that had to be done by hand.
- **Not here:** owners sign off their own reviews in the Compass Portal, which stays out of scope. The old campaign-style recertification, reached only by an old link, gets no tools (ADR, COM-1012).
- **Example:** "Which recertifications are overdue, and who hasn't signed off? Approve the removals on the Finance Analyst review."

## Notes (technical)

- Wraps the schedules routes in `access_recert.py`: list, owner-defaults, POST, PATCH, DELETE and trigger. Also wraps instances: list, get, evidence, approve-removals, and `items/{id}/confirm-removed`.
- Permissions: `access.view` to read. To write, `access.run_recertification` **or** `inventory.run_recertification`, the same pairing the routes use. A person with only the Inventory grant still gets the write tools. Say so in the description.
- Not wrapped: `recert-settings`, `recert-scopes`, `recert-campaigns`, `recert-items`, and the portal routes.
- `approve_removals` is `destructive`, because it runs `execute_recert_instance_removal`. Delete-schedule is `destructive` too. Trigger sends mail, so its description says so.
- The schedule's subject is resolved through the existing lookups: `find_business_role` (COM-1015), `find_group` and `find_mailbox` (COM-1013), and `find_directory_role` (COM-1014). Technology and data assets are named by their register reference.
- New module `tools/access_recert.py`.
- Tests: writes are hidden without either recert grant; approving removals is refused where the screen refuses it; a schedule change is recorded via assistant.

**Done when:** on staging, the assistant creates a schedule for a business role, starts it now, and the owner gets the email and the review in the Portal.
