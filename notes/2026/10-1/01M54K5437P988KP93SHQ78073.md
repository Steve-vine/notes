Sprint 75, SAP - Schedule (ADR in COM-1041). Built on COM-1045, because an occurrence's life ends in done or cancelled.

## Why

A SOC 2 Type II auditor samples occurrences across the audit period. "Show me Q1, Q2 and Q3's access review" needs three separate records, each with its own evidence.

## What people see

- **Repeats** on Add entry and on Edit: Doesn't repeat (the default), Monthly, Quarterly, Every 6 months, Yearly. These are the same words recertification schedules use.
- **A repeating entry is laid out 12 months ahead.** Picking Quarterly for an entry 1–14 January creates four occurrences: January, April, July and October. Each keeps the same length and offset. A nightly job keeps 12 months laid out as time moves on.
- **Each occurrence is its own entry**, with its own status, owner, evidence, comments, completion and cancellation. It's labelled "Quarterly · 2 of 4 this year" and links to its series.
- **Changing one occurrence** (moving its dates, changing its owner, cancelling it) touches only that one.
- **Changing the series** asks "this one only, or this and every later one that hasn't started?". It never changes an occurrence that is In progress, Done or Cancelled.
- **Stop repeating** ends the series. Later Planned occurrences are removed after confirmation; the rest stay.
- **Changing the repeat** (for example Quarterly to Yearly) re-lays later Planned occurrences, after confirmation.

## Rules

- Deleting the series deletes only Planned occurrences. Done and cancelled ones stay (Steve, B).
- Links to controls (COM-1049) belong to the series, so every occurrence carries them. There's one link, not one per occurrence.

## Notes (technical)

- A `sap_series` table (cadence, template fields); entries carry `series_id` + `occurrence_index`. Reuse `RecertCadence` / `CADENCE_MONTHS` words and month arithmetic (clamp end-of-month).
- The nightly lay-out job must be idempotent (Celery rules: IDs not objects; unique `(series_id, occurrence_index)`), and must respect wall-clock time (`report_cadence.py`'s reasoning; Beat crontabs are wall-clock).
- Watch the staging Beat memory pin (memory: staging overlay pins resources) if a new Beat entry is added.

**Done when:** a repeating entry lays out a year of occurrences, keeps them laid out nightly, and series edits, single-occurrence edits, stop-repeating and changes of cadence all behave as above.
