First-pass framing, 20 Oct 2026. To be worked properly in Sprint 3.

**1. Stay at Moneypenny, grow the role.** Lowest risk; known pension and benefits. Ceiling: Head-of-level pay at a mid-size firm. Question: is there a CISO/CTO-level step available internally, and is the 4% employer contribution negotiable?

**2. CISO at a larger or regulated employer.** Base £120k–£185k mid-market (2026 guides), bonus on top, usually better employer pension (6–10%). Highest impact on pot if the extra is saved. Cost: more pressure in the years Steve wants to wind down; a late-50s move needs a 3–5 year horizon to pay back.

**3. Interim / contract CISO.** £1,000–£2,000/day. 120 days/yr ≈ £150k gross; via a limited company this allows large employer pension contributions and controls income timing. Fits the 60–65 transition exactly. Needs a network and a track record built *before* leaving the permanent role. Market is lumpy; IR35 determines take-home.

**4. Own consultancy / vCISO for SMEs.** Retainers £3k–£15k/month per client. Scales down gracefully toward retirement, keeps Steve's SOC2/ISO/compliance and infrastructure strengths relevant. Slow build (12–24 months to a stable book); best started as a side line while employed if contract permits.

**Working hypothesis to test:** one more step up (2 or 1+) from 56–60 to lift the base and pension, then 3 or 4 from 60–65 as the glide path. Sense-check: does Steve actually *want* a bigger job for five years, or is the better trade a cheaper life and an earlier wind-down?
