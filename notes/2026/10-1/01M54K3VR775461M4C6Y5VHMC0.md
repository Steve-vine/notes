Sprint 75, SAP - Schedule (ADR in COM-1041). Built on COM-1042.

## What people see

- A **Comments** section on an entry's page that looks and works like the one on a gap: who said what and when, newest at the bottom, with an add box.
- **Anyone who can see the entry can comment:** people with `sap.view`, and its owner (from the portal in COM-1054).
- **The author edits and deletes their own comment.** An administrator can do so on any comment. Nobody else sees the buttons, and the API refuses them the same way.
- **Comments stay open on a done or cancelled entry.** They are how a correction is explained after the work is frozen (COM-1045).

## Rules

- As on gaps, comments are correspondence and not in Activity (COM-627's reasoning). What's said about the entry is kept on the entry.

## Notes (technical)

- Mirror `gap_comment` / `GapComments.tsx` (COM-627): a `sap_entry_comments` table. Reuse the component if it can be made generic without contortion; otherwise copy its shape.
- The comment count shows on the Schedule list.
