Sprint 78, Attestations (ADR in COM-1092). The first build task. Every later task builds on it.

## What people see

**Access Control ▸ Roles ▸ a role** gains a **Content** tab. Anyone who can see the role can see it; only people with `attestations.manage` can change it.

- A table of the content assigned to this role, with columns **Content** (title and type, linked to the content page), **Assign date**, **Deadline** ("14 days"), **Cadence**, **Next opens** and **Status** (Scheduled, Running, Stopped).
- **Add content** opens a window:
  - **Content**: a picker of published content (policies, standards, procedures), searchable by title, with content already on the role left out;
  - **Assign date**: defaults to today. Today means it opens straight away, and the window says the holders will be emailed today;
  - **Deadline**: days to complete, 1–365, default 14;
  - **Cadence**: One-off, Monthly, Quarterly, Every 6 months, Annually. Default Annually.
- A row's menu offers:
  - **Edit**: the deadline and cadence can change, and they apply from the next cycle. The assign date can change only before the first cycle opens;
  - **Stop**: no new cycles open. An open cycle runs to its deadline, the row stays as Stopped and its history is kept. A stopped assignment can be **Restarted**, which picks a new assign date.
- A role with nothing assigned shows a one-line empty state that says what assigning content does.

## Rules

- The same content can be assigned to many roles. It can be on one role only once.
- Only published content can be added. Retiring or deleting content stops its assignments automatically, and the row says why.
- A disabled role opens no new cycles.
- Every change goes in the activity log against the role.

## Notes (technical)

- New permission identifiers `attestations.view` and `attestations.manage` in the catalogue (`brief/permission-catalogue.md`, a new "Attestations" table), built-in Admin only.
- New company-scoped table for the assignment: role, content item, assign date, deadline days, cadence enum, status, and stopped reason. Audited.
- Use `SortableTh` (or a no-sort comment) on the table, and `w="fit-content"` on any Switch, Checkbox or Radio (screen-conventions test).

**Done when:** an admin can add, edit, stop and restart content on a role; others can see it but not change it.
