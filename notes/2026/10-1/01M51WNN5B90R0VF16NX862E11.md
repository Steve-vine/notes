Found in Steve's smoke test of sprint 71 (COM-990), 2026-10-16. Minor.

## What is wrong

The live preview in Content ▸ House styles uses a sample document, the Access Control Policy v3. It fills in the title, type, version, owners and Approved by. It leaves the **Effective date** and **Next review date** empty, so any part of the cover, header or footer that shows them appears blank in the preview. A designer can't see how those dates will look.

## What it should do

The sample shows both dates, as a real published document would:
- **Effective:** a fixed, plausible date, the day the sample's version 3 was published.
- **Next review:** a year after that.

**Confirmed with Steve (2026-10-16), and staying as it is:** a real document's Effective date is the day the version shown was published. A review without a new publish doesn't move it.

## Technical

- `core/render/sample.py`: `SAMPLE_INFO` gives `DocumentInfo` no `effective` or `next_review`. Set both to fixed dates, not `now()`, so the preview cache and tests stay stable.
- Check the cover and the header/footer fields (`effective_date`, the next-review field in `core/render/style.py`) both show in the preview. Check the Word path (`core/render/word.py`) too, if the sample feeds it.
- Test: the sample's rendered HTML contains both formatted dates.
