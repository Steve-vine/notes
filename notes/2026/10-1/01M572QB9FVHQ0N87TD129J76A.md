Sprint 76, Incidents - Register (ADR in COM-1063). Builds on COM-1066. It answers SOC 2 CC7.3, ISO A.5.25, HIPAA 164.308(a)(6)(i), and IMA.10–12.

## What people see

A **Reported** incident shows a **Triage** panel to handlers. Beside each category it shows that category's "what counts" definition, so the decision is made against the defined criteria. The handler picks one of:

- **It's an incident.** Confirm its categories and severity, set the owner, and say whether it is **Confirmed** or **Suspected** (security incidents count when reasonably suspected). It moves to **Investigating**.
- **Not an incident.** A **reason** is required, e.g. "phishing email reported, not clicked, blocked by the filter". It is closed with that outcome.
- **Duplicate of INC-NN.** Pick the incident. It is closed and links both ways.

The panel also records **when it was triaged and by whom**, and the incident shows **time to triage** (from reported).

- Not an incident and Duplicate outcomes **stay in the register**, filtered out by default, with a "Show triaged out" switch.
- **Suspected → Confirmed** (or "ruled out", which ends it as Not an incident with a reason) can be changed later. Each change is recorded.
- Severity and categories can change after triage. Each change is recorded with a reason ("upgraded to P1: customer-facing").

## Rules

- Only handlers triage. Triage decisions are final apart from the suspected/confirmed change above. A wrong "Not an incident" is fixed by **reopening** (closure task).
- Every decision is in Activity and, from the timeline task, on the incident's timeline.

**Done when:** triage records all three outcomes with their reasons; triaged-out incidents are kept and filterable; severity and category changes carry a reason.
