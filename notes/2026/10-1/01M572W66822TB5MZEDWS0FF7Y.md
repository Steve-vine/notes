Sprint 76, Incidents - Register (ADR in COM-1063). Builds on closure (COM-1075). It answers the SOC 2 auditor's population request, HIPAA 164.408(c) and UK GDPR Art 33(5) ("document any breach").

## What people see

On the register, **Export** (for `incidents.view`):
- **The register for a period** as **CSV** and **PDF**. It includes every incident reported in the period, triaged-out ones too, marked. Each row has:
  - reference and title;
  - categories and severity;
  - status;
  - occurred, aware, contained, recovered and closed times;
  - the notifications made (sent or not required, on time or late);
  - whether a review was done;
  - the closing summary.
  An auditor samples from this list. Restricted incidents appear redacted (COM-1076).
- **One incident as a full report (PDF)**: everything on the incident page in order. That is details, each category's section, the timeline, notifications with their facts, the evidence list, the review with its corrective actions, and the closure. It's what you hand an auditor who picked INC-41 from the sample, or a regulator who asks.
- **HIPAA yearly breach log**: for a calendar year, every Privacy incident with protected health information where fewer than 500 people were affected. The columns are the ones HHS's portal asks for. Underneath it shows the yearly obligation (due by 1 March, COM-1065), so whoever submits can then mark it sent.

## Rules

- Exports are in Activity (who exported what, for which period).
- The PDF uses the house style for documents (ADR 0110), as SAP's export does.

## Notes (technical)

- Follow SAP's export (`SapExport`): a background job, with the file handed back when it's ready.

**Done when:** the register exports for any period in both formats; a single incident exports in full; the HIPAA yearly log lists the right incidents.
