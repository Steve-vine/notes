Part of the POC Tweaks sprint. Last of four: needs the three boxes (COM-940), the Role mappings card (COM-941) and is built on the titles task (COM-942), which already carries each box's settings to the forms.

## What people see

- **A box that has a filter only lists the roles whose names match it**, on the new-joiner form, the mover form and the approver's edit of either. Typing in the box searches within those. A box with no filter lists every role, as now.
- **Each box is filtered on its own.** With "^Dept" on Primary role and "^Team" on Additional role 1, the first offers only departments, the second only teams, and the other two offer everything.
- **A role is still only in one box**, and the primary role is still not offered as an additional one.
- **A filter never takes away a role that is already there.** A mover opens on what the person holds today, and an approver on what was asked for; a role among them that matches no filter stays in its box and is kept unless somebody removes it. The filter narrows what can be newly picked.
- **Where roles land when a form opens with additional roles already on it:** each goes to the first Additional roles box whose filter accepts it, and to the first box if none does. This replaces "all in the first box" from COM-940, so a team opens in the Team box.
- **A box whose filter matches no role says "No roles match"** and is not just empty. If that box is Primary role, a joiner cannot be raised until the filter is put right; the match count on the Role mappings card is what warns of this beforehand.
- **The filter shapes the form; it is not a rule about who may hold what.** A request that names a role outside a box's filter is still valid, which is what lets a mover keep a role they already hold. *(My call.)*

## Notes (technical)

- **The server does the matching.** For each box with a filter, the list in `JoinerFormOut.role_fields` (from COM-942) carries the ids of the roles it offers (`offered_role_ids`; `null` for "all"), matched with COM-941's one function against the business role's name. The front end intersects that with `roleOptions`. No `RegExp` in the browser: it fails the sast gate, and it would give the form a second engine that can disagree with the admin card.
- **Nothing is enforced on raise or approve.** `access_requests.py` validation is unchanged.
- **`JoinerRoleFields.tsx`:** per-box `data` is (offered ∩ roles) minus the primary and minus what the other boxes hold, plus whatever that box already holds. The routing of an existing list into boxes is a pure function in `joinerRows.ts` (first accepting box, else the first), used by the mover's opening state, the mover's **Keep**, and `SubjectFieldsEditor`.
- **Roles change after a filter is written.** A renamed or new role is matched when the form is loaded, not when the filter was saved, so there is nothing to recompute.
- **Tests.** Backend: offered ids per box, case and anchors, no filter is `null`, company scoping. Front end: a filtered dropdown's contents, a held role outside the filter is kept and shown, routing on open, the empty-box message, and that a primary pick still leaves the additional boxes.

**Done when:** on staging, with a filter on Primary role and another on Additional role 1, the joiner form offers only matching roles in those two boxes and every role in the other two, and a mover for someone holding a role outside both filters opens with that role still shown and keeps it when raised.
