Sprint 81, Trust Centre Portal. Scoped with Steve on 2026-10-19/20. This record is the first task, and the rest wait for it.

## What Trust Centre is

Two things, modelled on Vanta's Trust Center:

- A new module, **Trust Centre**, in the Modules section of the menu, per company. Admins set up what the company shares and who can see it.
- A **portal** for outside people: customers, prospects, auditors, partners. They read and download the company's policies, certificates and test results without asking anyone to email them over.

What the module holds:

- **Settings**: logo, title, intro text, contact email, who emails appear to come from, NDA wording, a portal on/off switch, and the company's public page.
- **Documents**: library content (always the current published version, as a PDF in the company's house style) or uploaded evidence (certificates, pen test summaries, SOC 2 reports) with a valid-until date. Each document is marked **everyone invited** or **restricted**.
- **Visitors**: people invited by email. Compass sends a link that lasts 30 days by default, and admins can resend, extend or revoke it. Restricted documents are opened up per visitor.
- **Requests**: people who asked for access on the public page, or whose link expired. Each waits for an admin to approve or decline it.
- **Content**: a security overview (written sections), FAQs, subprocessors (from Vendor Management) and updates (notices, optionally emailed to visitors).
- **Activity**: who visited, and what they opened and downloaded.

What a visitor sees: the overview with certification badges, documents, the security overview, subprocessors, FAQs and updates. Restricted documents appear only once they're opened up for that visitor and the NDA is accepted. Every download is watermarked with their name, email and the date.

## What Steve decided (2026-10-19)

- **Every suggested feature is in**: restricted tier, NDA click-through, watermarked downloads, badges, activity, security overview, subprocessors, FAQs, updates, "my link expired", assistant tools.
- **A public page where someone can ask for access**, as well as invitations. Nothing is shown to them until an admin approves.
- **Sign-in is the link plus a 6-digit code** emailed to the invited address. A forwarded link is useless without that inbox.
- **Links last 30 days by default.** Admins can change it per visitor.

## What the record has to settle (Claude's calls, not confirmed by Steve)

- **One trust centre per company**, chosen with the company picker like Incidents. One portal address serves every company. An invite link decides which company's trust centre the visitor sees. Each company's public page is at its own short name (e.g. `/acme`), chosen in Settings, and exists only while the company has switched requests on.
- **The public page shows only** the logo, title, intro and the request form. Badges, documents and everything else need sign-in.
- **A requester confirms their email with a code first**, so unconfirmed junk never reaches admins. Confirmed requests go to the Actions queue of everyone who can manage visitors for that company, plus an email.
- **Every request is approved by a person.** Nothing is approved automatically by email domain. An approved requester gets the same invite as anyone else (30 days, restricted documents chosen at approval). A declined requester gets a polite email with no reason.
- **"My link expired"** shows only on an expired link. A revoked or unknown link gets the same "this link isn't valid" page as any bad link, which names no company. A new link is only ever sent to the original email address.
- **The code** lasts 10 minutes and allows 5 tries, then a new one must be sent. A successful sign-in lasts 12 hours on that browser, or until the visitor's access ends, whichever comes first.
- **Uploads are PDFs only**, so every download can be watermarked. Library content is rendered to PDF.
- **Expired evidence disappears from the portal** on its valid-until date. Admins get a to-do 30 days and 7 days before. A badge comes from an unexpired certificate, and drops off with it.
- **The NDA** is accepted once per visitor per wording. Changing the wording asks again. With no NDA wording set, restricted documents need only the per-visitor grant.
- **The security overview is written text**, not live control status from assessments, so the portal never reveals an open gap.
- **Subprocessors** are vendors ticked in Trust Centre, each with its purpose and location. Vendors that receive data in Inventory are suggested first.
- **Update emails** carry a "stop these emails" link. They go only to visitors whose access is current.
- **Activity is kept as an audit record** (sign-ins, views, downloads, NDA acceptances, requests). Nothing is purged this sprint.
- **Permissions:**
  - `trust_centre.view`: see the module, visitors, requests and activity;
  - `trust_centre.manage_visitors`: invite, extend, revoke, open up restricted documents, approve and decline requests;
  - `trust_centre.manage`: Settings, documents and content.

  Built-in Admin only; starter roles stay frozen. Visitors manage is split out so a sales lead can invite without being able to change what's published.
- **The assistant** can do what the person can, except upload files.

**Out of scope:** a custom domain per company (one portal address for now); answering security questionnaires from trust centre content; CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot) integration; approving requests automatically by domain; e-signature beyond a recorded click-through; live control status.
