Sprint 76, Incidents - Register (ADR in COM-1063). Builds on the obligations rubric (COM-1065) and the breach assessment (COM-1070). It answers HIPAA 164.404–410, UK GDPR Art 33/34, ISO 42001 A.8.3/A.8.4, IMA.8, IMA.19, VEM.13 and INS.18.

## What people see

A **Notifications** card on every incident whose categories trigger an obligation. One row per obligation shows:

- the obligation, who is told, and its **deadline** worked out from the incident's "aware" time, or from the breach's confirmation;
- a **countdown**: "41 hours left", turning amber inside 24 hours and red when overdue;
- its state: **To decide**, **Sent**, **Not required** or **Sent late**.

**Record as sent** asks for:
- when it was sent;
- how (email, portal, letter, phone, regulator's online form);
- to whom;
- a reference (e.g. the ICO case number);
- what was said, or a copy attached.

**Not required** needs a reason. The handler is shown the obligation's "when it applies" words, and the breach assessment's conclusion where there is one. For example: "UK GDPR risk assessed as unlikely; see assessment 14 Oct".

**Sent after the deadline** also asks **why it was late**. UK GDPR requires the reasons for delay.

Handlers can **add an ad-hoc notification** that no rubric obligation covers (insurer, police, a particular customer), with its own deadline.

Notifications can be **staged**: "initial report sent, follow-up due". A sent notification can have follow-ups added under it.

## Rules

- **Obligations are triggered when a category is added.** A category removed later leaves any obligation already decided in place, marked as no longer triggered. Undecided ones drop off.
- Deadlines keep the obligation's deadline as it was when triggered (COM-1065).
- Every decision goes on the timeline.

**Done when:** triggered obligations appear with live deadlines; each can be sent, not required or sent late with the facts above; ad-hoc and follow-up notifications work.
