Sprint 73, MCP - Vendor Management (ADR in COM-1033). Built on COM-1035 (an amendment names an engagement through its lookup).

Approvals are the same as on screen. This is the assistant's version of the Requests tab and its review window.

## What people can ask of it

- **Read (for a named company):**
  - the **Requests** queue, filtered by kind and status, and **what's awaiting my approval**;
  - **a request in full**, as the review window shows it:
    - what's asked for: scope, data, residency, access, sub-processors, annual cost;
    - for an amendment, what would change;
    - the answers given;
    - each approval area and owner approval, and who has decided;
    - the conversation.
- **Write (whatever the person's role allows on screen):**
  - **raise a request** for a new vendor, a new engagement with an existing vendor, or an amendment to an engagement. Compass works out which approvals it needs, as on screen. A higher annual cost or more sensitive data brings in more approvers.
  - **approve, reject, or ask for more information** (that needs a comment). As on screen:
    - you can't decide your own request;
    - you decide only for the areas you're an approver for, or as the vendor's owner;
    - a decision is final.
  - **respond** when more information was asked of your request, or **edit** it. That reopens it and tells the approvers.
- **Example:** "What vendor requests are waiting for me? Summarise the Acme amendment and what changed." Then: "approve it for Security, and ask Finance's question about the cost."

## Notes (technical)

- Wraps `vendor_onboarding.py`: POST, list (`mine_to_approve`), get, `approvals/{aid}/decide`, respond and edit. Also wraps `vendor_approvals.py` list (with `can_decide` per row).
- **Decide's gate is the route, not a permission** (COM-1033): `decide_from_body` checks area membership, ownership and self-approval. Offer the tool to anyone with `vendors.view`, and pass the route's refusal back readably. Use `can_decide` to tell the assistant which approvals are the person's own.
- **Raise is one tool per kind** (`vendors_request_new_vendor`, `vendors_request_engagement`, `vendors_request_amendment`), each with the fields the form asks for.
- Decide is `destructive`: it's final, and approving may make a vendor active. Raise is a write.
- New module `tools/vendors_requests.py`.
- Tests:
  - the requester is refused when deciding their own request;
  - a non-member of the area is refused;
  - info-requested without a comment is refused;
  - a raised request lists the approvals it needs;
  - a decision is recorded via assistant.

**Done when:** on staging, someone else raises a request, and Steve's assistant approves his area of it. The request shows the approval via assistant, and the requester is notified as usual.
