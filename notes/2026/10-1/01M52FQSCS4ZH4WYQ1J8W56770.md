Sprint 74, MCP - Inventory (ADR in COM-1023). Built on COM-1024 (it names technology assets through COM-1024's lookup).

## What people can ask of it

- **Read (for a named company):**
  - **the register**, filtered as on the tab: controlled data and status.
  - **a data asset in full**, as its page shows it:
    - its record and owners;
    - its classification: the data types and entities it holds, and the sensitivity that follows from them;
    - the GDPR (Article 30) fields;
    - the technology assets that hold it;
    - when it was last confirmed accurate, and when it's next due for review.
  - **the vocabularies the forms offer:** data types, data entities, sensitivity levels and criticality levels.
- **Write (whatever the person's role allows on screen):**
  - add a data asset;
  - edit one, including its classification, its GDPR fields and which technology assets hold it;
  - mark it live, in cold storage, or deprecated;
  - decommission it;
  - delete it (refused if cited, as on screen);
  - **confirm it accurate.**
- **Example:** "Which data assets hold special-category personal data but have no lawful basis recorded?" Then: "add 'Payroll records' held in AST-3, owned by me, with employee personal data."

## Notes (technical)

- Wraps `data_assets.py`: list, get, POST, PATCH, DELETE and confirm-accurate. Also wraps the read vocabularies: `/data-types`, `/data-entities`, `/data-sensitivity-levels` and `/criticality-levels`.
- Vocabulary values resolve by name (exact, otherwise refused with candidates), the way `resolve_framework` works.
- Delete and decommission are `destructive`.
- New module `tools/inventory_data.py`, reusing `find_asset`.
- Tests: writes are hidden without manage-register; an unknown data type is refused with candidates; an edit is recorded via assistant.

**Done when:** on staging, the assistant adds a data asset held in a named technology asset, and both pages show the link.
