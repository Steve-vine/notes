Point-in-time reference, 20 Oct 2026, tax year 2026/27. Re-check every April and after each Budget.

- **New State Pension:** £241.30/week = £12,547.60/yr, needs 35 qualifying NI years (more if contracted out). GOV.UK, gov.uk/new-state-pension/what-youll-get.
- **State Pension age:** 67 for those born 6 Apr 1960 – 5 Apr 1977 (Steve and wife). The independent review due in 2027 could bring 68 forward to this cohort; some reports say Treasury policy is already 2037–39. Plan for 67, stress-test 68.
- **Normal minimum pension age:** rises 55 → 57 on 6 April 2028 (Finance Act 2022). Affects those born after 5 April 1971.
- **Pensions in IHT:** unused pension funds count in the estate for deaths from 6 April 2027 (Finance Act 2026). Spouse exempt. Matters for what passes to the son; review death-benefit nominations.
- **Salary sacrifice:** from April 2029 only the first £2,000/yr of sacrificed pension contributions is NIC-exempt (Autumn Budget, 26 Nov 2025). Small effect at Steve's level (2% NI above UEL) but worth using before then.
- **Pension annual allowance:** £60,000 (tapers above £260k adjusted income). Carry-forward of unused allowance from the 3 previous tax years.
- **Tax-free lump sum:** 25%, capped at £268,275 (lump sum allowance).
- **ISA allowance:** £20,000 per adult. Cash ISA sub-limit reportedly £12,000 for under-65s from April 2027.
- **Income tax 2026/27 (England):** PA £12,570; 20% to £50,270; 40% to £125,140; 45% above. PA tapers £1 per £2 over £100,000 (60% effective band). Thresholds frozen to April 2031.
- **Employee NI:** 8% £12,570–£50,270; 2% above.
- **Retirement Living Standards (Pensions UK, 2025, two-person, after tax, home owned):** Minimum £22,500; Moderate £45,400; Comfortable £62,700.
- **CISO market (recruiter guides, 2026, indicative):** mid-market base £120k–£185k plus 20–35% bonus; interim/vCISO £1,000–£2,000/day; vCISO retainers £3k–£15k/month.
