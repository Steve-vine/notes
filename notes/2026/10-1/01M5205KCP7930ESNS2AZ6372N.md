Found in Steve's smoke test of sprint 71 (COM-989 / COM-990), 2026-10-16.

## What is wrong

A templated document's **Read** tab and its **Edit** tab's sections are shown in the house style's real colours, so the screen matches the PDF. Those colours were chosen for white paper:
- dark body text;
- a dark primary for headings;
- pale callout and table shading.

In dark mode they sit on Compass's dark background, and almost none of the text is readable.

## What people see

**1. In dark mode, the document is readable (Read and Edit).**
- Each colour of the style is turned into its dark-background counterpart. It keeps its hue, so a navy heading becomes a light blue heading and a red warning stays red, but it gets enough contrast to read:
  - **body text:** near-white, rather than the style's dark grey;
  - **headings, links, and text set in the primary or accent colour:** the same hue, lightened until it reads clearly on the dark background (at least 4.5:1);
  - **callout, table-header and banded-row shading:** the same hue, as a deep tint, with light text on it;
  - **borders, rules and code blocks:** dark-mode greys, not the paper greys.
- Fonts, sizes and spacing don't change; only colour does.
- **Pictures, drawings and diagrams** are left as they are. Each sits on a light card, so a drawing with a white background doesn't glow and one with dark lines doesn't vanish.
- Light mode is exactly as now.

**2. On the Edit tab: a "Show as on paper" switch.**
- It sits at the top of the sections.
- **On:** every section is shown on a white page, in the style's real colours, exactly as it prints, whichever mode Compass is in.
- **Off (the default):** the sections follow Compass's mode, readable in dark as above.
- It's remembered per browser, like the Rich text | Markdown choice.
- In light mode, switching it on also gives the sections a page-like white sheet with a soft edge.

**My call:** the switch is on the Edit tab only, as asked. The Read tab follows the mode, and Print preview is still the exact paper copy.

## Technical

- **Colours come from one place:** `core/render/css.py` `_variables()` / `_shared_rules()`, served to the screen by `screen_css()` (`/house-styles/{slug}/stylesheet.css`). The PDF must not change.
  - Move the hard-coded paper colours in `_shared_rules` into variables (`--rt-rule`, `--rt-code-bg`, `--rt-warning-edge`, `--rt-important-edge`) with today's values, so dark mode can override them. The PDF output stays byte-identical; test that.
- **In `screen_css()` only,** add a dark block:
  - `[data-mantine-color-scheme="dark"] .rt-doc:not(.rt-paper) { --rt-colour-text: …; --rt-colour-primary: …; … }`
  - Work the values out in Python from the palette:
    - foreground colours: convert to HSL, keep hue and most of the saturation, and raise lightness until WCAG contrast against Compass's dark body colour is ≥ 4.5:1 (text) or ≥ 3:1 (rules);
    - shades: same hue, lightness ~18–24%.
  - Put the helper beside `tint()` and unit-test it with the built-in palette and a deliberately pale palette.
  - Compass's dark surface colour: read it from `theme.ts` / the cssVariablesResolver (the Nocturne palette, ADR 0085). Don't guess.
- **Pictures** in dark mode: `.rt-doc .rt-picture img { background: #fff; padding; border-radius }` under the dark selector only.
- **Paper switch:** `pages/ContentDetailPage.tsx` EditTab.
  - A Mantine Switch (`w="fit-content"`; a Switch with a description needs `aria-label`);
  - persisted in localStorage in try/catch, beside `preferredView`/`rememberView` in `richtext/convert.ts`;
  - toggles a `rt-paper` class on each section's `.rt-doc` wrapper (RichSectionEditor and the Markdown preview, if it has one);
  - `.rt-paper` sets `background: #fff` and forces the light variables (the `:not(.rt-paper)` selector already leaves them in place).
  - Check that the Tiptap toolbar and the section card chrome still follow the app's mode; only the content area turns to paper.
- **Tests:**
  - Python: dark variables exist in `screen_css` for each colour and meet the contrast ratio; `document_css` is unchanged.
  - Vitest: the switch adds `rt-paper` and is remembered.
  - Real browser (the Playwright harness, `forceColorScheme="dark"`): a screenshot of a document in dark mode, and with Paper on, for the PR.
