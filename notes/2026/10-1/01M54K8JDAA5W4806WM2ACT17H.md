Sprint 75, SAP - Schedule (ADR in COM-1041; Steve, E). Built on COM-1050.

## What people see

- A **Coverage** tab in SAP. Pick a framework (only the company's adopted ones; HITRUST and CIS at the level the company is working towards).
- The framework's requirements in the standard's order, under their section headings. Each row shows:
  - **Planned activity:** the plan entries that reach it through their controls ("Security awareness training · Yearly · next due Mar 2027 · via SEA.1"), linking to each;
  - or **No planned activity.**
- Filters: All / Has activity / No activity, and a search box.
- Counts at the top: "23 requirements have planned activity · 91 have none · 6 not applicable (hidden)".
- **No percentage** (ADR, Claude's call). Most of a framework is technical controls that never need a scheduled activity, so a percentage would always look poor. The reader judges which gaps matter.

## Rules

- **Has activity** means a linked entry that isn't cancelled and is either done in the last 12 months or due in the next 12. An entry that was only ever cancelled, or is more than a year stale, doesn't count.
- Requirements the company marked **not applicable** are left out (Steve, earlier discussion).
- Reached through **any** mapping between the requirement and a linked control. The row shows which control, and how strongly it maps, so a weak link is visible.
- It reads only. Nothing changes compliance (Steve, F).
