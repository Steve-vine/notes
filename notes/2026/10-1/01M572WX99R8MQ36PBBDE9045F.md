Sprint 76, Incidents - Register (ADR in COM-1063). The last task; builds on all the others.

## What people see

**Search (⌘K and the assistant's `search`)**:
- finds incidents by reference (INC-41), title and closing summary, for people with `incidents.view`;
- restricted incidents are found only by those who can see them. Anyone else finds the reference alone, as "Restricted incident".

**The assistant** (ADR 0109) can, as the person and only with their permissions:
- **read**:
  - list and filter the register;
  - get an incident with its sections, timeline, notifications, evidence list, review and closure;
  - the Overview numbers;
  - the incident rubrics;
- **work**:
  - record an incident;
  - triage it;
  - change severity or categories with a reason;
  - move stages;
  - add a timeline entry or a correction;
  - fill the impact, breach and AI sections;
  - link and unlink assets, vendors, risks, controls, decisions and related incidents;
  - record notifications (sent, not required, late);
  - add held-elsewhere evidence;
  - write the review and raise its corrective actions;
  - close, correct and reopen;
- **report for someone**: when a person tells the assistant about an incident, it can report it for them as they would from the portal.

**Not through the assistant** (ADR 0109): uploading or downloading files, and exports. Restricting or unrestricting stays on screen too, so the decision to hide a case is always a person's click.

## Rules

- The assistant **says what will change before it records a notification as sent or not required, or closes an incident**. These are the facts an auditor relies on.
- Everything it does is in Activity as the person, via the assistant.
