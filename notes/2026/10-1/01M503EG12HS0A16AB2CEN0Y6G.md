Build out a single asset inventory covering the categories relevant to Callitech’s operations: workstations, laptops, and mobile devices across Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida; cloud-hosted infrastructure, identity systems, and communications platforms underpinning TAS, live chat, and AI-based answering; databases and application frameworks (MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Vue, React, CakePHP, Tailwind CSS); data assets such as employee and customer PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content; and third-party/vendor-managed assets (telecom, cloud providers). For each asset, record type, owner, location or hosting environment, and status (active, retired, disposed), and assign an owner responsible for keeping the record current as assets are acquired, redeployed, or decommissioned.

Using the asset inventory as a base, assign a sensitivity/criticality classification to each endpoint, cloud/identity/communications platform, database, application component, and data asset (including HIPAA-in-scope and PCI-relevant data). Document the corresponding security requirements applied, including device encryption and authentication for laptops and mobile devices used off-site, access controls and monitoring for cloud infrastructure and communications platforms supporting TAS and AI-based answering, physical security at Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida offices, and contractual/technical safeguards for telecom and cloud providers.

Asset owners confirm required controls are in place for assets under their ownership and flag any gap. Review classifications and applied controls at least semi-annually, and whenever a new device class, cloud service, or client contractual requirement (e.g., a new HIPAA or PCI client) is introduced.
