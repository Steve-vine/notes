Sprint 81, Trust Centre Portal (ADR in COM-1157). Builds on COM-1167 (the Content tab).

## What people see

**In the module, Content ▸ Updates:**

- **Post an update**: title, body (simple formatting), a kind (Security notice, Incident, Compliance, General), and **Email current visitors** (off by default).
- Before sending, the screen says how many people it will go to. Emails go from the company's sender with the company's logo: the title, the body and a link to the portal.
- Posted updates can be edited (the portal shows "edited") or hidden, but an email once sent is recorded as sent: when, and to how many.

**On the portal:** an **Updates** section, newest first, with date and kind. The overview shows the latest one at the top for 14 days.

**Stopping the emails:** every update email has a "stop these emails" link that works without signing in, for that visitor of that company. The visitor's row in the module shows "Update emails off".

Only visitors whose access is current (not expired, not revoked) are emailed.

## Notes (technical)

- `trust_centre_updates` (company, title, body, kind, shown, posted_by, emailed_at, emailed_count) and an opt-out flag on the visitor. Audited.
- Send as a Celery task taking the update id; idempotent (a visitor is emailed once per update even if the task retries). Unsubscribe link: a signed, single-purpose token, not the access link.

**Done when:** an Admin posts an update with email on, current visitors on staging receive it, an unsubscribed visitor doesn't, and it shows on the portal.
