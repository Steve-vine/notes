Sprint 75 fix-forward, found by Steve on staging.

## What was wrong

Coverage (and the Overview's coverage counts) listed only frameworks the company had "adopted", and a company counts as adopting one only once someone declares its scope statement or chooses a working-towards level on the framework's page. Nothing on screen says so. Test Company has neither on any framework, so Coverage said "This company has adopted no frameworks yet", while its Compliance page measures it against all nine.

The control picker on an entry had the same idea: framework requirements were labelled "· not adopted".

## Fix

- Coverage lists every framework the Compliance page measures the company against (every active, current framework), plus any older version the company has declared a scope for, since it may still be certified against it.
- The Overview's coverage counts follow the same list.
- The picker drops the "not adopted" label; requirements of frameworks the company has scoped still sort first.
- The empty message names the real case: no frameworks in the library.
- The assistant's tool descriptions drop "adopted".

**Done when:** Test Company's Coverage tab offers all nine frameworks with no scope declared, and the word "adopted" no longer appears in the plan.
