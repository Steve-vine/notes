Sprint 71, Improve templated content (ADR in COM-986). Builds on COM-988, COM-989 and COM-991. Steve chose Excalidraw for free-form diagrams on 2026-10-15.

## What people see

- **Insert ▸ Drawn diagram** opens Excalidraw over the page. It has shapes, arrows that stay attached, text, freehand drawing, and the shape library. Closing it puts the diagram into the section like an image, with a caption.
- **To change it,** double-click it to reopen the drawing, exactly as it was left.
- A diagram is part of the document: it is saved with the draft and kept in each published version.
- **Where it appears:**
  - the PDF: crisp at any zoom;
  - Word: as a picture;
  - plain text: as `[Diagram: caption]`.
- **Licence and privacy:** Excalidraw is free, under the MIT licence. It runs entirely inside Compass: nothing goes to excalidraw.com, and there's no live-collaboration server.

## My calls

- One person edits a diagram at a time, the same as the rest of the section. No live collaboration.
- Shapes can be added to the library from a `.excalidrawlib` file. Libraries can't be browsed from the excalidraw.com website (it would leave Compass).
