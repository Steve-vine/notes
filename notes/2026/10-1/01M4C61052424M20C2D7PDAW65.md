Found investigating ACR-56 (a leaver stuck on *Executing*, staging, 2026-10-07).

## What happened

The leaver started at 16:40, disabled Abigail Sky in AD, then never finished. It was still running when a deploy replaced the worker at 17:06. AD, Graph and Exchange all have time limits; Kerberos's conversation with the domain controller on port 88 has none. Tested: when a KDC accepts the connection and never answers (what a stall in the Twingate tunnel looks like), the Kerberos library in the image (MIT 1.20) waits indefinitely, more than 5 minutes in the test with no end.

## What should happen

If a domain controller doesn't answer Kerberos within about 15 seconds, the change **fails** with *"Can't reach a domain controller on port 88 (Kerberos)"* and offers a **Retry** (ADR 0083 §4: broke → Failed + Retry). Test connection and the health check say the same. Nothing waits forever.

## Notes (technical)

- MIT krb5 ≥ 1.22 has `request_timeout` in `[libdefaults]`. Tested on 1.22: it fails after the limit with KRB5_KDC_UNREACH, which already maps to the port-88 sentence. The image has 1.20.1 (bookworm), which ignores it. Set it anyway, for when the base moves.
- For 1.20, bound each blocking gssapi call that can reach a KDC: `acquire_cred_with_password`, and `SecurityContext.step` (the TGS-REQ for `ldap/<dc>`). Run it on a helper thread with a deadline; python-gssapi releases the GIL. On expiry, raise `KerberosFailure` (unreachable). The abandoned call finishes, or dies with its socket, on its own; log it.
- Test: a fake KDC that accepts TCP and never replies. `connect()` raises within the bound, with the port-88 message.

**Done when:** a silent KDC fails a Kerberos connection within the bound, in CI, merged to main.
