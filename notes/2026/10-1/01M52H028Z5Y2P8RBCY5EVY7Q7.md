Sprint 73, MCP - Vendor Management (ADR in COM-1033). Built on COM-1034.

## What people can ask of it

- **Read (for a named company):**
  - the **questionnaire library**: each form, whether it's active or retired, and its questions;
  - for each question: the prompt, help text, kind, options, whether it's required, and **"Only ask when…"**;
  - the **Assessment rules**: each rule set, the forms it requires, and the conditions that trigger it.
- **Write (whatever the person's role allows on screen; that is *Design questionnaires*):**
  - add, edit or retire a form;
  - add, edit, reorder or remove its questions, including when each is asked;
  - add, edit or delete a rule set, choose its forms, and add or remove its conditions.
- **Example:** "Draft a short AI-supplier questionnaire: what models they use, where prompts are stored, and whether customer data trains them. Ask the training question only if they say they process our data." Then: "add it to the rule set for engagements holding customer personal data."

## Notes (technical)

- Wraps `vendor_forms.py` and `vendor_form_questions.py` (CRUD), and `assessment_rule_sets.py` (CRUD, `PUT /{id}/forms`, and rules POST/DELETE). A rule's conditions are whatever the rule editor offers today. Describe them to the assistant from the route's schema, and don't invent new kinds.
- **Shared lookup:** `find_form(company, name_or_id)`, which COM-1039 reuses when adding a questionnaire to a vendor.
- "Test" on screen is a preview with no record. Leave it out: the assistant can read a form back instead.
- Question and rule-set delete are `destructive`. Retiring a form is a write.
- Changing a question doesn't change answers already given. Those are frozen with the wording at the time. The edit tool's description says so.
- New module `tools/vendors_forms.py`.
- Tests: writes are hidden without design-questionnaires; a condition referencing an unknown question is refused readably; an edit is recorded via assistant.

**Done when:** on staging, the assistant drafts a new form with a conditional question and adds it to a rule set, and both show on the Assessments and Assessment rules tabs.
