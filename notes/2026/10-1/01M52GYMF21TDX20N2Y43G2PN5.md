Sprint 73, MCP - Vendor Management (ADR in COM-1033). Built on COM-1034.

## What people can ask of it

- **Read:**
  - **engagements:** for each one,
    - its title, scope and status;
    - criticality and access rung;
    - estimated annual cost;
    - where the data is held, access requirements and sub-processors;
    - the data types and entities it involves;
    - any risk-tier override, with its reason.
  - the **assurance profile:** DPA, security clauses, audit rights, liability, breach-notification hours, BC/DR, regulatory scope, SLA, exit strategy, cyber insurance, financial standing, and the notes on external posture and reputation.
  - **certifications**, with issuer, valid until and evidence link, and which are expiring.
  - **contacts**, and which receive questionnaires.
- **Write (whatever the person's role allows on screen):**
  - add or edit an engagement, including overriding its risk tier with a reason;
  - edit the assurance profile;
  - add, edit or delete a certification, with its evidence link (a link, not an upload);
  - add, edit or delete a contact, and mark whether they receive questionnaires.
- **Example:** "Which vendors hold our customers' personal data without a DPA recorded?" Or: "Acme renewed ISO 27001 until next October. Here's the certificate link. Update it."

## Notes (technical)

- Wraps `vendors.py`: engagements GET, POST and PATCH; certifications CRUD; contacts CRUD. The assurance profile fields are on the vendor's PATCH; give them their own tool, `vendors_update_assurance`, so the main update stays readable.
- Data types, entities and sensitivity resolve by name from the shared vocabularies (as in sprint 74's COM-1025, if that lands first). Otherwise add the resolvers here.
- **Shared lookup:** `find_engagement(vendor, title_or_id)`, which COM-1037's amendments reuse.
- Contact and certification delete are `destructive`. Making someone a questionnaire contact means they'll get the next questionnaire link, so the description says so.
- New module `tools/vendors_engagements.py`.
- Tests:
  - writes are hidden without manage-register;
  - a risk-tier override without a reason is refused readably;
  - a change is recorded via assistant.

**Done when:** on staging, the assistant adds a certification with its link and expiry, and the vendor page shows it.
