Part of sprint 67, Inventory discovery - Software. Asked for by Steve on 2026-10-18. Builds on ADR 0099 §10 (findings, COM-888).

## Why

Findings are what discovery observes about the resources beneath a technology asset that its owner should know: not encrypted, reachable from the internet, backups off, a Windows Server release out of support, a server not seen for 90 days, a disabled account. Today they are only on each asset's own page, in its Findings card. To see them all, someone has to open every asset. Test Company has two open findings today, an account disabled and a server not seen, and nothing on the Inventory screen says so.

## What people see

- **A new Inventory tab, Findings,** between Discovered and Settings. Anyone who can see Inventory can see it.
- **One row per finding,** across every technology asset in the company:
  - the finding's sentence ("Database kora-… is reachable from the internet.")
  - the technology asset (a link to it) and the resource it's about, or "the asset itself"
  - where it came from (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Directory)
  - when it was opened
  - its state: **Open**, **Accepted** (with who, when, why and any decision), or **Risk raised** (with a link to the risk)
- **Open findings are shown by default.** A state filter switches to Accepted, Risk raised, Closed (the fact has since changed), or All. Further filters cover the kind of finding, the technology asset and the source, plus a search. Like other lists, the filters live in the address, so a view can be shared.
- **The tab title carries the open count** ("Findings · 2") so it's visible without opening the tab.
- **The same actions as on the asset, from the row:** **Accept** (reason, optionally a decision) and **Raise a risk**, for someone who may edit Inventory. Either one updates the asset's own card too: it's the same finding, not a copy.
- **Columns sort**, defaulting to newest opened first.
- **Nothing new is raised:** this lists the findings that already exist. Which checks raise findings is unchanged.
- **My call (overturnable): not shown in the Inventory portal.** An asset owner in the portal still sees their own asset's findings on its page.

## Notes (technical)

- `resource_findings` already holds everything per company (`company_id`, `container_id`, `discovered_resource_id`, `key`, `sentence`, opened/closed/accepted, `decision_id`, `risk_id`). Add `GET /inventory/findings?company=…&state=open|accepted|risk|closed|all&key=&container=&provider=&q=` with paging, plus a count for the tab title. Accept and raise-risk reuse the existing `/findings/{id}/accept` and `/findings/{id}/raise-risk` routes (they return the container; the tab invalidates its own list as well).
- Provider comes from the resource's `provider`. Asset-level findings (no resource) come from the asset's resources (`all_resources_gone`), so show "the asset itself".
- Check-key labels for the kind filter: one human label per key (`not_encrypted` → "Not encrypted", …), kept beside `checks.py` so a new check can't land without one. Add a unit test that every key has a label.
- Frontend: a new panel in `InventoryPage.tsx` (`value="findings"`), a `FindingsRegister` built like the other registers. Use SortableTh, `w="fit-content"` on any Switch/Checkbox (screen-conventions test), URL-backed filters (useQueryState + flushSync), and an Accept modal shared with the asset's `FindingsCard`. Read the *Screen conventions* section of `brief/information-architecture.md` first.
- Regenerate `schema.d.ts`; run the drift script. If an MCP tool for listing findings is wanted, add `inventory_list_findings` beside `inventory_accept_finding` (auto-discovered).
- Tests: the list across two assets and two providers; each state filter; accept from the tab shows on the asset; a closed finding appears only under Closed/All; another company's findings never appear; the tab count is open findings only.
