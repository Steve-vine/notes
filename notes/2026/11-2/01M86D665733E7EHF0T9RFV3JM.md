Sprint "Vendor Departments" (Steve, 24 Nov 2026). Follows COM-2131.

## Why
A company needs departments before anyone can add or request a vendor engagement for it (COM-2133), so setting up a company should include them.

## What people see
- Admin → Companies → **Create company** has a **Departments** section: type a name and add it, as many as needed, remove one before saving, and arrange the order.
- It's optional at this point. A company can be created with none, and the window says engagements can't be added for it until departments are set up.
- Duplicate names in the list are pointed out before saving.
- Once created, the company's Departments panel (COM-2131) shows exactly what was entered, in that order.
- **Edit details** on an existing company doesn't show this section. Its departments are edited in the panel, where retire and the in-use guard apply.

## Done when
- Creating a company with three departments shows all three on its page in the order entered, and each appears in the activity log.
- Creating a company with none still works, and its Departments panel is empty with a prompt to add some.
- A duplicate in the list is caught in the window, and the company isn't created half-way (company and departments save together or not at all).

## For the build
- `admin/CompaniesSection.tsx` `CompanyFormModal` (L342) is shared with CompanyPage's "Edit details", so show the section only in create mode.
- Either extend `POST /companies` (`api/v1/companies.py` `create_company` L96) with an optional `departments: [name]`, saved in the same transaction, or create the departments straight after. Prefer the single transaction.
- Probably **on the chain**: API change (no migration).
