Steve (20 Nov, after SAP-47/48 in production): a plan entry marked Done by mistake can't be reopened, and SAP-48 (next year's insurance renewal) now reads Done, so nothing will chase it in 2027. Several everyday registers have the same trap. Records that are evidence stay frozen as they are: approved risk assessments, DPIAs, BIAs, AI impact and PCI analyses, a sent DDQ, confirmed imports, completed recertifications and incident reviews, recorded notifications.

**What it does**
One **Reopen** that works the same everywhere it appears. The person gives a reason; the record goes back to being open; its history keeps the original close (who, when, the outcome) and the reopen (who, when, why), side by side, the way corrections are kept now. Nothing is deleted.

**Done when**
- **Plan entries**: a Done or Cancelled entry can be reopened by someone who manages the plan. It goes back to Planned (In progress if it had a start date that has passed and it was In progress before). Its outcome, completion date and any correction stay in the history; evidence, findings and comments stay attached. It stops counting as done on the Overview, the year's sign-off and coverage, and comes back on its owner's actions. Works for an entry in a repeating series without touching the other occurrences.
- **Systems and data assets**: a Decommissioned one can be reopened by someone who can manage the inventory register. It goes back to the status it had before. Its links, access entries and recertification history are as they were.
- **Vendor questionnaires**: a Withdrawn one can be reopened by someone who runs questionnaires; it is outstanding again and the vendor's link works again. A Submitted one stays frozen (it's the vendor's own answers); to reassess you send it again, as now.
- **Decision records**: a Declined decision can be reopened by someone who records decisions; it goes back to Proposed. Superseded is undone by changing the decision that superseded it, as now.
- Reopen asks for a reason (required) and says what will happen in one line, e.g. "SAP-48 goes back to Planned. Its outcome is kept in the history."
- The record's history and the activity log show "Reopened" with the reason.
- An archived company's records can't be reopened (it is frozen).
- Polaris can reopen each of these with a reason, and names the record back.
- An ADR records that these closes are reversible with a reason (amends ADR 0115 §5 "Done is final" for plan entries, and the decommissioned/withdrawn/declined rules).
- Tests cover each kind: reopen, the reason required, who may and may not, the history kept, and an archived company refused.
