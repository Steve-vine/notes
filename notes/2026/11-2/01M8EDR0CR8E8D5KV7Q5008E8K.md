**Independent** (wording only — no migration, no API shape change).

Raised by Steve on 27 Nov 2026 while working the Domain Admins recertification. Production's Actions read "Remove C Forster from 1 on-premises group in AD — flagged at review" (×4, ACR-36), so you have to open the review to learn the group is **Domain Admins**.

## What people see
- An on-premises to-do in **Actions** names what it changes:
  - "Remove C Forster from Domain Admins in AD — flagged at review"
  - "Add Sam Jones to Finance Users and Sales UK in AD"
  - "Let Ada Lovelace send on behalf of Accounts, in AD", as the send-on-behalf to-dos already do
- **More than three names:** the first three are named, then "and N more", e.g. "Add Sam Jones to Finance Users, Sales UK, Payroll and 2 more in AD". The full list stays on the request or review, one line per change.
- **Same wording everywhere:** the title is the same in the Actions list, the daily digest email, and the immediate email a leaver's to-do sends.
- **Leavers and account changes** keep their own wording ("Disable Sam Jones's account in AD", "Update Sam Jones's details in AD"). Only titles that count groups or lists change.

## Notes (technical)
- Titles are built in `core/actions/access.py`: `_count_noun`, `_manual_step_title` and the recertification one in `_recert_manual_steps`. Group names come from the mirror (`DirectoryGroup.display_name`); a group that has since gone from the mirror falls back to "1 on-premises group".
- Tests that pin the old titles need updating; check the digest email test too.

**Done when:** on staging, a to-do raised for a group outside the managed OUs reads with the group's name in Actions and in the digest email.
