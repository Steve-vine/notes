Sprint "Registers - Phase 2" (ADR 0167 §1, in COM-2087). Builds on the Registers sprint's links (COM-2079), and starts after COM-2084 so the phase-1 chain is on main.

## What people see
- On an entry's **Links**, **Link ▸ Entry in a register** picks a register (any, including this one) and then an entry by ID or title.
- Both entries list the link under the other register's name: "Obligations ▸ OBL-4 UK GDPR Art. 32", opening that entry. A link has no direction and no label. Unlink from either side.
- So an Interested parties entry lists the obligations linked to it, and each of those obligations lists the party.
- Company rules (ADR 0165 §4): two shared registers' entries link once, for every company. If either side is a per-company entry, the link belongs to that company and is seen only within it. Two per-company entries link only within the same company; the picker offers only those.
- Someone who can't open a Restricted register sees "a Restricted entry" in its place, with no ID or title, and can't unlink it.
- Adding and removing a link is in both entries' history, so the as-at view shows the links an entry had on that date.
- Who: anyone who can edit entries on this side can link to any entry they can see on the other.

## Implementation notes (technical)
- The entry-link table gains a target kind of register entry. One row per pair, the two entry IDs ordered (lower first), unique, so a pair can't be linked twice and both sides read it back.
- The company column follows the rule above; reject a pair of per-company entries from different companies.
- Link history: follow whatever COM-2079 does for other links (added/removed dates, or revision rows). Don't invent a second mechanism.
- Restricted filtering on read in both directions.

## Done when
- An entry links to an entry in another register and in its own; both show it and either side can unlink.
- A per-company entry can't be linked to another company's entry.
- A Restricted entry shows as "a Restricted entry" to someone who can't open it.
- The as-at view on a date before the link was made doesn't show it; on a later date it does.
