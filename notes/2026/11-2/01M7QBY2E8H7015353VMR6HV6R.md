Three small things from the Library pre-UAT pass on staging (c15afb4d, 2026-11-18).

1. **Reader headings in dark mode (COM-1922).** The reader's section headings are a fixed mid-grey (rgb 140,140,140 on rgb 35,37,50), dimmer than the body text (rgb 215,222,229). In light mode they're near-black. Headings should use the adaptive text colour, as the brief requires ("Text reads in both schemes"; no fixed shades for text). Screenshots: ~/compass-uat/library/COM-1922/06, 10.
2. **Contents lists itself (COM-1921/1922).** A templated document whose house style adds a "Contents" heading shows "1 Contents" as the first entry of the pane's Contents, the reader's Contents menu and the Markdown export's headings. The Library's own contents should leave that heading out, because it's the template's table of contents and not a section of the document.
3. **"Its 1 folder goes too" (COM-1916/1919/1923).** When the folder being deleted has exactly one folder inside, the question should read "The folder inside it goes too." (or "Its one folder goes too."). This applies on the Library page and in Polaris's delete question. "1 document stays in its other folders" and "2 will be Not in a folder" read fine.

**Done when**
- Reader headings match the body text's contrast in dark mode, checked in a browser.
- The template's own "Contents" heading is left out of the pane, the reader's Contents menu and the Markdown export. The numbering of the remaining sections is unchanged from what the house style prints.
- The one-folder wording reads naturally on the page and in Polaris, with tests.

Independent, apart from the one wording line shared with the Polaris tool.
