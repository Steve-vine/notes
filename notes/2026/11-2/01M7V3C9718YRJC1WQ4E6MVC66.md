Found in pre-UAT of round 3, by Tester C on staging 7ea67f64, against COM-1997 and COM-1998.

**Steps:** on a document's Edit tab, select a wide, short flowchart (e.g. 672×92) and drag its bottom-right corner handle.

**Seen:** a little downward movement outweighs a lot of sideways movement. Moving 25 px down grows it by about 360 px, so it jumps to Full width. The corner doesn't stay under the pointer.

**Done when**
- While dragging, the picture's corner follows the pointer as closely as its fixed proportions allow. The size comes from whichever of the sideways and downward movements best matches where the pointer is, never from a small movement multiplied by the picture's aspect ratio.
- A wide, short flowchart, a tall, narrow image and a square drawing can each be resized smoothly in small steps, both up and down, without jumping to Full width.
- Full width still happens when the picture is dragged to the column's edge (COM-2004's assumption).
- Tests cover a wide, short picture and a tall, narrow one being dragged, with the new width matching the pointer's position.

Independent: frontend only (richtext/pictureResize.ts).
