    Done — PR #2005, merged 36791399.
    Dragging a picture's corner handle now keeps the corner as close to the pointer as the picture's proportions allow. The movement across and the movement down each suggest a size, and the one that puts the corner nearer the pointer wins. Before, the bigger of the two always won, so a little movement down a wide, short picture counted as a big one.
    A wide, short flowchart now follows the pointer across, and 25 px down no longer throws it to Full width. A tall, narrow image follows the pointer down. A square drawing follows it either way. Each grows and shrinks in small 5% steps.
    Moving down no longer counts double on a centred picture.
    Full width still happens when the corner reaches the column's edge. A picture just short of the edge (say 672 px in a 680 px column) no longer rounds up to Full width at the first touch.
    Assumption: on a very wide picture, dragging straight down changes nothing; drag across or diagonally. On a tall, narrow one it's the other way round.
    To check on staging: on a document's Edit tab, drag the corner of a wide, short flowchart, a tall, narrow image and a square drawing a little at a time, bigger and smaller. The corner should stay near the pointer, and only reaching the column's edge should make it Full width.

Found in pre-UAT of round 3, by Tester C on staging 7ea67f64, against COM-1997 and COM-1998.

**Steps:** on a document's Edit tab, select a wide, short flowchart (e.g. 672×92) and drag its bottom-right corner handle.

**Seen:** a little downward movement outweighs a lot of sideways movement. Moving 25 px down grows it by about 360 px, so it jumps to Full width. The corner doesn't stay under the pointer.

**Done when**
- While dragging, the picture's corner follows the pointer as closely as its fixed proportions allow. The size comes from whichever of the sideways and downward movements best matches where the pointer is, never from a small movement multiplied by the picture's aspect ratio.
- A wide, short flowchart, a tall, narrow image and a square drawing can each be resized smoothly in small steps, both up and down, without jumping to Full width.
- Full width still happens when the picture is dragged to the column's edge (COM-2004's assumption).
- Tests cover a wide, short picture and a tall, narrow one being dragged, with the new width matching the pointer's position.

Independent: frontend only (richtext/pictureResize.ts).
