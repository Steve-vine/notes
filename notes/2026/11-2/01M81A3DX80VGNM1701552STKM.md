Sprint "Registers - Phase 2" (ADR 0167 §5, in COM-2087). Builds on the phase-1 registers (as-at view and PDF from COM-2082, reviews from COM-2083).

## What people see
- An audit pack now includes, without anyone choosing, every register kept for a control in its scope (its framework's controls, or the core controls) where that control is applicable in the pack's company.
- For each register:
  - **the register as it stood at the end of the period**, as the register PDF (cover, entries, archived marked). A shared register shows every entry with only this company's links.
  - **what changed during the period**: entries added, changed, archived and restored, with who and when (a table in the same PDF, after the entries).
  - **its reviews during the period**, listed with date and reviewer. Their PDFs are already in the pack as evidence on the register's controls; the index points to them.
- **The index** gains a Registers section (register, prefix, controls, entries at period end, last review), and each control's section says "Kept in: Obligations (OBL)".
- **The zip** has a Registers folder with one PDF per register.
- A Restricted register is included only if the person building the pack can open it; otherwise the index says "1 Restricted register was left out", without its name.
- The pack's preview (before building) lists the registers it will include.

## Implementation notes (technical)
- A new collector in `core/audit_pack.py` `collect_pack` and a block in `index_markdown` (no pluggable section list exists). Render each register via the COM-2082 PDF export with as-at = end of period (end of day, company time zone) plus a changes-in-period table from revisions.
- Place PDFs in a `Registers/` folder in `write_zip`.
- Restricted check against the pack's requester.

## Done when
- A SOC 2 pack for a company where INS.5 is in scope and applicable includes the obligations register as at the period end, its changes during the period and its reviews.
- An entry changed after the period shows its value as it was at period end.
- A Restricted register the builder can't open is left out and counted, unnamed.

Independent if the pack's API is unchanged; on the chain if the preview response gains a registers list (it probably does — then API).
