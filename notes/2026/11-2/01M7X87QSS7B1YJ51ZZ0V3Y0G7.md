Built to the ADR in COM-2038, after COM-2043.

**What changes**
- **An audit request's evidence** (Provided evidence on a request) uses the window. Add new uploads, with the period defaulting to the audit's period. The Link tab opens filtered to the request's controls. It replaces today's separate upload and pick-from-register controls. Suggestions (the request's suggested evidence and "Evidence of operation and check history") stay as they are, beside the button.
- **A finding's closing evidence** uses the window, opening filtered to the finding's controls (or its linked gaps' controls). Withdrawing closing evidence stays as it is (unlinks it from the finding).
- **Reviewing and closing an audit:** check what the review section and Close audit upload. Where it's evidence, it uses the window. Where it's the audit's own document (the auditor's report, kept on the audit), it keeps its own upload, and the PR says which and why.
- An ended audit keeps what it was finished with (COM-2040).

**Done when**
- A request's evidence and a finding's closing evidence are added through the window, with new and linked pieces showing their EVD IDs.
- The Link tab opens filtered to the request's or finding's controls.
- Their old upload and pick controls are gone.
