Sprint "Registers - Phase 2". Found in the pre-UAT pass of COM-2093 on staging e2248892e (23 Nov 2026). Screenshot: `~/compass-uat/sprint-122/COM-2093/ush-shared-register-p2.png`.

## The problem
A shared register in an audit pack prints the real names in its Company fields: "Demo", and "E2E Test Company, Demo" in the Applies to column of a pack made for E2E Test Company. A pack goes to auditors, so it tells them about another company.

## What people see
- In a register's PDF in a pack, a Company field shows the pack's company by name, and any other company as "another company".
- A value naming several companies shows the pack's company by name, and "another company" once for the rest.
- The same rule applies wherever the PDF shows a Company value, including What changed.
- The ordinary register export is unchanged: it's for people inside Compass.

Assumption, for Steve to confirm: packs follow the auditor portal's rule (COM-2095, ADR 0133 §6), because a pack also goes to auditors. COM-2093 didn't state it.

## Done when
- A pack for one company never contains another company's name in a register's PDF, in the entries or in What changed.
- The pack's own company still shows by name.
- The ordinary export still shows the real names.

No migration or API change expected.
