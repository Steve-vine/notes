From Steve's testing of round 2 (staging 43d03f09). Steve: "there are 2 types of checks, 'Built-in' and 'Custom', built-in checks are shipped with Compass and custom ones are defined by the user. Built-in checks also don't use workflows and aren't compatible with Workflows today."

**Today**
- **Built-in checks** ("Checks Compass runs") pass or fail. Each raises and closes its own gap ("Raised by Compass check"). They produce no evidence with a status, so a workflow can't be started by one, can't name one in its conditions, and can't act on its gap.
- **Custom checks** ("Evidence Compass collects") only collect. Workflows judge them.

**Behaviour** (assumptions decided here, not by Steve)
1. **One pair of names everywhere: Built-in and Custom.**
   - **Built-in** means shipped with Compass, with the test written into Compass.
   - **Custom** means defined for the company: designed by Polaris and approved by a person.
   - "Checks Compass runs" and "Evidence Compass collects" go. Both kinds sit in one "Checks" list on a control's Evidence tab (COM-1883), each card labelled Built-in or Custom.
   - Evidence Collection ▸ All checks gets a **Type** column (Built-in / Custom), which sorts and can be filtered, with the filter kept in the address.
   - The check's page header shows the label.
   - Polaris uses the same words.
   - The builder checks that Type reads clearly beside COM-1876's Kind column (Records / Snapshot).
2. **A built-in check's results become evidence.** Each run files its result as evidence on each of its controls, the way a snapshot custom check does.
   - **Status:** Compliant when it passes, Non-compliant when it fails, Unchecked when it can't tell.
   - **Contents:** what it failed or listed, as at that run.
   - **An unchanged result** moves "still true on" forward instead of filing again, so frequent runs don't flood the history.
   - **Its own verdict** counts as one opinion. A workflow may give another, and the two combine as any two verdicts do: Non-compliant wins (ADR 0155 §8).
3. **Workflows can use built-in checks:**
   - "Evidence collected" can be narrowed to a built-in check.
   - A condition can name a built-in check, and read its listed or failing items like a custom check's captured items.
   - "Evidence status changed" fires for its evidence.
   - Testing a workflow (COM-1855) works on built-in evidence too.
4. **Its own gap, or a workflow's.** Each built-in check has **Raises its own gap** (on by default), which keeps today's behaviour.
   - **Switching it off:** this asks first, closes the open gap the check raised, and leaves gaps to workflows from then on.
   - **Where:** it's set on the Built-in Checks tab (COM-1885) and in the check's Edit (COM-1882).
5. **Recorded.** Each change is an Activity line on the check.

**Done when**
- The behaviour above works on staging (Test Company). For example: a workflow "when CHK-n (a built-in check) is Non-compliant, raise a gap on its control" raises a gap after its own gap is switched off, and closes it when the check passes again.
- On a control's Evidence tab, a built-in check's evidence shows its status and history like a custom check's.
- Polaris can explain a built-in check's evidence and its status, naming the check and any workflow. Polaris can also draft workflows that use built-in checks, and the MCP instructions describe the two kinds.
- A new ADR records the two kinds of check, built-in results as evidence, and the "Raises its own gap" choice. Amendments to ADR 0132 and ADR 0155 point to it.
- Tests cover:
  - filing a built-in result, including an unchanged result moving forward;
  - the status combining with a workflow's;
  - each trigger and condition with a built-in check;
  - switching its own gap off and on;
  - the Type column, its sort and filter;
  - the no-per-service-code test still passing.

**Where** (for the builder): core/checks (engine, collected, directory), core/workflows (triggers.py `checks` narrowing, conditions, registry Names), core/collection/coverage.py, the evidence status model (ADR 0155), control/EvidenceTab.tsx, pages/ChecksPage.tsx, assistant/tools and MCP instructions. This needs a migration (built-in evidence and the per-check gap choice). It is on the chain.
