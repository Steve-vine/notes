ADR 0169 §2, §11 and Consequences.

**Chain:** independent (production infrastructure; no Compass code).

**Steve's decision (2026-11-23):** copies stay in the same S3 bucket as the evidence and the scheduled backups. The bucket is backed up externally, so there is **no second location**. This task was re-scoped from that to the part still needed.

**Why**

A copy holds everyone's personal data and is meant to be gone after 30 days. If the bucket keeps old versions, a "deleted" copy stays recoverable indefinitely.

**Done when**

- Production's actual versioning and lifecycle settings have been read from the live bucket.
- A copy deleted in Compass, or past 30 days, stops being recoverable from old bucket versions within a stated time.
- The rule applies only to the `compass-backups/` prefix. **Evidence and `postgres-backups/` are untouched.**
- The change and the way to check it are written down in the deployment repo.

**Technical notes**

- The setup script (`examples/aws/s3-irsa.sh`) turns versioning on and adds no lifecycle rule. That was read from the script, not the live bucket, so check first.
- Changes are in `devops.infrastructure.aws` / `devops.application.compass`: **Claude prepares them and Steve commits and applies.**
- The external backup's own retention is outside this task and outside Compass (ADR 0169 §11).
