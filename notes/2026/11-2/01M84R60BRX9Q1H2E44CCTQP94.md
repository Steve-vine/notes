Sprint "Registers - Phase 2". Found preparing the auditor portal check of COM-2095 on staging f6abe2fd5 (23 Nov 2026). Sample: `~/compass-uat/sprint-122/COM-2095/EVD-112-filed-review-as-held.pdf` (EVD-112 on AUD-7, AQ-5).

## The problem
Marking a register as reviewed files a PDF of the register as evidence. That PDF holds real names: EVD-112 names "Demo" on 3 rows, and E2E Viewer and E2E Analyst. If someone provides it to an audit request, the portal serves the stored file with only a watermark added (confirmed in the code; not yet seen as an auditor, because the portal is off on staging). ADR 0133 says an auditor never sees a person's name or anything of another company, and COM-2095 applies that to a register provided directly. COM-2106 closed the same route for audit packs only.

## What people see (as the auditor)
- A filed review PDF of a register, opened or downloaded in the portal or included in Download all, shows people as "the company" and any company but the audit's as "another company", as the portal's own view of a register does.
- It shows no links or evidence on entries, and no review comment or reviewer, as in COM-2095.
- Its cover says it's a copy prepared for the audit from the register's history.
- A withdrawn one still can't be opened.
- Inside Compass, the filed PDF stays exactly as filed.

Assumption, for Steve to confirm: the auditor gets a rebuilt copy, as packs do since COM-2106. The alternative is refusing to provide a filed review PDF to an audit and asking people to provide the register itself.

## Done when
- With planted people's and other companies' names in a shared register, a filed review PDF provided to a request contains none of them when downloaded by an auditor, singly or in Download all, including in the PDF's metadata and file name.
- The audit's own company still shows by name.
- The filed evidence inside Compass is byte-for-byte unchanged.
- Other evidence files are served as they are today.

No migration expected. On the chain if a portal route changes.
