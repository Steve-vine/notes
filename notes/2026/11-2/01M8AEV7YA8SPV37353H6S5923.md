Split out of COM-850. **On the chain** (migration + API). Stacks on COM-2173, which holds, shows and watches the right.

The pattern is send on behalf on lists (COM-954 / COM-956, ADR 0106), applied to shared mailboxes.

## What people see

- **Roles.** A role's shared-mailbox line can include **Send on behalf** alongside Full Access and Send As. Joiners, movers and leavers follow, as they do for the other mailbox rights.
- **Requests.** Send on behalf can be asked for, or removed, on its own, through the usual approval.
- **Carrying it out**, once approved:
  - A **cloud mailbox**: applied by Compass through Exchange.
  - A mailbox **synchronised from on-prem AD**, in an OU Compass manages: applied by Compass in AD.
  - Otherwise: a **to-do for on-premises**, done by hand and confirmed when the next read sees it (ADR 0079).
- **Unrequested changes.** Undoing one (from COM-2173) follows the same routing as above.
- **Recertification.** A shared mailbox's review gains a row per person who can send on behalf ("Sam Jones — Send on behalf · from role Finance"). Removing it follows the same routing. A to-do shows "To be removed by hand, in AD" and offers **Confirm removed**.

## Notes (technical)

- **Routing:** copy `core/list_rights.py` `route()`.
  - Exchange: `exchange_scripts/set_mailbox_access.ps1` gains `Set-Mailbox -GrantSendOnBehalfTo @{Add=…}` / `@{Remove=…}`, and must return `manual` for a synchronised object, as the list script does.
  - AD: `ad_writer.add_public_delegate` / `remove_public_delegate`, as `_list_right_in_ad` in `tasks/access_execute.py` does.
  - To-do: new `ManualStepAction` values for mailbox send on behalf.
- **Kinds:** extend the mailbox-access change kinds and `BusinessRoleMailbox` with the new right (`core/mailbox_grants.py`). Every Compass write is stamped in the ledger so it doesn't come back as unrequested.
- **Recert:** add the row to the shared-mailbox scope in `tasks/recert.py`, next to the existing mailbox-access rows.
- **Check while here:** COM-956's done comment noted that flagged *direct shared-mailbox access* rows were never sent to Exchange when a review was completed by two attestors in the portal (dating from COM-741). Confirm whether that was fixed. If not, make sure the new rows go out on both paths, and raise the old gap as its own bug.

**Done when:** on staging, a role with send on behalf on a cloud test shared mailbox, applied by a mover, results in the right shown in Exchange and in the mirror after the next read. A synchronised mailbox outside a managed OU raises the on-prem to-do instead. Removing the right through a recert takes it away again.
