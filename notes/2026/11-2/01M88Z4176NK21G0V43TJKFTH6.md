Sprint "Business Services". Steve, 25 Nov 2026: "Allow linking from the asset's page too." Today (COM-2151) links are made only from the service's Depends on card; the asset's Supports services card is read-only.

## What people see
- On a system's, software's or data asset's page, the **Supports services** card has **Link to a business service** for people who can manage the inventory. It offers a picker of the company's active services, with an optional note (the same note as on the service's side).
- Each direct link on the asset's card has an unlink button. Routes shown "through" another system stay read-only, because they come from Inventory's own dependencies.
- A link made from either side is the same link: it shows on the service's Depends on card and the asset's Supports services card, with the same note and the same History line on the service.
- The rules are the same as from the service's side: a retired service can't be newly linked; a decommissioned asset can't take a new link; a service already linked isn't offered again.
- People who can only view the inventory see the card read-only, as now.

## Done when
- In E2E Test Company: from AST-42's page, link it to Payroll with a note. Payroll's Depends on card shows AST-42 with that note, and History records it. Unlink it from AST-42's page, and it's gone from both.
- Do the same from a software asset and a data asset.
- A viewer sees no link or unlink buttons.
