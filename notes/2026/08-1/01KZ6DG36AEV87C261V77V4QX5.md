Follow-up to ISE-526/527: the estate can say an app registration is somebody else's, but not **whose**. `app_owner_tenant_id` is stored and rendered nowhere, and a GUID is not an answer to "who".

## Live coverage, measured 2026-08-04

Read-only Graph probes with the existing EntraID credential, against 1,249 externally-owned service principals (the 1,447 "third-party" total minus the 198 that have no owning tenant at all — see the last section):

| source | principals | share |
|---|---|---|
| known Microsoft tenant GUID | 840 | 67% |
| `verifiedPublisher.displayName` | 324 | 25% |
| **still just a GUID** | **85** | **6%** |

**94% is nameable with no new permission.**

## 1. `verifiedPublisher` — free, and the good half

One extra field in the SP `$select` the connector already issues. No extra call, no new grant. It returns real vendor names: `Cisco`, `WORKNEST LIMITED`, `Microsoft Corporation`.

Only 18% of *all* principals carry it because publisher verification is opt-in for the vendor — but it is the one source that is a **verified claim** rather than an inference, so where it exists it should win.

## 2. A small map of well-known Microsoft tenants — the bulk

Three GUIDs cover two-thirds of the external estate:

- `f8cdef31-a31e-4b4a-93e4-5f571e91255a` — Microsoft Services (**992** principals on its own)
- `72f988bf-86f1-41af-91ab-2d7cd011db47` — Microsoft
- `9188040d-6c67-4c5b-b112-36a304b66dad` — Personal Microsoft accounts

Keep the map small, named, and a **fallback only**: an unknown GUID stays a GUID. ADR 0073's refusal to guess applies here too — a wrong vendor name is worse than an honest identifier.

## 3. `publisherName` is a dead end — checked, not assumed

Graph documents `publisherName` on `servicePrincipal`. It came back **empty for all 1,782 principals** in this tenant. Do not spend time on it; this note exists so nobody rediscovers it.

## 4. The last 6% needs a new permission — decide it separately

`GET /tenantRelationships/findTenantInformationByTenantId(tenantId='…')` resolves any tenant GUID to a display name and default domain. It **403s with the current credential** (verified against three GUIDs) — it wants `CrossTenantInformation.ReadBasic.All`.

That is a low-privilege read-only scope, and it would name the remaining 85 (Adobe, Amazon Alexa, Apple Business Manager, AskNicely…). But after ISE-525 a new Graph grant is a decision to take deliberately, not one to slip in: if it lands, it belongs in the credential spec, the standing-risk register, and the credential form's scope list — the same treatment `Policy.Read.All` got.

**Recommendation: ship 1 and 2 first.** They are free and cover 94%. Take 4 as its own decision once the value of the last 85 is visible.

## 5. The 198 that are not third parties at all

Separately, and arguably a bug in ISE-526: 198 principals have **no** `appOwnerOrganizationId` — 169 `ManagedIdentity`, 26 `ServiceIdentity`, 2 `Legacy`, 1 `SocialIdp`. `tenant_owned` is computed as `owner == our tenant`, so absence reads as false, so they are stamped `operated_by: external` and land under "Third-party".

A managed identity is provisioned in our own tenant for our own Azure resources. Calling it third-party is wrong.

Settle in plan mode: either treat absence as **unknown** and stamp nothing (silence already means ours, ADR 0073), or classify `ManagedIdentity`/`ServiceIdentity` explicitly as ours. Prefer the first — it states less.

**This will move the numbers ISE-527 was verified against**: third-party drops 1,447 → ~1,249 and ours rises 334 → ~532. Expected, and worth saying out loud before it looks like a regression.

## UI

The publisher belongs beside the Ours / Third-party badge on the entity page (ISE-526's `IdentityAppLabels`) — "Third-party · Cisco". Consider whether ISE-527's Estate filter should also offer the publisher; probably not a filter, but the Estate list column showing it would answer "who are all these vendors?" at a glance.

## Definition of done

An operator looking at a third-party app registration can see who it belongs to by name wherever Entra will tell us, and an honest identifier where it will not — with no name ISE had to guess at.
