Found in Sprint 46 Estate testing. On env-staging-uk, all 30 Argo Rollouts across the four chinwag namespaces (chinwag-dev/-test, chinwag-v2-dev/-test) sync correctly, but nearly all of them have been merged into a single entity ("chinwag-task-scheduler", with "chinwag-chat" holding one more). Searching "chinwag" therefore returns 3 workloads instead of ~30.

**Cause:** the Kubernetes connector emits a `datadog:service:<tag>` cross-key per workload (`connectors/kubernetes.py`). When many distinct workloads share the same DataDog service tag, they all resolve to the same cross-key and merge into one entity — and chained merges then bridge across namespaces and even across the chinwag / chinwag-v2 service tags. Merges are never torn down (Sprint 45 semantics), so the collapse is permanent without a fix + data repair.

**Scale:** env-staging-uk has 95 k8s workload aliases but only 67 workload entities — the collapse is not chinwag-specific.

**Needs:** stop the shared-service-tag cross-key from merging distinct workloads (e.g. only materialise when 1:1, or scope it), plus a repair path for already-collapsed entities.
