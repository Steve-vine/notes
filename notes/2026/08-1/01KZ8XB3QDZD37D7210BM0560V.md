Found during Sprint 50 incident-management testing: in the incident chat, the AI response often lands below the fold — the operator doesn't realise they need to scroll and misses it.

## Current behaviour

`IssueTimeline.tsx` (~line 95) pins to the bottom of the shared scroll viewport, but only when the reader is already within 160px of the bottom (so background polls don't yank someone reading history). Two failure modes:

- If the operator isn't near the bottom when they submit (e.g. after reading the header or earlier history), the autoscroll never fires and the answer arrives off-screen with no cue.
- When it does follow, it tracks the *tail* of the streaming answer — the question scrolls away and the operator watches text crawl at the bottom instead of reading from the top.

## Desired behaviour (the claude.ai / ChatGPT convention)

On submit — typing a question in the composer, or clicking any button that elicits a response (Diagnose, Analyse, Propose remediation, playbook run) — scroll the viewport so the just-sent question / action placeholder sits at the **top** of the visible area, leaving the rest of the screen for the incoming response. This should fire unconditionally on an explicit user action (an explicit send is not a background poll — yanking is correct here), while the existing near-bottom guard stays for passive poll updates.

Applies to the shared viewport owned by IssueBody. Check whether AssistPage has the same pattern and wants the same fix.
