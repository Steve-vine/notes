The user-facing deliverable for the Application layer. Without it the layer exists only in the database, which is the failure mode the DoD exists to prevent.

- **List** — Applications as `app.env`, filterable by environment and by Business Service, showing member count and current signal state.
- **Detail** — the membership predicate and what it currently resolves to; member Resources; the Business Services above; environment; annotations; signals and incident history.
- **Proposal flow** — pending "this looks like an Application" proposals reachable from here as well as from the proposals queue, with the resources they'd claim shown before confirming.
- **Empty state that teaches** — an estate with no Applications yet should say what tag would create one, not show a blank table.
- Nav entry under the estate section (`components/nav.ts` shows the full information architecture, placeholders included).

Cross-check against `docs/briefs/ui-brief.md`; the entity-detail patterns already exist and should be reused rather than reinvented.

**Acceptance**: an operator can see every Application, what it is made of, and what it serves — and confirm a proposed one — without leaving the UI.

Depends on the Applications-as-entities task.
