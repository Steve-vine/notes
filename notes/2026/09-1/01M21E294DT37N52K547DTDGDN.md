Asked for by Steve while smoke-testing, 2026-09-08. Follows COM-625 (the new status vocabulary) — the status picker's shape depends on it, so land that first.

## Search

A **Search** box joins the filter row, first in the row as on the Controls list, matching the words you can already see on the register: the control's reference, the gap's title and description, and the owner's name. Typing narrows the list as you type; the match is case-insensitive and applies on top of whatever the pickers say. The count under the filters reflects the result.

## The three pickers take more than one value

**Gap status**, **Tier** and **Owner** each accept several values at once — pick On hold *and* Under review, Essential *and* Expected, two owners. A gap shows if it matches **any** of the picked values within a picker, and **every** picker that has something picked. Each picked value shows as a removable chip in the box; clearing a picker means "any".

- **Gap status.** Defaults to the four open states ticked (New, In progress, On hold, Under review) — that *is* COM-625's "All open". An **All open** entry sits first in the list and, when picked, re-ticks exactly those four, so getting back to the default is one click rather than four. (Assumption, stated because COM-625 described All open as a single option in a single-value picker: with several values pickable, the default is the four open states pre-ticked and All open is the shortcut back to them.)
- **Tier.** Same three tiers, several at once. The Controls list and the Assessments queue share this picker; **they keep their single-value behaviour** — this task only changes the Gaps register. If Steve wants them multi-valued too, that is a separate task.
- **Owner.** Several people at once, plus **Unassigned** as a value that can be combined with people. The **Owned by me** switch stays and keeps its rule: turning it on clears the Owner picker, picking an owner turns it off.

The status column's sort and grouping are unchanged.
