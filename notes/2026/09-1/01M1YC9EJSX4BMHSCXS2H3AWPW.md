## Control Objective

Callitech Limited provides data subjects with a process to object to specific types of processing of their personal information, such as use of call recordings or CRM data for marketing, quality analytics, or AI model training/improvement, and to have such objections enforced and monitored.

## Implementation Guidance

Data subjects may submit objections to processing (e.g., marketing communications, use of recordings for AI training/analytics) via the Service Desk or published privacy contact.Objections are acknowledged, and the requester's identity is verified consistent with the access-rights process.Approved objections are propagated to the relevant system(s) (CRM marketing lists, call-recording/AI processing workflows) and tracked to confirm enforcement.A record of objections received, decisions made, and enforcement actions taken is maintained.Complaints and disputes related to processing objections are logged and tracked to resolution, with periodic review by IT/Cybersecurity leadership.

Privacy Inquiries, Complaints and Dispute Resolution

The published privacy contact and the Service Desk also serve as the intake channels for any privacy-related inquiry, complaint or dispute, including matters unrelated to objections (for example, challenges to the accuracy of personal information, concerns about a disclosure to a third party or sub-processor, or a claim that Callitech Limited has not met a commitment in its privacy notice or client contracts).Every privacy inquiry, complaint or dispute is logged in a single privacy case register with the date received, the intake channel, the nature of the matter, the assigned owner, the investigation outcome, and the date closed.Each matter is acknowledged to the requester on receipt and the resolution — including the decision reached and, where the matter is declined, the reason and any escalation route available to the individual — is communicated back to the requester in writing within the timeframe applicable to the individual's location (EU/UK data subjects: no later than one month from receipt, consistent with the access-rights process; US-based individuals: within the same standard timeframe unless a shorter statutory period applies).Matters that cannot be resolved by the Service Desk, that involve a potential breach of a privacy commitment, or that are escalated by the individual are referred to the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity for decision, with legal counsel engaged where a regulatory notification or contractual client notification may be required.

Periodic Compliance Monitoring and Corrective Action

At least annually, and following any material change to Callitech's processing (including changes to AI features, hosting arrangements in the EU or US, or sub-processors), IT/Cybersecurity leadership performs a review of compliance with Callitech's stated privacy commitments and applicable privacy obligations. The review covers: the completeness and timeliness of responses in the privacy case register, whether objections and other data subject requests were enforced in the relevant systems, adherence to the published privacy notice, and the outcomes of any client or regulator enquiries.Deficiencies identified by the review, by a complaint investigation, or reported by a client or regulator are recorded as remediation items with a named owner, a severity rating and a target completion date. Remediation status is tracked to closure and reported to senior management; overdue items are escalated.Where a deficiency affected identifiable individuals or client data, the correction made is documented and, where appropriate, communicated to the affected individuals and to the client acting as controller.

## Evidence Request

Objection intake log with request type, verification, and resolution status.Evidence that an approved objection was enforced in the relevant system (e.g., marketing suppression, AI-processing exclusion).Complaint/dispute log with resolution notes.Periodic review notes confirming continued compliance.

Ownership

Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, with Service Desk handling intake.

Additional Evidence request

Privacy case register covering inquiries, complaints and disputes, showing acknowledgement date, resolution date and evidence that the resolution was communicated to the requester.Most recent periodic privacy compliance review report.Remediation tracker showing deficiencies, owners, due dates and closure evidence.
