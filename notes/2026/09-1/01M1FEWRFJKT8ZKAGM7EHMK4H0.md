Build what the Business Services & Definitions spec defines: the store the Correlator reads, and the screen a human authors it in.

**The screen is the point.** The current definition is fragmented across the tag dictionary, the Collections trio and dashboard services, and is read only by screens — which is how it ended up near-empty without anything breaking. One place to say what a business service is made of, and what happens to the business when it breaks.

Includes the tag-predicate composition, the direct-from-Estate override for the legacy exceptions, and the business-context prose the Oracle and the Visibility layer both use to explain a problem in terms an operator recognises.

**Watch:** 198 proposals sit unworked today, and they are the mechanism that would populate this. An editor nobody uses reproduces the current state — the flow from proposal to definition needs to be part of the design, not an afterthought.

**Blocked by** the Business Services & Definitions spec.
