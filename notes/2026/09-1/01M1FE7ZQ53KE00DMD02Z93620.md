Record the layered flow design as architecture: Integrations → Differ → Correlator → Oracle → Visibility → Actor, with the Conductor pacing all of it and the Arbiter on the outbound edge.

Amends ADR 0025 (an Incident is created by the Correlator, not at ingest from severity alone) and ADR 0030 (the Obs Loop's outbound detection returns to the Integrations, its cadence to the Conductor, its name retires in favour of the Differ).

Design recorded in the Notuvia memos **ISE Flow** and **ISE Naming Convention**. Documentation only — no code change. Three follow-on ADRs are deliberately deferred: Business Services &amp; Definitions, the prioritisation vocabulary, and Playbooks.
