## Control Objective

Callitech Limited maintains network and data flow diagrams documenting system components, processes, data stores, and interconnections across its Wrexham (UK), Atlanta and Florida (US) offices, and its EU/US-hosted cloud infrastructure.

## Implementation Guidance

Network diagrams map physical and logical components across office locations and cloud-hosted environments, including connections to third-party telecom and cloud providers that are operationally critical to service delivery.Data flow diagrams illustrate how employee PII, CRM data, call recordings, and customer content move between systems, users, and storage locations, including flows related to AI-based answering systems and third-party workflow tools that expose APIs.Diagrams are reviewed and updated when significant infrastructure or application changes occur (e.g., changes to cloud architecture, new AI answering capabilities, or new third-party integrations), and otherwise on a periodic basis.Diagrams are made available to Infrastructure and Cybersecurity personnel to support security planning, risk assessment, and incident response.

## Evidence Request

Current network diagram(s) covering office and cloud environments.Current data flow diagram(s) reflecting sensitive data movement, including AI answering system and third-party integration flows.Record of the most recent diagram review/update.
