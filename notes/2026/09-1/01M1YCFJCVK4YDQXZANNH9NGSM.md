## Control Objective

A documented change management process is established and maintained to systematically track and control changes to Callitech Limited's computing infrastructure, including the cloud, identity, and communications systems that support its mission-critical telephone answering services, as well as changes involving key telecom and cloud provider dependencies.

## Implementation Guidance

Proposed changes to infrastructure, applications, or configurations are documented, including the purpose of the change and the systems affected.Changes affecting mission-critical services (telephone answering, cloud, identity, and communications systems) are reviewed for potential impact, including dependencies on third-party telecom and cloud providers, before approval.Changes are approved by appropriate personnel (e.g., the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity or a designated reviewer) prior to implementation.A record of changes is maintained, including what was changed, who approved it, when it was implemented, and the outcome.Rollback or remediation steps are identified for changes so that systems can be restored promptly if a change causes disruption to telephone answering, live chat, or related services.The change management process is reviewed periodically to ensure it remains effective as the organization's infrastructure and third-party dependencies evolve.

## Evidence Request

Change request records showing description, impact assessment, and approval.Change log or tracking record covering recent infrastructure changes.Examples of rollback procedures or records of a rollback being performed, if applicable.Evidence of review/approval by an authorized individual prior to implementation.
