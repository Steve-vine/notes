## Control Objective

Callitech Limited identifies the information required to support the functioning of internal control over its telephone answering, live chat, and AI-based answering services, obtains that information from defined internal and external sources, and takes reasonable steps to ensure it is relevant, accurate, timely, and complete enough for management decision-making.

## Implementation Guidance

Identification of information requirements: The Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, with input from the DevOps, service desk, and HR functions, maintains a list of the information needed to operate and monitor the security program — for example: system and administrative access logs, authentication/MFA events, monitoring and alerting output, vulnerability and patching status, change records, incident and service desk tickets, training completion records, third-party/vendor inventory and due diligence results, and risk assessment (BIA/TRA) outputs.Internal sources: Information is captured from Callitech's own infrastructure and applications across its UK (Wrexham) and US (Atlanta, Florida) operations and its EU- and US-hosted environments, including cloud and identity platforms, the supported databases (MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, SQL Server), and internal ticketing/collaboration tools.External sources: Information is also obtained from external parties where it affects internal control — for example telecom and cloud provider status and security notices, vendor security documentation and attestations, AI-vendor release notes, client contractual security requirements, and regulatory guidance relevant to UK/EU GDPR and, where applicable to specific clients, HIPAA and PCI DSS.Processing into usable information: Relevant data is consolidated (currently within the Carbide platform, SharePoint, and the service desk tooling) so that control performance, open risks, and incidents can be reviewed by management. Records are dated and attributable to a source system or owner.Quality expectations: Information used for control decisions is expected to be current, sourced from an authoritative system or named owner, and complete for the period under review; obvious gaps or stale records are corrected or flagged before the information is relied upon. Access to the underlying records is restricted to authorized personnel, and records are retained for a period sufficient to support audit and regulatory obligations.Review: The set of information requirements is reviewed at least annually, and when the technology stack, client obligations, or the AI-based answering product line change materially.

## Evidence Request

The current list or register of information requirements/sources supporting internal control.Sample reports or extracts drawn from those sources (e.g., access review output, monitoring alerts, vendor notices, training completion export).Evidence of review or correction where information was found incomplete or out of date.
