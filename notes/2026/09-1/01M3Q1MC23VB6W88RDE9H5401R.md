Carries the trail from COM-787 into Access. Much of Access happens in pop-ups: a person, a group, a device, a conditional access policy. Today those pop-ups have no address of their own. So when you follow a link out of one (say, a person's business role), neither the trail nor the browser's Back can bring the pop-up back. You return to the Users list and have to find the person again.

## What people see

- **Fixed back links go** from the role, directory role, shared mailbox, request and recertification campaign pages, which today show "← Role matrix", "← Requests" and so on. The report page, which has no back link today, gets the trail too.
- **Access's tabs** (Roles, Requests, Users, Groups, …) behave like any other tabs: the Access step returns to the tab you were on.
- **The pop-ups for a person, a group, a device and a conditional access policy each get their own address.** The trail can then reopen them.
  - Example: Users → open *Jane Smith* → follow her *Finance* business role. The trail reads `Access › Jane Smith › Finance`. Clicking *Jane Smith* returns you to the Users list with her pop-up open.
  - As a side benefit, these pop-ups can be ctrl-clicked into a new tab, and their links can be copied and sent. That's what the pop-up convention already promises.
- **Pop-ups are still not steps of their own**, and a pop-up opened from a pop-up stays the "leaf" the convention allows.
- **Following a group from an asset's access card opens that group.** Today it lands on the Groups list and ignores which group you meant.
