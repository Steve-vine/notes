## Control Objective

Callitech Limited maintains a capacity management process to ensure its telephone answering services (TAS), live chat platform, AI-based answering systems, and supporting cloud and identity infrastructure have adequate resources to remain available and performant, including during periods of rapid growth and demand spikes.

## Implementation Guidance

Resource utilization across mission-critical systems — telephone answering services, cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and communications platforms — is monitored on an ongoing basis to identify usage trends.Given the recent rapid growth of AI-based answering systems and associated scaling challenges, capacity needs for these systems are assessed and reviewed more frequently to anticipate demand before it affects service availability.Alerting thresholds are configured so that the Infrastructure team is notified before critical infrastructure components (e.g., call handling capacity, cloud compute/storage) approach exhaustion.Where supported by the cloud provider, auto-scaling or similar elastic capacity mechanisms are used to help absorb demand spikes for cloud-hosted components.Capacity plans and thresholds are reviewed periodically and updated to reflect business growth projections, seasonal call volume patterns, and new service offerings.

## Evidence Request

Summary of capacity monitoring approach and thresholds for TAS, cloud, and identity/communications systems.Evidence of monitoring/alerting configuration and a sample of capacity-related alerts.Evidence of scaling actions taken during periods of demand growth (e.g., AI answering system growth).Record of periodic capacity plan review.
