## Control Objective

A structured training and awareness program is established, maintained, and enforced to ensure that employees, contractors, and relevant third parties receive ongoing education to develop and maintain the skills, technical knowledge, and security awareness needed to protect Callitech Limited's systems and data.

## Implementation Guidance

The program covers core security topics including phishing and social engineering awareness, password and authentication practices, protection of PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content, and incident reporting. Given the company's HIPAA obligations for certain clients and its self-certified PCI DSS status, training content addresses safe handling of sensitive customer and payment-adjacent information. New hires complete required training as part of onboarding, and all personnel complete periodic refresher training; personnel in Infrastructure, DevOps, and Cybersecurity roles receive additional targeted training relevant to emerging threats and the company's growing AI-based answering systems. Completion is tracked, including dates, topics covered, and participants, and records are retained by HR/the security function. The program and its content are reviewed periodically to reflect regulatory developments (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA) and the evolving threat landscape.
