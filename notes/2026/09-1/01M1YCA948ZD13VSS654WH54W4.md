## Control Objective

Callitech Limited maintains a breach notification process to notify affected individuals, regulatory authorities (including the UK ICO where required under UK GDPR), and impacted clients in the event of a breach involving employee PII, customer CRM data, call recordings, or customer content, including breaches affecting clients with HIPAA-related expectations.

## Implementation Guidance

Incident types requiring breach notification are defined (e.g., unauthorized access to CRM, call recordings, or customer content; compromise of telecom/cloud provider systems).Notification timelines follow applicable regulatory requirements (e.g., UK GDPR notification to the ICO without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours; applicable US state and client-contractual notification timelines).The breach notification process is integrated with Callitech's incident response procedure, including investigation, containment, and legal/compliance review before notification.Responsible parties (IT/Cybersecurity leadership) and current contact lists for regulators, affected individuals, and impacted clients are maintained.Notification templates and records of notifications sent, including rationale for any delay or exception, are retained.The breach notification process is reviewed periodically, particularly as AI-based answering and additional third-party dependencies grow.

Register of unauthorized disclosures

In addition to notification records, Callitech maintains a register of every detected or reported unauthorized disclosure of personal information, whether or not it ultimately meets a regulatory or contractual notification threshold:

The register is populated for all events involving actual or suspected unauthorized access to, or disclosure of, employee PII, CRM records, call recordings, or customer content, including events reported by telecom, cloud, or AI-tooling vendors under their notification commitments.Each entry is created promptly on detection or report and records: date and time detected or reported, source of the report (internal staff, monitoring, client, or vendor), description of the event, categories of personal information and approximate number of data subjects affected, jurisdictions involved (UK/EU and US), containment actions, the notification assessment decision and its rationale, and, where applicable, a reference to the notifications issued.Entries are kept accurate and updated as the investigation progresses, and are closed only after the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity confirms the assessment and any required notifications.The register is retained for the period required by applicable law and client contracts (no less than the retention applied to other incident records), and access to it is restricted to authorized IT/Cybersecurity and Service Desk personnel.The register is reviewed periodically for completeness against incident response tickets and vendor-reported incidents.
