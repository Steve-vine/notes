## Control Objective

Callitech Limited limits the collection of personal information—including customer contact details captured during telephone-answering and live-chat interactions, call recordings, and employee PII—to what is necessary to deliver contracted services and meet legal or client obligations.

## Implementation Guidance

Purposes for collecting PII are documented by service line (TAS, live chat, AI-based answering, HR/employee data).New collection points (e.g., new client intake scripts, CRM fields, AI answering configurations) are reviewed by IT/Cybersecurity leadership for necessity before deployment.Privacy notices and call-recording announcements communicate what information is collected and why.Staff involved in call handling and CRM data entry are made aware of data minimization expectations as part of onboarding/training.Collection practices are reviewed periodically, particularly when onboarding new clients with additional compliance requirements (e.g., HIPAA, PCI DSS).

## Evidence Request

Documentation listing collection purposes by service line.Sample review of a new collection point (client intake form, CRM field, or AI configuration) showing minimization consideration.Privacy notice/call announcement excerpts describing information collected.Training materials or records covering data minimization for call-handling staff.

Ownership

Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, with input from client account and Service Desk teams.
