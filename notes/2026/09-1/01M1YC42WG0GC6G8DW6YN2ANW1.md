## Control Objective

Policies are established to define Callitech Limited's approach to privacy, security, and compliance across its Wrexham (UK), Atlanta (US), and Florida (US) operations.

## Implementation Guidance

Callitech maintains a set of governing policies and procedures owned by the security function (led by the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, with responsibility spanning service desk, infrastructure, DevOps, and cybersecurity). These policies establish a consistent framework across both UK and US locations and cover:

Legal and regulatory compliance: alignment with UK GDPR/GDPR, EU Cyber Essentials requirements, and, for applicable client relationships, HIPAA and self-certified PCI DSS obligations.Roles and responsibilities: accountability for privacy, security, and compliance activities, including how responsibilities are divided across infrastructure, DevOps, and service desk functions.Access control and segregation of duties: definition of authority levels to prevent unauthorized access or conflicts of interest, particularly given the sensitivity of PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content handled by Callitech's telephone answering and live chat services.Incident response: procedures for identifying, escalating, and remediating security or privacy incidents across both regions.Training and awareness: periodic communication of policy obligations to employees in both offices.Continuous improvement: periodic review of policies as the business grows (including the rapidly scaling AI-based answering line of business).

Policies are being migrated into and tracked through the Carbide platform as Callitech moves away from ad hoc SharePoint/spreadsheet-based documentation.

Change log requirements

Each policy includes a change log recording, at minimum: the reviewer's name and title, the date of review, and a description of the change (classified as minor, e.g., wording/typo corrections, or major, e.g., substantive scope or control changes). Minor changes are logged without requiring re-approval. Major changes trigger a review, an employee awareness communication, and re-approval by the policy owner.

Policy review guidelines

All policies are reviewed at least annually, or sooner when a significant change occurs (e.g., regulatory change, new client compliance requirement such as HIPAA, material business change such as the AI answering system expansion, or a security incident).

## Evidence Request

Policy approval records tracked in Carbide.Acknowledgment/deployment evidence for policies communicated to employees.Policy ownership records identifying the responsible owner (e.g., Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity).Change logs showing minor vs. major revisions and re-approval where required.
