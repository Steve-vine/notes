## Control Objective

An audit and compliance program is established and maintained to verify Callitech Limited's adherence to applicable laws, regulations, policies, and standards, ensuring controls effectively mitigate risk and support accountability.

## Implementation Guidance

Callitech's audit and compliance activity supports its obligations under UK GDPR/GDPR, EU Cyber Essentials, and (for applicable clients) HIPAA and self-certified PCI DSS, in addition to the SOC 2 program now being pursued to meet client expectations across both UK and US operations. This includes:

Regular evaluations: periodic internal reviews and, as the SOC 2 program matures, independent external audits assessing whether controls are present and functioning across the Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida offices.Compliance assessments: identification of non-compliance risks, including those arising from fragmented vendor management practices (currently tracked via SharePoint/spreadsheets without a dedicated vendor management resource) and from the rapid scaling of AI-based answering systems.Corrective actions: remediation plans with assigned ownership (typically within the infrastructure/cybersecurity function) and target dates, tracked to closure.Stakeholder reporting: findings and remediation status communicated to leadership and the Board.

As Callitech's use of the Carbide platform matures, audit evidence and corrective action tracking are being migrated from manual, spreadsheet-based methods to the platform's evidence manager.

## Evidence Request

Audit reports (internal or external) detailing findings and recommendations.Corrective action plans with owners, deadlines, and status.Audit schedules and criteria applied.Trend analysis or repeat-finding tracking demonstrating proactive improvement.
