Raised by Steve, 2026-09-06.

On the assessment panel, picking a maturity level shows that level's definition — *"Performed and broadly consistent, but informal — relies on experience, not documentation; quality varies."* It appears **above** the picker, between the label and the box. It should sit **below** it.

## Why it is the odd one out

Every other description in the app — 173 of them — is a static hint: it explains what to put in a field, and it belongs above the box because you read it *before* you act.

This one is not a hint. It is the definition of the level you have just chosen, and it changes each time you change the selection. That is an echo of a decision, not an instruction, and it reads after the act rather than before it — which is the other side of the box.

It also stops the field jumping. The maturity picker sits beside the status picker, and today the definition appears above the input, so choosing a level pushes the box down and the two fields fall out of line; clearing it pulls the box back up. With the text underneath, the input stays where it is and the definition grows downward into space.

## What changes

- The definition renders below the maturity picker on the assessment panel.
- **Only this field.** The other 173 descriptions are static hints and stay where they are — this is not a sweep, and it should not become one. Mantine takes the order as a prop on the field, so nothing global is touched.
