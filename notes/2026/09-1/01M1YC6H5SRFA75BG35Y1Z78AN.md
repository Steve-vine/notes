## Control Objective

A standardized onboarding process is implemented and maintained to ensure new-hire transitions at Callitech Limited are managed consistently, securely, and in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.

## Implementation Guidance

As part of the joiner process, HR and the service desk/IT function coordinate to provision access to required systems (e.g., CRM, telephony/call-recording platforms, email) based on the employee's role, following documented authorization by the appropriate manager before credentials are issued. New hires complete orientation covering job expectations, security protocols, and organizational policies, and complete initial security awareness training (per the training and awareness program) before or promptly upon gaining system access. Signed agreements, including confidentiality and code-of-conduct acknowledgments (per the employee agreements control), are collected and retained. Onboarding is documented via a checklist, and completion records are securely stored across the company's UK and US locations.
