## Control Objective

Assess and mitigate privacy risks related to personal data processing through a structured Privacy Impact Assessment process.

## Implementation Guidance

Conduct PIAs for new administrative practices, systems, or material changes affecting personal information.Identify data types, processing purposes, legal basis, privacy risks, and mitigation actions.Route PIAs for required internal and external review before implementation where applicable.Retain review dates, approvals, comments, and mitigation tracking.Reassess periodically and when processing changes materially.

## Evidence Request

Provide a copy of the completed PIA, including sections that outline the types of personal information collected, the purposes for handling data, identified risks, and mitigation measures. Ensure the document shows any review dates and updates made over time to maintain accuracy and alignment with current practices and regulations.
