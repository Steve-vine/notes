## Control Objective

Callitech Limited establishes security requirements to protect its assets — including devices, systems, and data used outside of its Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida offices — from unauthorized access, theft, loss, damage, or destruction.

## Implementation Guidance

Assets identified in the asset inventory (endpoints, cloud/hosting infrastructure, databases, application components, and data assets such as PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content) are categorized based on sensitivity and criticality, informed by client requirements including HIPAA obligations and PCI DSS considerations for payment-related data.

Security requirements are applied commensurate with this classification, including:

Endpoint safeguards (e.g., device encryption, access controls, and authentication) for laptops and mobile devices used by staff working from any of Callitech's offices or remotely, so that assets carried or accessed off-site remain protected against loss or unauthorized access.Access controls and monitoring for cloud-hosted infrastructure, identity systems, and communications platforms underpinning telephone answering and AI-based answering services, reflecting their status as mission-critical.Physical security measures at office locations to reduce risk of theft, loss, or damage to on-premises hardware.Contractual and technical safeguards addressing reliance on third-party telecom and cloud providers that host or process sensitive data or are operationally critical.

Asset owners are responsible for confirming that the appropriate controls are applied to assets under their ownership and for flagging assets that fall outside current security requirements. Security controls and their continued effectiveness are reviewed periodically and updated as the organization's environment, threats, and business needs evolve (e.g., as AI-based answering systems scale).

## Evidence Request

If using a dedicated asset module: Provide a generated report showing asset details, classification, and applied security controls, including logs demonstrating regular review.

If using another method: Submit documentation showing how assets are classified by sensitivity/criticality and the corresponding security measures applied, including measures covering devices and data accessed off-site, with evidence of periodic review.
