Found by Steve on staging, 2026-09-06: treatment plans cannot be edited or reassigned.

Once a treatment plan is created, the risk page offers exactly two things: **Close** and **Delete**. Everything else is frozen at the moment of creation —

- the **description / rationale** — a typo or a change of plan cannot be corrected;
- the **due date** — when a date slips, it cannot be moved;
- the **type** — a plan cannot go from mitigate to accept as the picture changes;
- the **status** — only open → done. `in_progress` is a real status the queue understands and the UI can never set;
- the **owner** — see below.

**So the only way to change anything is to delete the plan and write a new one.** On a governance record that is the wrong instrument: the trail then reads as a plan destroyed and a different one raised, when what happened was a date moving.

**The API has always allowed all of it** — type, description, owner, due date and status are all updatable. This is entirely a missing UI.

## The owner is the sharpest edge

At creation the owner is a switch: **Assign to me**, or nobody. Not a picker, and there is no way to change it afterwards at all.

Worse, **the owner is never displayed** — the treatments card shows type, description, status and due date, and no owner at all. Yet treatment plans are one of the Actions sources: the queue lists each open plan **with its owner**, and the reminder mail chases that person by name. So the field that drives the chasing is one the risk page will neither show you nor let you set.

## What changes

- A treatment plan can be **edited** — type, description, due date, status and owner — from the risk page. Not a re-create: an edit, so the audit trail says what actually happened.
- The card **shows the owner**, since that is who the queue and the mail are chasing.
- The owner becomes a **picker**, per COM-575's rule: anyone holding the permission that governs writing the record. *Assign to me* stays as the one-click common case.
- **Status gains `in_progress`.** It already exists, the actions queue already treats it as open work, and there is currently no way to say a plan has been started.
- Keep Delete for a plan raised in error. Deleting should stop being how a plan gets corrected, which it is today by default.

## Related

- COM-575 — the owner picker and the permission rule this uses; that task lists risk detail as one of its call sites, and this is the same screen.
- ADR 0055 — treatment plans as an actions source, with the owner the queue chases.
