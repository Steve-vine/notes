Part of sprint 65, UI Upgrade (ADR in COM-794). These are the Access tabs that fold under **More**. The prototype lists them without drawing them, so they follow its list and detail patterns. Nothing they do changes.

## What people see

- **Users, Groups, Shared Mailboxes, Devices and Conditional Access:**
  - the kit's filter bar, with the "Showing x of y" and page-size control kept at its right-hand end;
  - quiet list rows, with type and status pills in the new tones;
  - clicking a row opens its pop-up as today.
- **Directory Roles:** list and a directory role's page (detail layout, with members in the main column and facts in the side column).
- **A shared mailbox's page:** detail layout.
- **Access Graph:** the graph itself is unchanged, drawn in the new colours for both light and dark, with its controls in the kit's style.
- **Reports:**
  - the report library as a list;
  - a report's page in detail layout;
  - the report wizard in the new look, with the same steps.
- **Access Admin:** its sections in the kit's cards. Integrations and the on-premises setup screens keep their fields and wording.
- **Pop-ups** (person, group, device, policy) take the new look: header, facts, and sections that fold.
