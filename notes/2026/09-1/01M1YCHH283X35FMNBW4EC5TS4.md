## Control Objective

Callitech Limited communicates with external parties — clients in the EU and US, data subjects whose PII or call recordings it processes, and regulators — regarding its security, availability, and confidentiality commitments, the boundaries of the services it provides, and matters affecting internal control such as security incidents and complaints.

## Implementation Guidance

Commitments and service description: Callitech's security, availability, and confidentiality commitments and the responsibilities retained by the client are set out in client-facing documentation — service agreements, data processing terms (reflecting UK/EU GDPR obligations and, where applicable to a specific engagement, HIPAA or PCI DSS flow-down terms), and a published privacy notice. This documentation describes, at a level appropriate for external readers, the scope of the telephone answering, live chat, CRM integration, and AI-based answering services, and the EU and US hosting locations used.Client onboarding and change communication: Security-relevant configuration responsibilities (e.g., permissions, integration credentials, data-handling settings) are communicated to client administrators at onboarding, supported by the installation and user guides. Material changes to the services, hosting arrangements, or subprocessors that affect client obligations or data handling are communicated to affected clients in advance where practicable.Inbound external reporting channel: A published contact route (e.g., a monitored security/privacy mailbox and the service desk) is maintained so that clients, data subjects, vendors, and other third parties can report suspected vulnerabilities, security concerns, privacy complaints, or data subject requests. Inbound reports are logged by the service desk, acknowledged, routed to the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity or the privacy contact as appropriate, and tracked to resolution; those indicating a security or privacy event are entered into the incident response process.Incident and breach notification: Where a confirmed security or privacy incident affects client data, PII, call recordings, or CRM data, Callitech notifies affected clients in line with contractual timeframes and notifies the relevant supervisory authority and, where required, affected data subjects within the timeframes set by UK/EU GDPR; for engagements with HIPAA obligations, notification is made to the client as covered entity so that its own notification duties can be met. Notifications describe the nature of the incident, data affected, and remedial actions taken.Ownership and records: External communications of this type are approved by the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity with executive management involvement for incident or regulatory notifications, and copies of notifications and complaint records are retained.

## Evidence Request

Published privacy notice and sample client agreement / data processing terms showing security and confidentiality commitments.Evidence of the published external reporting contact and a sample log of inbound external security/privacy enquiries with resolution.Sample client change notification, and any incident/breach notification correspondence with clients, data subjects, or a supervisory authority (or a documented template where none has occurred).
