## Control Objective

Management performs formal risk assessments to systematically evaluate internal and external risks associated with Callitech Limited's business activities, including its telephone answering, live chat, and AI-based answering services delivered from its Wrexham (UK), Atlanta (US), and Florida (US) offices.

## Implementation Guidance

Scope of assessment

A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) identifies Callitech's critical functions — telephone answering services (the most critical), cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and communications systems — and the operational dependencies on third-party telecom and cloud providers, assessing the impact of potential disruptions (e.g., outages, natural events, cyber-attacks, telecom/cloud provider failure).A Threat Risk Assessment (TRA) analyzes threats and vulnerabilities affecting employee and customer PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content, considering the growth and scaling of the AI-based answering platform, and Callitech's regulatory obligations (UK GDPR, and — for applicable clients — HIPAA and self-certified PCI DSS).The assessment explicitly considers the potential for fraud (e.g., social engineering targeting call center or service desk staff, misuse of payment data in scope for PCI) as part of risk identification and analysis.Objectives for each assessed activity or project (e.g., a new client onboarding, a platform change) are defined with sufficient clarity before the assessment to enable meaningful identification and analysis of the risks to those objectives.

Cadence and process

Formal risk assessments are conducted at least annually and upon significant change, such as new technology adoption (e.g., AI answering system expansion), material growth, new office locations, or regulatory/client-driven requirements (new HIPAA or PCI-relevant engagements).Risk assessments are conducted and tracked using the Carbide risk assessment tool as Callitech's SOC 2 program matures; until fully adopted, supporting documentation and reports are retained for audit purposes.Risk assessment outcomes are used to develop or refine risk mitigation activities, incident response plans, and business continuity/disaster recovery plans addressing business disruption risks arising from Callitech's dependency on TAS availability and third-party telecom/cloud providers.

Insurance

Cybersecurity insurance coverage is evaluated as part of the risk mitigation strategy to address financial and operational impact from security incidents, with executive management oversight of the policy and its renewal.

External communication

Where risk assessment outcomes affect matters relevant to external parties — for example, client contractual security requirements, regulatory obligations under GDPR/HIPAA/PCI, or communications with the cybersecurity insurance provider — management communicates relevant information to these external parties as appropriate.

Specification of objectives and risk tolerance

Entity-level objectives

Executive management documents and approves Callitech Limited's objectives at a level of detail sufficient to allow risks to those objectives to be identified and analyzed. These objectives are grouped as:Operational objectives — continuity and quality of the telephone answering service (TAS, the most critical function), live chat, and AI-based answering services delivered from the Wrexham (UK), Atlanta (US), and Florida (US) offices, including the availability of the cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and communications platforms these services depend on.Compliance objectives — meeting obligations under UK GDPR (and EU data protection obligations arising from EU hosting and EU customers), and, for applicable client engagements, HIPAA and self-certified PCI DSS requirements.Reporting objectives — accurate and timely internal reporting to management on security, availability, and confidentiality performance, and accurate external reporting to clients, regulators, and the cybersecurity insurance provider.

Service commitments and system requirements

Security, availability, and confidentiality commitments made to clients (for example, contractual service levels for answering services, and commitments over the handling of employee and customer PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content) are documented, together with the internal system requirements needed to meet them. These documented commitments and requirements form the objectives against which risks are identified and analyzed in the BIA and TRA.

Risk tolerance

Management defines and documents the levels of risk it is willing to accept for each objective, including impact and likelihood rating scales and the thresholds at which a risk must be treated, escalated to executive management, or formally accepted. Risks identified in the BIA/TRA are scored against these thresholds, and acceptance decisions are recorded with the accepting owner.

Review

Objectives, service commitments, system requirements, and risk tolerance thresholds are reviewed and re-approved by executive management at least annually and upon significant change (for example, material growth, new office locations, expansion of the AI-based answering platform, or new HIPAA/PCI-relevant client engagements), so that subsequent risk assessments are performed against current objectives.

## Evidence Request

Completed BIA and TRA documentation (in Carbide or equivalent), risk treatment/mitigation plans, records of fraud considerations within the assessment, the current cybersecurity insurance policy, and records of relevant external communications tied to risk assessment outcomes.

Additional evidence

Documented and approved statement of entity objectives, service commitments and system requirements, the risk rating/tolerance scale in use, and records of management approval and annual review.
