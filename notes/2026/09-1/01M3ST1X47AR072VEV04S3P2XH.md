Part of sprint 65, UI Upgrade (ADR in COM-794). The prototype's control page, built on the kit's detail layout. Today the page is one long stack. It becomes three tabs, plus a side card showing how the current company stands.

## What people see

- **Header:**
  - the ref as a violet badge, "was ACU.1" where it had an older ref, Active or Disabled, the tier, and the domain as a link;
  - the control's full title;
  - **Edit** (authors) and a **⋯** menu for the rest of today's actions.
- **Tabs:** **Overview**, **Gaps** (with a count) and **History** (with a count).
- **Overview:**
  - *What this means*, in slightly larger text;
  - two cards side by side: *What good looks like* and *Evidence to collect*;
  - **Framework mappings**, "n requirements across m frameworks": one row per framework, with each requirement's ref and name linking to it;
  - two cards: **Linked content** (each document with its type) and **Decisions** (with **Link** for those allowed, as today).
- **Gaps:** the gaps raised against this control for the current company, each with who raised it and when, its status, and **Raise gap**.
- **History:** a timeline of this company's assessments of the control. Each entry shows version, status, maturity, who and when, newest first.
- **The side card** (sticky), headed with the company name and the assessment version:
  - the status in large type, and the maturity bars with the level's name and definition;
  - Owner, Evidence ("2 links · 1 file"), Reviewed, and Next due (with the cadence, e.g. "12-month");
  - **Assess →**, which opens this control in the Assessments queue.
- **An Open gaps card** under it when there are any: "3 open gaps", "Oldest raised 25 Sep 2026 · 4 days ago" and **View**, which goes to the Gaps tab.
- **Narrow screens:** the side cards drop below the tabs.

## Notes (technical)

- **Where.** `pages/ControlDetailPage.tsx` on the kit's detail layout, with the tab in the URL (`useTabParam`). The trail step label is unchanged (`ref · title`).
- **Guidance.** The kit's guidance renderer.
- **Framework mappings.** Keep today's data and links.
- **Gaps.** `ControlGapsCard` becomes the Gaps tab body, and the open-gaps summary card reads the same query.
- **History.** Use the existing revisions route (`/assessments/{id}/revisions`, as the panel's version list does). Render the kit's timeline.
- **Side card:**
  - Evidence counts: links from `evidence_links`, files from assessment attachments.
  - Next due from the review cadence settings (`review_cadence_months`). It isn't fixed at 12.
- **Assess →** navigates to `/assessments/<ref>` (the focus view) as a trail push.
- **Retire** the stacked layout. Empty this page's entry from the page-header ratchet.
- **Tests.** Tabs in the URL, the history timeline order, the side card with and without an assessment, and Assess → navigating.

**Done when:** on staging, a control's page reads as the prototype. History shows this company's past assessments, and Assess → opens it in the queue.
