## Control Objective

Callitech Limited maintains a unified, documented process enabling data subjects (employees, customers, and end users of client telephone-answering and live-chat interactions) to access, review, correct, or request deletion of their personal information, and to obtain an accounting of information held and disclosed about them, in compliance with UK GDPR (for the Wrexham office and EU/UK data subjects) and applicable US privacy laws (for the Atlanta and Florida offices).

## Implementation Guidance

The Service Desk acts as the single intake point for data subject requests (access, correction, deletion, objection) submitted via email or the published privacy contact.Identity of the requester is verified against HR or CRM records before any personal information is disclosed or acted upon.Requests are logged in a tracker recording date received, type of request, verification status, and resolution date, and are tracked against the applicable regulatory deadline (e.g., 30 days under UK GDPR).Where access is fulfilled, the individual is provided a copy of the personal information held about them, including, where requested, an accounting of the categories of information held and any third parties to whom it has been disclosed (e.g., telecom, cloud, or CRM sub-processors).A record of authorized disclosures to third parties (sub-processors, telecom/cloud providers, client systems) is maintained, distinct from records of unauthorized disclosures handled under the breach process.Where a request is denied (e.g., legal exemption applies), the individual is informed in writing of the denial and the reason.Access to the request tracker and underlying PII is restricted to authorized Service Desk and IT/Cybersecurity personnel.

## Evidence Request

Data subject request tracker showing intake date, verification, disclosure log reference, and resolution date.Sample request records demonstrating identity verification and final communication, including at least one accounting-of-disclosures response.Log of authorized third-party disclosures (sub-processors, telecom/cloud providers).Sample denial communication citing the applicable legal basis.Access control listing for the request tracker and PII systems.

Ownership

Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, with day-to-day intake handled by the Service Desk.
