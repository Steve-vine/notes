Smoke finding, 2026-09-11: Owner and Additional owners on both modals list only people who already have a Compass account (`/api/v1/users/directory`). Most asset owners do not, so the picker cannot name them — the register's most important field is unusable for the people it is meant to record. ADR 0072 §8 said "picked from the directory"; the implementation picked from users.

**Decision (the ADR 0046 admin-created-account path, not JIT)**
* Both pickers search the **directory mirror** (`directory_users`, active accounts, name · job title · UPN; server-side search like the portal's `/portal/inventory/directory/users`). Same list for internal modals, the portal edit form (additional owners), and the CSV importer's owner column (email/UPN → mirrored user).
* Saving an owner who has no Compass account **creates the account at save**: `users` row with `entra_object_id` from the mirror, email = UPN, display name, no password, `asset_owner` as the only role (the existing `sync_asset_owner_roles`). This is the account shape ADR 0046 §… already defines for admin-created SSO users, so their first sign-in matches on `entra_object_id` and lands them on the portal's Inventory tab. No tokenized side door; ADR 0040's same-login rule stands.
* The warn-at-save from ADR 0047 §2 is retired for assets: there is nothing to warn about once the account exists. Recert schedule owners on a *container* schedule prefill from these owners and therefore always resolve.
* The account stays when the person is later un-owned (roles reconcile to none; the row is a harmless SSO-linked user with no role). Deactivating a mirrored user marks the owner field with the existing "vanished" treatment; the asset lists surface *owner has left* (feeds the review-due logic, not blocking).
* Owner display everywhere (lists, detail, portal, CSV export, recert) shows the mirrored name and job title, whether or not the account has been used.
* ADR 0072 §8 amended to say this.

Tests: pick an unprovisioned mirrored user → a `users` row appears with the object id and only `asset_owner`; the same person picked twice creates one row; SSO sign-in as that object id resolves to it; the importer resolves a UPN to a new account the same way.

**Acceptance**: any active person in the mirror can be chosen as owner or additional owner of either asset kind, from the internal modals, the portal and CSV; they can sign in and see the asset without an admin touching Users.
