Found by Steve on staging after sprint 56 deployed (COM-639). On the Compliance and Frameworks charts the y-axis ticks and tooltip values read `50%%`.

Cause: the charts pass both `unit="%"` and a `valueFormatter` that already appends `%`; Mantine's chart appends the unit to the formatted value, so it lands twice. Fix forward: drop `unit` where the formatter carries it (the gap, maturity and risk charts have no unit and are unaffected).
