## Control Objective

Callitech Limited enables data subjects to request correction or updating of their personal information held in CRM systems, employee records, or associated with call-handling services, ensuring the information remains accurate and complete for its intended purpose.

## Implementation Guidance

Correction requests are submitted via the Service Desk or privacy contact and logged with date received and requested change.Requestor identity is verified before any correction is made, consistent with the access-rights process.Corrections are applied in the relevant system (CRM, HR system) and, where the information has been shared with a third party (e.g., client CRM, telecom provider), the correction is communicated to that party where feasible.Where a correction request is denied, the individual is informed of the denial and reason.Requests, decisions, and any denials are tracked to resolution and retained per Callitech's record retention practices.

Entity-initiated data quality assurance

In addition to corrections triggered by data subject requests, Callitech Limited maintains the accuracy, currency, completeness, and relevance of personal information on its own initiative:

Validation at collection: Intake forms and CRM/HR data entry fields used to capture personal information (contact details, employee records, caller identifiers) apply format and mandatory-field validation, and records are reviewed for completeness at the point of creation by the entering CRM or HR administrator.Periodic accuracy and relevance review: At least annually, CRM and HR system owners review the personal information held in their systems for records that are stale, duplicated, incomplete, or no longer relevant to the purpose for which they were collected. Stale or duplicate records are corrected, merged, or flagged for deletion under Callitech's record retention practices; fields no longer needed for the stated purpose are discontinued.Third-party sourced data: Where personal information is received from a client or other third party (for example, contact data supplied for call-handling services) or from a telecom provider, Callitech records the source and relies on the provider for initial accuracy, but escalates identified inaccuracies (such as failed or misdirected contacts) back to the supplying party for correction and records the escalation.In-process accuracy signals: Inaccuracies identified during normal operations — bounced communications, failed call routing, returned correspondence, or client-reported data errors — are logged with the Service Desk and routed to the relevant CRM or HR administrator for correction using the same workflow as data subject correction requests.Reviews, escalations, and resulting corrections are documented and retained so that ongoing data quality activity can be demonstrated.
