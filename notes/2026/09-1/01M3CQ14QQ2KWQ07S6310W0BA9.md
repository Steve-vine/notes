Follow-up to COM-748, from Steve's review on staging (the screenshot shows the COM-748 tree).

## What changes

- **Rename:** the Read tab's **Linked** section becomes **Posture**.
- **Box:** it sits in its own bordered box, like the **Details** box at the top of the Read tab.
- **Contents:** the section shows only:
  ```
  Controls
    Gaps
  Decisions
  ```
  - **Controls:** the controls linked to this document.
  - **Gaps:** under each control, the gaps against it, for the selected company. Only open gaps are shown, as before.
  - **Decisions:** the decisions linked to this document only.
- **Risks are removed completely:** neither the risks under a gap nor the risks a control mitigates are shown. The decisions under a control or a risk go too.
- **No status pills:** the status on the far right of each row is removed.

Unchanged from COM-748: every row opens its own page; gaps follow the company picker; a reader who can't view posture doesn't get gaps; empty headings are hidden.
