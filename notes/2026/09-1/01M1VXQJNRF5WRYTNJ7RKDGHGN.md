The way in. A light bulb in the top bar, and the form it opens.

## The button

`AppLayout.tsx`, in the right-hand `Group`, **between the search form and `<ThemeToggle />`** — the order asked for. An `ActionIcon variant="subtle"` with `IconBulb` at `size={20}`, matching `NotificationBell` exactly, and `aria-label="Issues and improvements"` so it is reachable and testable.

`AppLayout` only. `PortalLayout` does not get one (ADR 0068) — vendor contacts and recertifiers are outside people.

Unlike every nav item, this is **not gated on a permission**. Anyone signed in to the internal app can tell us what is wrong with it.

## The dialog

`suggestions/SuggestionsModal.tsx`, a Mantine `Modal` titled **Issues and Improvements**, size `lg`.

* **Title** — `TextInput`, required.
* **Description** — `Textarea`, required, `autosize` with `minRows={5}` so a long one grows rather than scrolling in a stamp.
* **Save** and **Cancel** at the bottom, Save on the right and disabled until both fields have something in them.
* A **list icon** (`IconList`) as an `ActionIcon` in the **top right corner of the dialog**, beside the close X. It switches the dialog to the list — COM-601 builds what it switches to; here it can be present and inert behind the same component's view state, so the two tasks do not fight over the same file's layout.

Save posts, closes, and shows the usual success notification. Nothing navigates: the dialog opens on whatever screen you are on and leaves you there, which is the whole point of it being in the top bar.

## Unsaved text

This dialog is local state, not a route, so the app-wide unsaved-changes guard (COM-543) never sees it — the guard only fires on navigation. Cancel, X and Escape with text typed must therefore ask on their own, using the wording COM-595 settled on: *"You have changes you haven't saved. Leaving now loses them."* Anything less and the light bulb becomes the one place in Compass where typing is silently thrown away.
