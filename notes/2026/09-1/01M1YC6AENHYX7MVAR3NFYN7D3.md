## Control Objective

A structured workforce competency assessment process is implemented and maintained to evaluate employee skills, knowledge, and performance, ensuring alignment with Callitech Limited's organizational goals and ongoing improvement.

## Implementation Guidance

Skills gap analysis – Management defines the skills required for roles across service delivery (telephone answering, live chat), Infrastructure/DevOps/Cybersecurity, and support functions, and periodically assesses current skill levels against these requirements, including skills relevant to the company's growing AI-based answering systems. Identified gaps inform training priorities, upskilling, or recruitment decisions.

Performance reviews – Employees receive periodic performance reviews (e.g., annually) combining goal achievement with qualitative feedback on role-specific and behavioural expectations. Review outcomes, including development plans, are documented and retained, and managers are expected to provide constructive, consistent feedback.

The assessment process is reviewed periodically for fairness and alignment with organizational priorities and evolving business needs.
