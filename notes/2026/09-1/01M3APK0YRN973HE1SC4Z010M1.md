Part 2 of 2, stacked on COM-747, which adds the Links tab and lets a document be linked to controls.

Once a document is linked to controls, a reader should see what hangs off them: where the company falls short of those controls (gaps), and what could happen as a result (risks). The reader shouldn't have to go looking. Everything shown is read-only and links back to its own page.

## What changes

At the bottom of the **Read** tab, above **Review record**, a **Linked** section shows a tree:

```
Controls
- ACC.4 Privileged access is reviewed          → the control's page
  Gaps
  - GAP-12 Quarterly review not evidenced      → the gap
    Risks
    - RSK-7 Stale admin rights are misused     → the risk
Decisions
- DEC-3 We accept a 90-day review cycle        → the decision
```

- **Controls:** every control linked to the document (COM-747), shown by ref and title.
- **Gaps (under each control):** the control's gaps for the **company currently selected**. Gaps belong to a company, so they follow the company picker, the same way the control's own page does. Only **open** gaps appear (new, in progress, on hold, under review); complete and cancelled gaps are left out.
- **Risks (under each gap):** the risks linked to that gap. A risk linked to two gaps appears under both.
- **Decisions:** the decisions linked to the document itself, shown as they were before COM-747 moved the picker, but read-only.
- **No company selected:** the controls and decisions still show, with a line in place of the gaps: *"Select a company to see its gaps and risks."*
- **Permissions:** someone who can't view posture doesn't see the gap level. Someone who can't view risks doesn't see the risk level. The rest of the tree stays.
- **Empty levels:** a control with no open gaps says so in one quiet line rather than showing an empty heading. A document with nothing linked shows *"Nothing is linked to this document."*, plus a pointer to the Links tab for people who can link.

## Done when

- A document linked to a control that has an open gap linked to a risk shows all three, each a working link.
- Switching company changes the gaps and risks, not the controls or decisions.
- Closed gaps don't appear.
- A reader without posture or risk rights sees the tree cut at the right level, and the server enforces this too, not only the screen.
- The section follows *Screen conventions* (`brief/information-architecture.md`).

## Notes

- Gaps already carry `core_control_id` and `company_id` (denormalised at raise-time), and risks link to gaps through `risk_gap_links`, which requires the same company. The tree needs no new data.
- Prefer **one read endpoint**, e.g. `GET /api/v1/content/{slug}/links?company_id=…`, that returns the nested tree trimmed by permission on the server. Building it from per-control and per-gap calls would be N+1 on the page. This makes it an `api/v1` change: regenerate `schema.d.ts` and run the drift check.
- Page tests: the new sub-resource route needs stubbing (see the prefix-stub gotcha), and the company-scoped fetch must be awaited.
