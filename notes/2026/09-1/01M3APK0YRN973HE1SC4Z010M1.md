Part 2 of 2, stacked on COM-747, which adds the Links tab and lets a document be linked to controls.

Once a document is linked to controls, a reader should see what hangs off them: where the company falls short of those controls (gaps), what could happen (risks), and what has been decided about each (decisions). The reader shouldn't have to go looking. Everything shown is read-only and links back to its own page.

## What changes

At the bottom of the **Read** tab, above **Review record**, a **Linked** section shows a tree. **Every gap, risk and decision sits under the thing it's linked to**, so where it came from is plain from its position (decided 2026-09-24):

```
Controls
- ACC.4 Privileged access is reviewed          → the control's page
  Gaps
  - GAP-12 Quarterly review not evidenced      → the gap
    Risks
    - RSK-7 Stale admin rights are misused     → linked to GAP-12
      Decisions
      - DEC-8 We accept RSK-7 until Q1         → linked to RSK-7
  Risks
  - RSK-9 Excess privilege goes unnoticed      → linked to ACC.4 (the control mitigates it)
    Decisions
    - DEC-9 Treat RSK-9 with PAM tooling       → linked to RSK-9
  Decisions
  - DEC-5 Break-glass accounts are exempt      → linked to ACC.4
Decisions
- DEC-3 We accept a 90-day review cycle        → linked to this document
```

- **Controls:** every control linked to the document (COM-747), shown by ref and title.
- **Gaps (under a control):** the control's gaps for the **company currently selected**. Gaps belong to a company, so they follow the company picker, the same way the control's own page does. Only **open** gaps appear (new, in progress, on hold, under review); complete and cancelled gaps are left out.
- **Risks under a gap:** the risks linked to that gap, for the selected company.
- **Risks under a control:** the risks linked directly to the control (the risks it mitigates), for the selected company.
- **Decisions under a risk:** the decisions linked to that risk, wherever the risk appears (under a gap or directly under a control).
- **Decisions under a control:** the decisions linked to that control.
- **Decisions at the top level:** the decisions linked to the document itself, headed so it's clear they belong to the document, not to a control or risk.
- **Repeats are intended.** A risk linked to two gaps, or to a control and one of its gaps, appears in each place, carrying its decisions with it. A decision linked to more than one item in the tree appears under each. Each entry says something true about where the item attaches.
- **No company selected:** controls, their decisions and the document's decisions still show. In place of the gaps and risks (and so the risks' decisions) there is one line: *"Select a company to see its gaps and risks."*
- **Permissions:** someone who can't view posture doesn't see gaps. Someone who can't view risks doesn't see either kind of risk, or the decisions hung under them. The rest of the tree stays.
- **Empty levels:** an empty heading is left out. A control with nothing under it shows just the control. A document with nothing linked shows *"Nothing is linked to this document."*, plus a pointer to the Links tab for people who can link.
- **Depth:** the deepest branch is control → gap → risk → decision. Indentation and headings must keep each level readable on a narrow screen; the phone-width layout is part of done.

## Done when

- A document linked to a control that has an open gap linked to a risk, with a decision on that risk, shows all four, nested, each a working link.
- A risk linked directly to the control shows under the control's own **Risks**, not under a gap, with its decisions beneath it.
- A decision linked to the control shows under that control. A decision linked to the document shows in the top-level **Decisions**.
- Switching company changes the gaps and both kinds of risk (with their decisions), not the controls, the controls' decisions or the document's decisions.
- Closed gaps don't appear.
- A reader without posture or risk rights sees the tree cut at the right level, and the server enforces this too, not only the screen.
- The section follows *Screen conventions* (`brief/information-architecture.md`) and holds up at phone width.

## Notes

- All of the links already exist; the tree needs no new data:
  - gaps carry `core_control_id` and `company_id` (denormalised at raise-time)
  - risks link to gaps through `risk_gap_links` (same company enforced) and to controls through `risk_control_links`; risks are per-company, so filter both joins by the selected company
  - decisions link to controls, risks and documents through `decision_links` (`target_type` `core_control` / `risk` / `content_item`), and aren't company-scoped.
- Prefer **one read endpoint**, e.g. `GET /api/v1/content/{slug}/links?company_id=…`, that returns the nested tree trimmed by permission on the server. Building it from per-control, per-gap and per-risk calls would be N+1 on the page. Fetch each link table once for the whole set of ids and assemble the tree in memory. This makes it an `api/v1` change: regenerate `schema.d.ts` and run the drift check.
- Page tests: the new sub-resource route needs stubbing (see the prefix-stub gotcha), and the company-scoped fetch must be awaited.
