## Control Objective

Callitech Limited defines retention periods and secure disposal procedures for personal information and confidential data—including call recordings, CRM data, customer content, and employee records—across its Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida offices, and for physical/logical assets (workstations, servers, storage media) once they are decommissioned.

## Implementation Guidance

Retention periods for call recordings and CRM data are set based on client contractual requirements, applicable law (e.g., UK GDPR storage limitation principle), and operational need, and are reviewed periodically. When retention periods expire, or when physical or logical assets (laptops, servers, storage media) used to process PII or confidential information are decommissioned or repurposed, data is securely deleted or media is destroyed such that recovery is no longer possible before logical or physical access protections are removed.

## Evidence Request

Documented retention schedule for call recordings, CRM data, and employee records.Disposal/destruction procedure for physical assets and storage media.Sample record of a decommissioned asset showing data wipe/destruction prior to disposal or reuse.Evidence of assigned responsibility for retention and disposal activities.
