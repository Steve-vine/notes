Asset owners are mostly not Inventory admins. They reach their assets in the **Compass Portal** (ADR 0047 §6/§7 pattern) without the internal app (ADR 0072).

* **Role**: a portal-only `asset_owner` role, **auto-granted** when someone becomes an owner or additional owner of a container or data asset, if they hold nothing broader (the `recertifier` precedent). Removed from every asset → the grant is withdrawn.
* **Portal ▸ Inventory** tab: "My assets" — the containers and data assets the signed-in user owns, across companies, with ref, name, classification/criticality, status, last verified and an **overdue review** marker.
* Asset page in the portal: the same record sections as the internal detail, **editable** for the fields the owner is accountable for — description, aliases, hosting, criticality + RTO/RPO, access methods, support status, dependencies; on a data asset the data types, subjects, GDPR fields, retention, transfers, container mapping; the manual holder list on a container. **Edits apply immediately and are audited** (not the ADR 0054 owner-approves pattern). **Reserved for internal users**: owner/additional owners, status transitions, the links to risks/decisions/controls, Entra group grants.
* **Confirm accurate**: one button that stamps `last_verified_at` and writes an audit row — the A.5.9 maintenance evidence. Also available on the internal detail.
* Served by a narrow, ownership-scoped `/api/v1/portal/inventory/*` router (ADR 0040 §3): never by widening the internal API. Internal users who are owners reach the same tab.

**Acceptance**: a person with no internal role, made owner of a container, signs in, sees only that container in the portal, edits its description (audit trail shows them), cannot change its owner or status, and Confirm accurate updates last verified.
