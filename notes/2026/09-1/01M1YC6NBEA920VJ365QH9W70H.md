## Control Objective

A defined offboarding process ensures the secure and consistent removal of access, recovery of company assets, and compliance with legal requirements when personnel leave Callitech Limited.

## Implementation Guidance

As part of the leaver process, the service desk/IT function revokes access to systems, networks, and premises (across the UK and US offices) upon termination, coordinated with HR notification of the departure date. Company-issued assets (e.g., laptops, headsets, access badges) are recovered. HR reviews confidentiality and any post-employment obligations with the departing employee and conducts an exit interview where practicable to capture feedback. Offboarding actions are recorded via a checklist, and records are retained securely for audit purposes.
