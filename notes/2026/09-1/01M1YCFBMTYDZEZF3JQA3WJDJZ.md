## Control Objective

A comprehensive testing process is established to evaluate the security and functionality of Callitech Limited's telephone answering, live chat, CRM integration, and AI-based answering systems prior to production release.

## Implementation Guidance

Changes are exercised through the development, staging, and UAT environments before being promoted to production, with functional testing performed to confirm expected behavior.Security-relevant testing, such as vulnerability identification and review of authentication, authorization, and data validation logic, is performed on systems and applications that handle PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content.Given the rapid growth of the AI-based answering product line, testing also considers performance and scalability under increased load as part of pre-release validation.Findings from testing are documented and tracked through to remediation before release, with retesting performed to confirm fixes.Testing procedures are reviewed periodically and adjusted as the platform, its integrations, and its user base evolve.

## Evidence Request

Testing plan or procedure documentation covering functional and security testing.Examples of test case documentation and results for recent releases.Records of identified issues, remediation actions, and retest confirmation.Evidence that testing was performed in the staging/UAT environments prior to production deployment.
