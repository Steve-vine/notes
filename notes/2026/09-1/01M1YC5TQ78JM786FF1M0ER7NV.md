## Control Objective

Effective communication practices are established to ensure clear, timely, and reliable information sharing across Callitech Limited's Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida offices.

## Implementation Guidance

Management uses appropriate channels (e.g., email, team messaging tools, meetings, and internal announcements) suited to the urgency and audience of the message, to communicate policy changes, security alerts, operational updates, and organizational objectives to employees across all sites. Employees are given clear guidance on how to report issues or security incidents, including escalation paths appropriate to a 24/7 telephone answering and live chat operation. Communication practices support cross-site coordination between UK and US operations and are reviewed periodically to ensure they remain effective as the organization grows, including the expansion of AI-based answering services.
