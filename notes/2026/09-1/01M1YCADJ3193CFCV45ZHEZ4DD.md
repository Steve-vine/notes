## Control Objective

Send notifications to relevant parties to communicate any collection, modification, or denial of personal information requests.

## Implementation Guidance

Define notification triggers for corrections, amendments, denials, and downstream repository updates.Use approved templates that identify the change, date, recipient, and communication channel.Maintain records of approvals, transmissions, and confirmations.Ensure repository or third-party follow-up is tracked to completion where required.

## Evidence Request

Provide an example notification template used for communicating the collection or correction of PII. Include the date, recipient, content of the message (e.g., what was corrected or collected), and the communication channel used. Attach records that show the approval or review process undertaken before sending the notification.
