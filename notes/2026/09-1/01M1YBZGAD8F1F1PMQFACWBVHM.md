## Control Objective

Callitech Limited tests its Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCDR) plan at least annually to confirm preparedness for disruptive events affecting telephone answering, live chat, and AI-based answering services across its Wrexham (UK) and Atlanta/Florida (US) locations.

## Implementation Guidance

Testing exercises recovery procedures for the systems and services identified as critical in the BCDR Plan — the telephone answering platform, cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and communications systems — and, where feasible, simulates loss of a critical third-party dependency such as a telecom or cloud provider. Testing verifies that personnel understand their roles and responsibilities in a disruption, that recovery time objectives for critical services are realistic, and that the plan's procedures function as documented. Results, including any gaps identified, are recorded, and corrective actions are tracked to completion, with the BCDR Plan updated accordingly. Given the company's growth in AI-based answering systems, testing scope is reviewed each cycle to confirm it still reflects the current critical infrastructure and third-party dependencies.
