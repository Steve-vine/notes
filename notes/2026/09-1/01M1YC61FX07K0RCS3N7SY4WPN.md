## Control Objective

Roles, responsibilities, and authority levels are defined to ensure that tasks are developed and decisions are made by the appropriate individuals or groups within Callitech Limited.

## Implementation Guidance

Management documents roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authority for key functions, including Infrastructure and Cybersecurity (encompassing service desk, DevOps, and security, led by the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity), HR, and operational leadership for telephone answering and live chat service delivery across the UK and US offices. Role descriptions and authority levels clarify who is responsible for approving access, system changes, vendor decisions, and incident response actions, reducing the risk of overlapping or conflicting duties. These definitions are communicated to relevant personnel and reviewed periodically by management to reflect organizational changes, such as growth in AI-based answering services or restructuring.
