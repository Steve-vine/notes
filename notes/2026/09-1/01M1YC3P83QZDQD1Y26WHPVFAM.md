## Control Objective

Penetration testing and, where appropriate, red team exercises are performed against Callitech Limited's environment, with findings integrated into structured vulnerability management, exception handling, and transparent reporting to senior leadership, to identify and assess security risks that scanning alone may not surface.

## Implementation Guidance

Periodic penetration tests are conducted against Callitech's externally facing and business-critical systems — including infrastructure supporting telephone answering services, live chat, and AI-based answering systems hosted across the UK and US — to simulate real-world attack scenarios and validate the effectiveness of existing controls. Testing may be performed by qualified internal staff or an engaged third party, scoped to align with the organization's priorities (e.g., internet-facing services, identity, and communications platforms).

Findings from each test are documented, including exploited scenarios, affected systems, and the effectiveness of detection/response at the time of the test. Results are fed into the same centralized vulnerability tracking used for scan-based findings, prioritized by severity and business impact, and tracked through to remediation with follow-up verification (e.g., retesting) where warranted.

Where a finding cannot be remediated immediately, a formal exception is raised with documented justification, risk assessment, any compensating controls, an accountable owner, and a review/expiration date, consistent with the organization's broader exception management process.

Summary results — critical findings, remediation status, and residual risk — are reported to senior leadership as part of the organization's overall vulnerability and security risk reporting, enabling leadership to weigh in on prioritization and resourcing for identified gaps.

## Evidence Request

Penetration test / red team engagement reports, including scope, findings, and severity ratings.Evidence that findings were logged into the vulnerability tracking process, with remediation owners, timelines, and status.List of any exceptions raised from testing findings, including justification, approval, and review/expiration dates.Reporting materials shared with senior leadership summarizing test results and remediation status.
