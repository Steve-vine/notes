## Control Objective

Callitech Limited publishes a privacy notice describing its practices for collecting, using, disclosing, and protecting personal information across its UK (Wrexham) and US (Atlanta, Florida) operations, covering employee data, customer CRM data, call recordings, and customer content processed in delivering telephone-answering, live-chat, and AI-based answering services.

## Implementation Guidance

The notice explains data subjects' rights (access, correction, deletion, objection), how call recordings and chat interactions are used, applicable legal bases, and third-party sharing (e.g., with telecom and cloud providers). It is made available on Callitech's website and referenced in call-recording/chat notices, and is reviewed and updated when data practices change, including as AI-based answering capabilities expand.

## Evidence Request

Current published privacy notice or link to hosted version.Version history or change log showing review dates and updates.Evidence of communication to data subjects when the notice was materially updated.
