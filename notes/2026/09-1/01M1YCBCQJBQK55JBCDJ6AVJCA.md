## Control Objective

Callitech Limited obtains and documents consent from individuals, where required, before collecting, using, or disclosing their personal information, including consent for call recording, marketing communications, and website data collection across its UK and US operations.

## Implementation Guidance

Callers and chat users are informed via a recorded announcement or on-screen notice of the purpose of data collection (e.g., call recording for quality/training purposes) and, where consent is the legal basis, are given the opportunity to decline or opt out. Marketing consent is collected and tracked separately from operational data collection, and individuals may withdraw consent at any time by contacting the Service Desk or designated privacy contact. Consent status is recorded and updated in the relevant CRM or marketing system.

Communicating consequences of choices and retention/disposal options

Where a choice is offered to a data subject, Callitech Limited also communicates, at the point the choice is presented, the consequences of exercising or declining that choice. Examples include: declining call recording may mean a call cannot proceed on a recorded line and must be handled by an alternative method; withdrawing marketing consent means the individual no longer receives service updates or promotional communications; refusing optional website or chat data collection may limit certain functionality. Where declining a choice has no consequence for the individual, this is stated as such.

Notices and privacy information provided to UK/EU and US data subjects also set out the choices available in relation to retention and disposal of their personal information — including the applicable retention periods for call recordings, chat transcripts and marketing records, the individual's ability to request earlier erasure or restriction of processing, and the consequence of such a request (for example, loss of the recording as a quality or dispute-resolution record). Requests relating to retention or disposal are routed to the Service Desk or designated privacy contact and actioned in line with the retention schedule.

Additional requirements

Every consent or opt-out mechanism states the consequence, if any, of each available choice.Privacy notices describe retention periods and the individual's options to request deletion or restriction, together with the consequences of those requests.Records of retention/disposal choices exercised by individuals are logged alongside consent records.

Communicating the consequences of declining consent

Where explicit consent is the basis for collection, Callitech Limited communicates to the individual, at or before the point of collection, both the need for consent and what will happen if consent is not given.

Call recording announcements and live-chat notices state the practical consequence of declining (for example, that the interaction cannot proceed on a recorded line, that an alternative unrecorded handling route is offered where the client service permits it, or that a specific feature such as AI-based answering cannot be used).Marketing opt-in forms state that declining means the individual will not receive marketing communications and that this does not affect delivery of contracted telephone-answering or live-chat services.Where consent is withdrawn, the individual is informed of the effect of withdrawal on the service or communications concerned at the time the withdrawal request is processed.Notice wording covering consequences is reviewed by IT/Cybersecurity leadership whenever a new consent-based collection point is introduced (e.g., new client intake scripts or AI answering configurations) and is kept consistent across UK and US operations.
