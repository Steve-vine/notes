Found by Steve on staging, 2026-09-05, on Access Control → **Validation** (the tab the unrequested-change queue lives on; reported as the Requests screen).

Opening an item gives the **Unrequested change: …** dialog, and the first thing in it is who made the change. When the actor is a service rather than a person, that name can be long — "Directory Synchronization Accounts" is the one that shows up most, and it is the reading that matters, because a change from on-prem AD is a completely different investigation from a rogue admin. The dialog is not wide enough to hold it, and the line spills.

**It needs to be about twice the width.**

## Why it spills rather than wraps

Two things, and fixing only the first leaves the bug one longer name away from coming back:

1. The dialog takes Mantine's default width (`md`), which no screen in the app has had a reason to reconsider — most dialogs hold a form, not a sentence with a pill in it.
2. The service-actor line is built as a `Group` with **`wrap="nowrap"`**, so the pill and the "service · *timestamp*" that follows it are forbidden from breaking onto a second line. With nowhere to go, they go outside the dialog.

That second one is the screen convention being contradicted: a pill shows its whole label, and where it cannot fit, *the layout gives* (`brief/information-architecture.md` → Screen conventions). Here the layout was told not to.

## What changes

- The dialog gets roughly double its width — `size="xl"` is the standard step and the nearest to what Steve asked for. Reach for an explicit pixel width only if the real actor names still do not fit at that size; check before assuming.
- The service-actor line is allowed to wrap, so a name longer than anything we have seen reflows instead of overflowing.
- **Do not touch the badge at the call site.** The no-truncate rule is a theme override (`theme.ts`) that every pill inherits; a width or an overflow set here is a fix that comes undone. COM-433/435 are the precedent.
- Same component renders twice more on the Validation tab, in the list rows, where the space is tighter still — check those while you are in there.
