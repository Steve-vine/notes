## Control Objective

Callitech Limited labels information in accordance with its classification, sensitivity, and handling requirements, as part of its data inventory and mapping process, covering employee and customer PII, CRM data, call recordings, and customer content across its UK and US operations.

## Implementation Guidance

As part of maintaining the data inventory, data owners classify datasets and systems according to defined sensitivity levels (e.g., distinguishing general business data from confidential/regulated data such as PII, HIPAA-relevant client data, and PCI-scoped data).

Datasets and the systems that store them are labelled or otherwise identifiably marked (e.g., through system/folder naming, metadata, or documented classification in the data inventory) to reflect their classification, so that personnel handling the data can apply appropriate storage, transmission, retention, and disposal practices consistent with the organization's data protection and retention procedures.

Labelling/classification conventions are documented and communicated to relevant personnel, and are reviewed as part of the periodic (at least quarterly) review of the data inventory, to confirm they remain accurate as data types, systems, or client requirements (e.g., new HIPAA-covered clients) change.
