## Control Objective

Data integrity controls are implemented to ensure accuracy, consistency, and reliability throughout the data lifecycle, protecting against unauthorized modifications, corruption, or loss.

## Implementation Guidance

These measures establish protocols and systems to ensure data is processed correctly, unaltered, and available as expected. Key measures include validation checks, error detection and correction procedures, access controls, and audit trails.

## Evidence Request

Provide policies and procedures outlining data integrity measures.Provide monitoring logs for access and modifications.Provide checksums, hashing, or reconciliation reports.
