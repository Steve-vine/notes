Found by Steve while smoke-testing, 2026-09-08.

Each row of the Gaps register shows the gap's status twice in the same cell: a coloured pill reading "Open" / "In progress" / "Closed", and next to it the picker that changes it, showing the same word. The pill adds nothing the picker does not already say, and it costs the column width.

**Change.** Remove the pill from the Gap status cell. The picker stays as it is: same options, same inline change, still disabled for readers.

**Out of scope.** The Control status column keeps its pill (it is read-only, there is no picker beside it). The gap pill on the control's own Gaps box and on the gap page are untouched — they have no picker next to them.
