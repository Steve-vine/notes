Wire the core `checkin` module into the Tauri app as `kind: desktop` (ADR 0064).

## Agreed work

- [ ] Shortly after startup (deferred a few seconds, clear of the startup update check), call `checkin::send(Desktop)` if `due`. Do it from Rust in `lib.rs` setup, using `app_config_dir()`. The frontend doesn't need to know.
- [ ] An hourly background tick that re-checks `due`. The app lives in the tray for days, so without the tick it would check in once per launch, not once per day (the same reasoning as DEV-988).
- [ ] No Settings UI and no toggle. The check-in is always on and covered by the privacy policy.
- [ ] Verify with a release build against the live Worker: exactly one row appears for today, and a relaunch the same day adds no second row.

## Notes

Nothing on the user's side may ever wait on or show the check-in. If it fails, nobody should notice.
