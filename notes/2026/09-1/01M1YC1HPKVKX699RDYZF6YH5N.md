## Control Objective

Callitech Limited implements logging and monitoring processes across its information systems and networks to detect configuration changes that introduce vulnerabilities, identify susceptibility to newly discovered vulnerabilities, and detect anomalies indicative of security events.

## Implementation Guidance

Key events are logged across critical systems, including TAS infrastructure, cloud environments (EU and US), identity systems, and communications platforms — for example, access attempts, configuration changes, and administrative activity.Logs are collected and retained in a manner that protects their integrity and availability for review and investigation.Configuration changes to critical infrastructure and cloud components are monitored so that changes introducing new vulnerabilities can be identified; vulnerability scanning results and threat intelligence are used to assess susceptibility to newly disclosed vulnerabilities.Automated alerting is used, where feasible, to flag anomalous activity indicative of malicious acts, errors, or events affecting service availability, supporting timely investigation by the Infrastructure and Cybersecurity team.Logs and alerts are reviewed on a regular basis, with findings used to adjust monitoring scope and alert thresholds as the environment (including AI-based answering systems) evolves.Logging and monitoring coverage is periodically tested to confirm it captures relevant data accurately.

## Evidence Request

Screenshots or exports of logging/monitoring dashboards showing active collection across critical systems.Sample of logs/alerts related to configuration changes and detected anomalies.Evidence of periodic review of monitoring configuration and alert thresholds.
