## Control Objective

Callitech Limited conducts risk assessments of third parties — including telecom carriers, cloud hosting providers, AI-processing/answering-system vendors, and workflow/CRM tool providers — before onboarding and periodically during the engagement, to identify and manage risks such as data breaches, non-compliance, and service disruption.

## Implementation Guidance

Third parties are assessed and risk-tiered based on the sensitivity of data accessed (PII, call recordings, CRM data, customer content) and operational criticality (telecom and cloud providers, given their role in telephone answering services, are treated as the highest tier). Assessments include:

Security evaluation: Review of the vendor's security controls relevant to the data or systems they touch — e.g., encryption of call recordings and PII in transit and at rest, access controls, and incident response capability.Regulatory compliance: Review of the vendor's ability to meet applicable obligations, including GDPR (given EU operations and customers), and, where relevant to a specific client engagement, HIPAA or PCI DSS requirements the vendor may need to support.Financial and operational stability: For vendors supporting mission-critical services (telephone answering, cloud, identity, and communications infrastructure), consideration of the vendor's financial and operational stability to gauge continuity risk.Reputation and history: Consideration of the vendor's track record, including any known security incidents or public breaches.Fraud consideration: Assessment of the potential for fraud in the vendor relationship or selection process (e.g., verifying the vendor's legitimacy and the integrity of the engagement/procurement process) is factored into the review.Risk mitigation: Where risks are identified, mitigation actions are determined — such as contractual provisions, enhanced monitoring, or contingency arrangements for critical telecom/cloud dependencies.

Assessments are performed prior to onboarding a new third party and repeated periodically (or upon material change to the relationship) for higher-tier vendors, given the current absence of a dedicated vendor management resource; this is coordinated by the service desk under the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity.

## Evidence Request

Use the Vendor module within the Carbide Platform. Evidence should demonstrate that risk assessments are completed prior to onboarding and on a periodic basis, including risk tiering, assessment findings, and any resulting mitigation actions.
