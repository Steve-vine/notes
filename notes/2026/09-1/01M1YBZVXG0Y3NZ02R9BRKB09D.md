## Control Objective

Callitech Limited defines and communicates clear incident reporting guidelines to enable prompt, coordinated responses to security incidents, and obtains commitments from third parties with access to personal information to notify Callitech of actual or suspected unauthorized disclosures.

## Implementation Guidance

Internally, the guidelines describe how staff identify and categorize a suspected incident, who to notify (service desk and the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity), what details to record, and how post-incident analysis is used to address underlying vulnerabilities, in line with the Incident Response Plan.

Externally, because Callitech engages third parties (including telecom and cloud providers, and other vendors with access to employee or customer PII, call recordings, or CRM data), vendor agreements or onboarding records obtain a commitment from such vendors to notify Callitech promptly of any actual or suspected unauthorized disclosure of personal information they hold or process. Given that vendor oversight is currently managed by the service desk using SharePoint and spreadsheets rather than a dedicated vendor management function or GRC tool, these notification commitments are tracked within the existing vendor inventory so that any vendor-reported disclosure is routed to appropriate personnel and actioned under the Incident Response Plan (see BCIM1). As Callitech matures its vendor management process, this tracking is expected to move to a more structured tool, but the underlying commitment and escalation path remain in place regardless of the tracking mechanism used.

## Evidence Request

Provide:

The documented internal incident reporting procedure, including classification and escalation criteria.Evidence that vendor/third-party agreements or onboarding records include a breach/incident notification commitment (e.g., contract clause, vendor questionnaire).The vendor inventory (e.g., SharePoint list or spreadsheet) showing tracked notification commitments.Records of any vendor-reported incidents and how they were routed to the Incident Response Plan.
