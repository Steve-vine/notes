A Word template gains a `[posture]` placeholder. In the generated PDF it becomes the same content as the **Posture** box on the document's Read tab (COM-748, COM-749).

Part 2 of 2, stacked on COM-751, which adds the data-placeholder mechanism and `[domain-controls]`.

## What it shows: exactly the Read tab's scope (decided 2026-09-25)

```
Controls
  INS.1  An acceptable use policy has been created…
    Gaps
      G-2  Close gap: INS.1 …
      G-4  INS.1: Do some stuff
  INS.2  All employees … confirm …
Decisions
  D-1  All services must support SSO going forward
```

- **Controls:** the controls linked to the document (Links tab), as ref then title.
- **Gaps:** under each control, its open gaps for the **company selected when Generate PDF is pressed**. This is the same company picker the Read tab follows. Complete and cancelled gaps are left out.
- **Decisions:** the decisions linked to the document.
- **Conditions:**
  - **No company selected:** the PDF has controls and decisions, but no gaps.
  - **A reader who can't view posture:** the PDF has controls and decisions, but no gaps. The server applies the same rule as the Read tab's data, so the PDF never shows more than the person generating it could see.
- **Consequence to know:** two people with different companies selected get different PDFs of the same document. That's intended, because the PDF follows the Read tab.
- **Layout:** plain indented lines with small bold labels (Controls, Gaps, Decisions), mirroring the Read tab. The lines are not Word headings, so they don't appear in `[contents]`. An empty label is left out. A document with nothing linked gets nothing, and the line is removed. There are no status pills and no links, because a PDF is read outside Compass.
- **Every generate path:** the document's Generate PDF, a historic version from History, and the Content list's bulk PDF all use the selected company. A historic version shows today's links and gaps, not those at the time.
- **Templates tab:** the help lists `[posture]` and says it follows the selected company.

## Done when

- A PDF with `[posture]` matches the document's Read tab for the same company: the same controls, open gaps and decisions.
- Switching company and generating again gives that company's gaps. The cached PDF from the other company is not reused.
- Without a company, and for someone without posture view, the PDF has no gaps.
- Linking or unlinking a control or decision, or raising or closing a gap, changes the next PDF.

## Notes

- PDF generation is company-agnostic today. `PdfGenerateRequest` holds only `slugs` and `version`, and the Celery tasks take only ids. Add an optional `company_id` to the request, the endpoint and the task arguments, and pass the requester's posture permission (or `None` for company when they lack it). Send it from `useGeneratePdf` using the company context.
- Reuse COM-748/749's tree query. Factor it out of `api/v1/content_links.py` into a function both the endpoint and `tasks/pdf.py` call, so the PDF and the Read tab can't drift.
- The cache key includes the company id and a digest of the tree. Bump `_RENDERER_VERSION`.
- Bulk PDF (`generate_bulk_pdf`) takes the same `company_id`.
