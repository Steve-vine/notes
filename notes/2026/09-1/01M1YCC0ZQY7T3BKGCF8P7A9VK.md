## Control Objective

A mechanism is in place to enable individuals to receive and transfer their personal data to another organization in a commonly used, machine-readable format.

## Implementation Guidance

A structured data portability process allows individuals to request and transfer their personal data securely. The process includes identity verification, secure transfer methods, and adherence to data format standards.

Provide examples or screenshots of the method used for data export requests, such as request forms or email templates.Include records that detail the data format (e.g., sample CSV or JSON structure) and logs showing retrieval, packaging, and secure transmission.Document identity verification steps and any encryption protocols used for transfers.
