## Control Objective

Callitech Limited implements retention and secure disposal procedures so that information — including employee and customer PII, CRM data, call recordings, and customer content — and the physical/logical assets that store it are retained only as long as necessary and disposed of securely, in line with security policy, client contractual requirements, and applicable regulatory obligations (including GDPR, HIPAA where applicable to certain clients, and PCI DSS self-certification scope).

## Implementation Guidance

Retention periods are defined for the major categories of data Callitech holds (e.g., employee records, CRM/customer records, call recordings, customer content), based on legal/regulatory requirements, client contractual terms, and legitimate business need, and are informed by the data inventory.

When retention periods expire, or when equipment or media (e.g., workstations, laptops, servers, removable media) used to store this data across the Wrexham, Atlanta, and Florida offices, or in cloud-hosted environments, is decommissioned, logical and physical protections over the asset are not removed until the ability to read or recover the data has been eliminated (e.g., secure wipe, encryption key destruction, or certified physical destruction as appropriate to the media type).

Disposal activity is recorded to demonstrate that decommissioned assets and expired data were disposed of securely.

Retention and disposal procedures are reviewed periodically and updated to reflect changes in regulatory requirements, client obligations, or business needs.

## Evidence Request

Documented retention schedule covering the organization's key data categoriesDisposal/decommissioning records or logs (e.g., wipe confirmations, destruction certificates) for retired assets or mediaEvidence of periodic review of retention and disposal procedures
