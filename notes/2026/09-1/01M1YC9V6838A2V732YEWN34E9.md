## Control Objective

Callitech Limited documents the legal basis for each category of personal information it processes—employee PII, customer CRM data, call recordings, and customer content generated during telephone-answering and live-chat services—consistent with UK GDPR and applicable US privacy requirements.

## Implementation Guidance

Legal bases used include performance of a contract (delivering telephone-answering/live-chat services to clients), legitimate interest (service quality, security), consent (e.g., call recording notices, marketing), and legal obligation (e.g., regulatory record-keeping, HIPAA-related obligations for applicable clients). Callitech maintains a record of processing activities identifying the purpose and legal basis for each category of PII collected, reviewed periodically by IT/Cybersecurity leadership as services (including AI-based answering) evolve.

## Evidence Request

Record of processing activities identifying purpose and legal basis for each PII category.Privacy notice and call-recording announcement language referencing the basis for processing.Evidence of periodic review of legal bases (e.g., meeting notes, updated record of processing activities).
