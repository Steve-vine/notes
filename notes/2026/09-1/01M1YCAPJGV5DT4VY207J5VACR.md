## Control Objective

Ensure personal health information is disclosed only with proper authorization and in compliance with individual consent and legal requirements.

## Implementation Guidance

Verify authorized recipients before disclosure.Document consent preferences and legal authority for disclosure.Limit disclosures to the minimum necessary amount of information.Maintain disclosure logs and evidence of recipient validation.Align disclosures with individual expressed wishes where applicable.

## Evidence Request

Provide documentation demonstrating the unified process, including:

Screenshots or templates of request submission forms (access, deletion, objection, revocation, update).A copy of the organization's privacy policy or internal procedures detailing data subject rights and request handling.Sample request records showing identity verification, processing steps, and final communications.Example acknowledgment and decision templates, including citations of legal provisions for denials.Screenshots of the self-service portal and access controls for managing data preferences.Disclosure tracking reports and consent management logs.Third-party notification records for consent revocations and data deletions.Secure deletion procedures, including handling of archived and backup data.Reports from tracking systems showing request statuses, deadline alerts, and compliance metrics.Retention policy excerpts confirming the storage duration for request and disclosure records.
