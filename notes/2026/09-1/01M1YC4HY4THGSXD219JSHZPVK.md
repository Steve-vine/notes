## Control Objective

Independent oversight and strategic direction over security, privacy, and compliance are provided by Callitech's Board of Directors, informed by the security team's day-to-day management of the program, to align decisions with organizational goals and ensure accountability.

## Implementation Guidance

As an SMB-scale organization (100+ employees) operating across Wrexham (UK), Atlanta (US), and a Florida satellite office, Callitech's security program is designed, implemented, and managed by the security function under the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, who oversees service desk, infrastructure, DevOps, and cybersecurity. This function makes the majority of day-to-day security decisions, balancing risk against the operational demands of a growing telephone answering, live chat, and AI-based answering business.

The Board of Directors provides higher-level oversight rather than operational management: it receives periodic updates on security posture, key risks (including risks associated with third-party telecom/cloud dependencies and the pace of AI system growth), compliance status (GDPR, EU Cyber Essentials, SOC 2 program progress, and client-driven HIPAA/PCI DSS obligations), and material deficiencies identified through internal reviews or audits. This ensures the Board can confirm the program is appropriately resourced and that identified risks and control deficiencies are being addressed in a timely manner by responsible parties.

As the SOC 2 program and organization mature, Callitech may formalize a Steering Committee to provide additional strategic alignment across infrastructure, DevOps, HR, and compliance stakeholders; until then, this function is fulfilled by the security leadership team reporting into the Board.
