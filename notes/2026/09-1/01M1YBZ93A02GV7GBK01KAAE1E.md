## Control Objective

Callitech Limited maintains a set of documented plans to prepare for, respond to, and recover from unexpected events that could disrupt its telephone answering (TAS), live chat, and AI-based answering services. These plans comprise a Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCDR) Plan and an Incident Response Plan (IRP), covering both the Wrexham (UK) and Atlanta/Florida (US) operations.

## Implementation Guidance

The BCDR Plan identifies mission-critical services and dependencies — telephone answering services, cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and communications platforms — together with third-party dependencies on telecom and cloud providers. It defines recovery objectives and procedures to keep essential operations running or restore them promptly following disruptions such as system failures, cyberattacks, or telecom/cloud provider outages.

The IRP, owned by the Head of Infrastructure and Cybersecurity, defines how security events are evaluated to determine whether they constitute a security incident affecting the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of systems and data — including employee and customer PII, call recordings, CRM data, and customer content (CC7.3). Where an event is confirmed as an incident, the IRP is executed to contain, remediate, and communicate the incident, including internal escalation to relevant infrastructure, cybersecurity, and service desk personnel and, where required, communication to affected clients, regulators, or law enforcement, taking into account contractual obligations to clients requiring HIPAA-aligned handling or PCI DSS considerations (CC7.4). Following containment, recovery activities are identified, developed, and implemented to restore affected systems and services to normal operation, with lessons learned fed back into the plans (CC7.5).

Both plans are reviewed and updated at least annually or following a significant incident, organizational change, or material change to critical infrastructure or third-party dependencies.
