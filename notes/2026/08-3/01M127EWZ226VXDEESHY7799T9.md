Land `decisions/0060-access-is-a-rubric-and-a-vendor-is-as-risky-as-its-worst-engagement.md` and move it to Accepted.

It decides: access becomes a fixed four-rung rubric; the vendor risk tier is a **new** rubric (not `risk_score_bands`); the tier is proposed as the highest tier any single input triggers, confirmed by a person, and rolled up to the vendor as the max of its engagements' tiers — never recomputed from rolled-up inputs. It also records, explicitly, that **no vendor `type` taxonomy is added** — what a supplier sells is a property of the questionnaire, not the register.

Amends ADR 0039 §5–6 and ADR 0042. Everything else in this sprint stacks behind it.
