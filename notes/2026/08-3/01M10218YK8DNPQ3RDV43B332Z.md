Stacks on COM-450. Part 7 of COM-446 — and not the future enhancement it started as. This is the migration: how 1,500 unattributed people become explained ones.

Compass launches with every existing membership unattributed. That is the honest state, and the alternative — mapping 1,500 users to roles before launch — designs roles at the point of least knowledge, from guesses, using the very data the exercise is trying to explain.

## What changes for the reader

**Compass tells you which business roles you are missing.** It reads what people actually hold, finds the clusters, and proposes: *eleven people hold these four groups and no role explains any of it — is that a business role?* You accept, amend or dismiss.

## Scope

**The proposals.** Read membership plus provenance (COM-447) and surface:

- **A role waiting to be written** — a cluster of people sharing groups that no role covers.
- **A role someone nearly matches** — a person holding all of a role's groups but not the role, or all but one.
- **A role worth retiring** — one nobody has held for a long time.

Start simple and honest. Exact-overlap clustering that proposes obviously-true roles beats clever clustering nobody trusts; a proposal that is wrong once is a tool people stop opening.

**Accepting a proposal.** Creates or amends the business role, assigns it to the people, and **attributes the memberships that match** — role-derived, replacing unattributed. Everything that does not match **stays unattributed**. Never auto-promote a leftover to an exception: an exception is a decision a person made about a group, and the moment the system invents them the distinction is worthless.

Assignment is a change to directory state, so it goes through the request path like everything else — this proposes and prepares, it does not write to Graph.

**The number, on the dashboard.** Explained vs unattributed membership per company, from COM-447's API. It is what makes anyone finish the migration, it moves visibly as proposals are accepted, and it is the kind of figure an auditor likes to see trending. Worth a sparkline over time rather than a bare percentage.

**Recertification drains the same pool from the other end** — a campaign already asks a group owner whether these people belong, and that answer is provenance. Check whether certifying an unattributed membership can attribute it; if it is a small change, take it here.

## Tests

Component and integration tests for the proposal engine over a fixture tenant: a clean cluster proposes a role; accepting attributes only the matching memberships; leftovers stay unattributed; a dismissed proposal does not return next run. The explained-count moving after an accept is the assertion that proves the whole loop.
