ADR 0062 §2, the authoring half. Four steps, driven entirely by the catalogue (COM-487) — the wizard knows nothing about users, groups or devices of its own.

1. **What it's about** — pick the subject.
2. **Which ones** — add conditions. The field list, the operators offered, and the shape of the value input all come from the field's declared type: a date field offers *before / after / in the last N days*, a boolean offers Yes/No, an enum offers its values. Nothing free-text that isn't a text field.
3. **What to show** — pick and order the columns, pick the sort.
4. **What it's called** — name, description, the question it answers.

**A live preview of the first rows at every step**, because the honest test of a report is whether it returns what the author expected, and finding that out after saving is how people stop trusting the tool.

Editing an existing report is the same wizard, opened on its definition. Duplicating is the same wizard, opened on a copy.

The steps should read as a sentence when they're done — the summary line the report's page shows.
