A handful of frontend tests fail under the full suite and pass every time on their own. They are timing-sensitive assertions starved of CPU by the parallel workers, not real breakages — but they cost real time, because a failure looks exactly like something the change under test broke.

Confirmed on clean `main` with nothing else applied, so this is not any one branch's doing.

## Why it is worth fixing now

**It is getting worse as the suite grows.** Adding test files anywhere increases parallel load. During COM-445 the failure count went from 1 to 5 purely because two new test files were added elsewhere in the codebase — nothing near the Portal pages. The natural reading is "my change broke five tests", and it takes a stash-and-rerun against clean `main` to prove otherwise.

Left alone it will eventually go red on CI rather than only locally, and `auto-rerun.yml` will not absorb it — that job matches infrastructure signatures, and a starved assertion is not one. It will need a human every time.

## The tests

- `src/pages/PortalRouting.test.tsx` — several cases, including "lands a portal-only user on the portal after signing in" and "bounces a portal-only user off the dashboard".
- `src/pages/PortalVendorDetailPage.test.tsx`
- `src/access/RecertPage.test.tsx` — the same class, noted previously.

## Scope

Find why these particular tests are timing-sensitive and make them not be, rather than papering over it. Likely candidates: `findBy*` assertions racing a query that resolves on its own schedule, or fake-timer / real-timer mixing. Raising timeouts would make the symptom rarer without making the tests correct, and would slow the suite for everyone — worth resisting unless the investigation says the assertion is genuinely right and only the budget is wrong.

Worth checking whether they share a cause, since three files behaving identically under load suggests one pattern rather than three coincidences.

## Definition of done

The full suite passes repeatedly under load — run it several times rather than once, since a single green run proves nothing about a flake.
