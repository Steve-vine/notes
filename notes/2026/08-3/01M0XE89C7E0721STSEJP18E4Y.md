ADR 0055 §5. Depends on COM-408 for the scoping; better after COM-409, when
there is more than review follow-ups to show.

Vendor owners and recertification reviewers are the largest group with work
they cannot see, and most of them never open the internal app. Building the
go-to place for required activity and leaving them in the portal would rebuild
the problem one screen over.

## What changes for the reader

A portal user gets an **Actions** entry showing things assigned to them, and
only those — their vendor review follow-ups, a request waiting on their owner
approval, a recertification waiting on their attestation, a question an
approver has asked them.

No module-wide rows: a portal user holds no module, so the second rule from
COM-408 has nothing to match. One rule instead of two.

## Implementation

Same `_collect`, same per-reader scoping, `mine`-only. The portal route reads
under `require_portal_read` and must **not** be a second implementation — the
whole reason the internal and portal vendor reads share `vendor_reads` is that
two implementations drift, and this is the same trap.

Frontend: an entry in `portalNav.ts` and a page that reuses the internal
Actions list component. Deep links need checking — an action's `link` points
at internal routes (`/vendors/{id}`, `/gaps`), and a portal reader needs the
portal path for the ones they can reach, and no link at all for the ones they
cannot. That is the only real work in this task.

Tests: a `vendor_user` sees their own follow-ups and no module-wide rows; a
`recertifier` sees their outstanding attestations; neither sees a gap; links
resolve to portal routes.
