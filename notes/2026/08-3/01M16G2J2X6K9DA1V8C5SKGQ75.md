A way to remove a company and its data for good, once it has been archived.

## Behaviour

- **Delete** appears only on an archived company, in Admin ▸ Companies. An active company has no delete — archive first. The default company can never be deleted.
- The confirmation states plainly that this is permanent and cannot be undone, lists what is about to go (counts of vendors, assessments, risks, decisions, access records, files), and requires the admin to **type the company's name** before the button enables.
- Admin-only.

## Why this is bigger than it looks

Company-scoped data is deliberately wired to **refuse** deletion — that is why archive exists at all (`db/mixins.py:60-71`, `models/company.py:1-6`). A real delete has to walk roughly **30 directly company-scoped tables and ~35 more reachable only through them** (~65 total), in constraint order rather than model order, because most chains are RESTRICT→RESTRICT: `companies → vendors → certifications/contacts/revisions`, and `companies → assessments → gaps → risk_gap_links ← risks`. Soft-deleted rows have to go too — `SoftDeleteMixin` entities never hard-delete today, so a purge overrides that intent.

Precedent for the shape: `core/vendor_purge.py` (12 tables for a single vendor, 6 the DB won't cascade) and the read-only pre-flight in `core/user_deletion.py`. The pre-flight is what should produce the counts shown in the confirmation.

**Four tables disagree with the model** about what happens on delete — `CompanyScopedMixin` declares RESTRICT but the migrations say otherwise: `assessment_rule_sets` (CASCADE, `0094:58`), `compliance_rules` (CASCADE, `0104:63`), `vendor_portal_branding` (CASCADE, `0100:58`), and `vendor_request_messages` (no `ondelete` at all → NO ACTION, `0086:37`). Worth reconciling as part of this rather than discovering it mid-purge.

Must **not** be touched: `report_definitions` rows with `company_id IS NULL` (built-in/global), and the tenant-wide `directory_*` mirror plus the `entra_settings` / `m365_settings` / `sso_settings` singletons.

## Two decisions to make during the work

1. **The activity log.** `activity_log` deliberately carries no FK on `company_id` so the trail outlives archival (ADR 0023; `models/activity_log.py:8-11`). A purge either leaves orphaned rows by design, or deletes the audit trail of a company that no longer exists. Take a view and record it.
2. **Files outside the database.** Evidence attachments live in object storage (`attachments.storage_key` — `risks/{id}/…`, `assessments/{id}/…`); today even a soft delete leaves the bytes. Deleting the rows without purging the objects leaves orphaned files with no pointer.

## Won't be cleaned up, and the confirmation should be honest about it
Groups and users Compass created in Entra (`tasks/access_execute.py`) stay in the tenant — while `access_changes` / `unrequested_changes`, the only record that Compass made them, go with the company. Already-sent email is gone regardless. Also guard in-flight Celery work: there is no DB outbox, so a queued `execute_access_request` could still write to Entra after the rows are gone.

## Tests
Delete refused on an active company and on the default company; refused without an exact name match; a full purge leaves no orphans across the scoped tables; global `report_definitions` and directory mirror rows survive.
