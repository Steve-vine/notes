Periodic recertification of group membership — the "Auditing" pillar, closing the loop from attest to executed change.

* **Campaign**: company-scoped, opened by a Beat task per configured cadence (per-group or per-business-role scope, quarterly default). Idempotent by dedupe key `(company, scope, period)` — the `tasks/reminders.py` shape, re-running creates nothing new.
* **Review items**: at open, **snapshot** the mirror's membership into per-member review items (ADR 0015 §4: append-only snapshots answer "what did it look like last quarter" — live mirror rows can't). Item lifecycle `pending → certified | flagged_for_removal → removed | removal_failed`, module-level transitions map.
* **Reviewers**: default from the business-role/group owner (the matrix's owner FK), reassignable. Reminders and overdue nagging through the existing engine — `Notification` rows batched into the owner's digest via `core/mail` (never a direct transport).
* **Execution**: a `flagged_for_removal` attestation is approved by a second person (maker-checker — sprint decision), then removal executes through the **same Graph execution path as JML** (one write path, one evidence trail), recording outcome on the item.
* **Campaign close**: completion stats (certified/removed/expired-unreviewed) frozen on the campaign — this record *is* the evidence for ISO A.5.18 / SOC 2 CC6.2-6.3 / CIS 5-6, and Compass's own controls track exactly these.

Campaigns + items join `_AUDITED_TABLES`. Refs: ADR 0045, 0015 §4, 0006, 0044 §4; `tasks/reminders.py`, `models/vendor_review.py` (periodic-review prior art).
