The graph already says "a group is not a resource" — a squarer box (`borderRadius: 10` vs the pill's `NODE_H`) and a violet fill, both keyed off `isGroup()` in `lib/graphLayout.ts`. Business Applications and Business Services get none of it: they draw as ordinary blue pills, indistinguishable from the workloads and hosts they are composed of. Now that all three are Collections ([ISE-677]), the canvas should say so.

**Scope**
- **Shape**: `business-application` and `business-service` draw with the same squarer box as `group` (`EntityNode` in `components/EntityGraphView.tsx`).
- **Colour**: all three move off violet onto **one shared colour**, chosen to read as obviously not-a-resource against the blue of everything else and the gray root — not a neighbouring shade of it. Constraint from ISE-228: the fill is a light shade (`colourVar(colour, 0|1)`) with black label text in both themes, so the colour has to work light; the border takes shade 5/6. `grape` or `teal` are the plausible picks — judge them on the canvas, not in the palette.
- **Collapse**: `isGroup()` becomes `isCollection()` (`type === 'group' | 'business-application' | 'business-service'`) so folding applies to all three. The existing cascade already handles the consequence — collapsing a Business Service folds its Business Applications *and* their members, two levels into one node — which is the right reading of "same behaviour", but look at it on a real graph before calling it done.
- **Rename the toggle** to **`Collapse collections`** in `components/EstateGraphPanel.tsx`. Keep the persisted key `estate-graph-collapse` as it is: it already reads generically, and changing it silently resets every operator's saved preference for no gain. The `collapseGroups` prop/state name can follow the label.
- `lib/graphLayout.test.ts` covers the collapse — extend it to a Business Application and a Business Service rather than only renaming the existing group case.

**Watch**: jsdom does no layout, so vitest will stay green through a node that renders invisibly or a shape that never changes. The shape/colour half of this is judged in the browser rig or on staging.

**Done when** an operator can tell a Collection from a resource at a glance without reading a label, and one toggle folds all three.
