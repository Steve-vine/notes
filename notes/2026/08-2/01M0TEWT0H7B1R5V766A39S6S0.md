In Gantt view when a Task timeline is selected, the task title background becomes transparent, that shouldn’t happen.

## Cause

`.rail` (the label column) is `position: sticky; left: 0` with an opaque
`background: var(--pane-bg)`, so labels ride over the bars when the chart is
scrolled horizontally. `.row.selected .rail` then replaces that background with
`rgba(var(--accent-rgb), 0.15)` — a *translucent* tint — so the bars underneath
show through the title.

## Agreed work

Paint the selection tint as an opaque colour: mix the accent into `--pane-bg`
rather than laying a translucent accent over nothing. Same for the project row
and the unscheduled list's selected entry, which use the same pattern.
