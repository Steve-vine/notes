The portal grows beyond vendors — and gets its real name.

* **Rebrand: Vendor Portal → Compass Portal.** Shell title/wordmark, nav labels, page titles; routes stay `/portal/*` (no URL churn); the `vendor_portal` role keeps its id but its display name generalises. The vendor register/request screens are unchanged — they become one section of a broader portal.
* **New Recertifications tab**: lists the signed-in owner's review assignments (open instances where they are an owner) — entity name, triggered date, their submission state, instance progress (n of m owners submitted). The email deep link lands here. Reachable by `recertifier`, `vendor_portal`+owner, and internal users who are owners — assignment-scoped like the ADR 0040 portal router: a narrow `/api/v1/portal/recertifications/*` surface, not a widened internal one.
* **Performing a review**: opening an assignment shows the instructions, the member rows and the **shared live state** — each row's current decision (certify / flagged for removal) with **attribution** (who set it, when, visible history per row). Any un-submitted owner can set or reverse any row's state; changes are immediate for everyone (poll/refetch on the house TanStack patterns is sufficient — no websockets for this).
* **Submit**: an explicit action ("Submit recertification") requiring every row to carry a decision; after submitting, that owner's view becomes **read-only** (state keeps moving if others are still working — they watch, they don't touch). No un-submit; the confirmation says so.
* Empty/complete states: nothing assigned, all submitted, instance completed (with outcome summary).

Refs: the v2 ADR (portal decision + auth model), ADR 0040 (portal architecture being extended), 0022; the trigger task (assignments this renders).
