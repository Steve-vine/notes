Recert schedules can't be configured for group owners who have never signed in: owner-defaults resolves Entra owners to Compass users, but accounts are only JIT-created at SSO sign-in (ADR 0046 amendment). With several hundred users about to join the recertification group, getting each to sign in first is unmanageable.

Provision at sync time instead: after each mirror pass (and on mapping edits), create Compass accounts (`auth_provider="entra"`, derived roles, identity from the mirror) for every non-vanished, enabled directory user who derives ≥1 role and has no account. The gate is unchanged — membership of a mapped group is the grant. An unlinked local account with the same email is left for the existing one-time link at sign-in. Also match recert owners by Entra object id first, email as fallback. Second amendment to ADR 0046.
