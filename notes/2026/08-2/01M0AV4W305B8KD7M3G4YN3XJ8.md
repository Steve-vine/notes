Smoke test finding (2026-08-18): Entra's SCIM provisioning requires **inbound** connectivity to the app, and Compass is deliberately internal-only (`compass.citops.net` → 192.168.1.5, private even on public DNS). SCIM is retired before first use; the ADR 0045 **directory mirror** — outbound, already syncing tenant users/groups/memberships every 15 min — becomes the provisioning source.

Append an amendment section to ADR 0046 (append-only convention; amendments in place per ADR 0026/0045 precedent) settling:

* **§4 superseded**: no SCIM server. Group membership comes from the directory mirror; the Entra access connection (ADR 0045 §2) becomes a prerequisite for SSO.
* **The access gate moves**: from "provisioned by SCIM" to **"member of ≥1 mapped group"**. First successful sign-in **JIT-creates** the Compass account with derived roles. JIT was rejected when SCIM was the joiner pipeline; gating it on "derives at least one role" preserves the property that mattered — nobody signs in whom nobody granted anything. A sign-in deriving zero roles is denied (existing accounts included) — still no silent access, no role-less sessions.
* **Leavers/movers**: the mirror sync pass recomputes persisted roles for all Entra users; roles dropping to empty revokes the user's sessions. Freshness rides the mirror cadence (~15 min) plus the defensive refresh at sign-in — accepted trade-off vs SCIM's push.
* **Vocabulary correction**: the third user population becomes "mirror-provisioned app users"; `scim_groups`/`scim_group_members` and the SCIM bearer token are removed (migration drops them — append-only means a new dropping migration, never editing 0068).

Refs: ADR 0046, 0045 §3 (mirror), COM-249 (retired), COM-250 (mapping table survives unchanged — it keys on the group object id, which the mirror knows).
