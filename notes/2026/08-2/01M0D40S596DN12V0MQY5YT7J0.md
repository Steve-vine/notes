The recert model gets redesigned around **schedules** and the **portal**, replacing the campaign/cadence shape of ADR 0045 §8. Record it properly: a new ADR (next free number) superseding 0045 §8 and extending ADR 0040's portal beyond vendors — including the **rebrand: Vendor Portal → Compass Portal**.

**The model (designed with Steve, 2026-08-19):**

* A **recertification schedule** is a first-class entity targeting a Group or Business Role: Name, Entity type + entity, Owner(s), Description (internal), Instructions (shown to recertifiers), Cadence (Weekly / Monthly / Quarterly / Bi-Annually / Annually) + start date, and manual **Trigger now**.
* **Owners** default from the group/role owners, with additional owners addable. Per-owner **Required** toggle + schedule-level **Minimum attestations**.
* **Completion** = all Required owners have submitted **and** total owner submissions ≥ Minimum attestations (submissions counted across required + optional owners; validate minimum ≤ owner count).
* **Trigger** → per-owner review assignments over a membership snapshot; email "Scheduled Recertification - &lt;Entity name&gt;" carrying the Instructions and a portal link.
* **Shared live state**: all owners work the same row set; any un-submitted owner may flag *or un-flag* any row, every change attributed; **submitting freezes that owner's editing** while state stays live for the rest.
* **Removals execute at completion**, not per submission, through the existing removal execution path. Decide here: multi-owner completions already carry ≥2 humans; whether a **single-owner** schedule's removals still route through a second-person approval (lean **yes** — the two-person write rule shouldn't depend on schedule configuration).
* **Recertifiers are Compass users** — a portal-only `recertifier` role (the `vendor_portal` precedent) granted automatically when someone becomes a schedule owner; owners that don't resolve to Compass users surface as a warning at schedule save (provision via Entra assignment first). No tokenized-link side door; ADR 0040's same-login rule and ADR 0046's deny-unprovisioned stand.
* **Surfaces**: Access ▸ Recertification = a **Schedules** tab (single tab) + oversight (instance progress/evidence) for access managers; **performing** reviews lives only in the portal's new **Recertifications** tab.

Supersedes the open follow-ups COM-263 (per-scope cadence) and COM-264 (campaign owners + detail view) — both cancelled; their concerns (cadence per schedule, owners resolved at trigger and frozen, definition-vs-instance) are absorbed into this model. Refs: ADR 0045 §8, 0040, 0046; COM-241/242 (what's being replaced).
