Follows COM-286/COM-288. Once engagements have a title, the remaining surfaces that identify one by its `scope` must switch, or the same engagement is called two different things on two screens.

The places, from a sweep of every use of `engagement.scope` as a label:

- [ ] **Portal register sub-rows** (`PortalVendorsPage.tsx`) — the `– {scope}` anchor under each vendor becomes the title. Depends on `title` reaching `VendorEngagementSummaryOut` (COM-286).
- [ ] **The review surface** (`ReviewModal.tsx`) — `EngagementBox`'s heading is the title; scope moves into the field rows below, where it belongs alongside data types, entities and residency. This box is where an approver actually decides, so it is the one that most needs a name at the top.
- [ ] **`AmendmentDiff.tsx`** — add `title` to the `AMENDABLE` list so a proposed rename shows as a diff row rather than silently applying on approval.
- [ ] While in `AmendmentDiff`: **`criticality` and `data_entities` are missing from `AMENDABLE`** — a pre-existing gap, so a proposed criticality change (exactly the escalation the approval rules exist to catch) currently renders nothing for the approver. Fix it here or split it out, but do not leave it unrecorded.
- [ ] The internal Requests tab needs nothing — `OnboardingRequests` / `RequestGroupTable` label rows by vendor and request kind, not by engagement.
- [ ] Tests: each surface renders the title; the diff shows a rename, and shows a criticality change.
